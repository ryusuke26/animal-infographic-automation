## 1) Species/topic selection rationale

Selected topic: Purple Frog / Indian Purple Frog, `Nasikabatrachus sahyadrensis`.

This species is a strong curiosity-first subject because its natural history is visually and behaviorally surprising without needing alarm language: adults live mostly underground in the Western Ghats of India, appear briefly with early monsoon rains to breed, and have tadpoles that cling to rocks in fast water with sucker-like mouths. It is also well suited to a minimal field-notebook poster: one unusual adult form, one hidden-habitat fact, one seasonal breeding fact, and one tadpole adaptation.

Selection caveat: conservation-status summaries conflict online. The current IUCN Red List citation found for the 2022 assessment lists `Near Threatened (NT)`, while some secondary or older pages still display `Endangered (EN)`. The final copy uses the 2022 IUCN category and avoids stronger urgency.
