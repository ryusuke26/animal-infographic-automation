## English Thread

2. It roosts in caves by day and flies into northern South American forests at night to find fruit.
3. Big eyes, white spots, bill bristles, and audible clicks make this bird feel unlike most cave wildlife.
4. IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC). No population number used.
