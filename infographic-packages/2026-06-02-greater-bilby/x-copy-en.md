## Main Post

In dry Australian night country, long ears wake before the rest of the body.
Greater Bilby
*Macrotis lagotis*

This burrowing marsupial forages after dark, digging for insects, seeds, fungi, and other small foods.
Its long ears, pointed snout, strong claws, and black-white tail are the clues.
A small, hidden life under the sand.

IUCN Red List 2016: Vulnerable (VU)

## ALT Text

A vertical crayon and oil-pastel field-notebook poster of a Greater Bilby in an arid Australian dusk scene. The animal has very long pinkish ears, a pointed snout, strong digging forelimbs, grey-blue cream fur, and a black-and-white tail. It stands beside spinifex grass and a burrow. Labels note that its long ears listen at night, it digs for insects and seeds, and it spends the daytime in spiral burrows. Footer: IUCN Red List 2016: VU.
