# 140-Character Thread Draft - English

1. In dry Australian night country, long ears wake first. Meet the Greater Bilby, *Macrotis lagotis*.

2. It is a burrowing marsupial of arid grasslands and shrublands. After dark, it digs for insects, seeds, fungi, and other small foods.

3. Long ears, a pointed snout, strong digging claws, and a black-white tail are the clues. By day, it rests in deep spiral burrows.

4. IUCN Red List 2016: Vulnerable (VU). No population number here; the focus is a hidden life under sand.

5. Sources: IUCN Red List, Australian Government SPRAT/DCCEEW, Australian Museum, and Animal Diversity Web.
