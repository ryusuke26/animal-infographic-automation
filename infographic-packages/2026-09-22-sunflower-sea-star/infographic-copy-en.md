Title: Sunflower Sea Star
Scientific name: *Pycnopodia helianthoides*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Five arms become as many as 24
   Explanation: Juveniles begin with five, then add more arms as they grow.
2. Heading: Thousands of tube feet in motion
   Explanation: Rows of tube feet ripple beneath the arms and carry it over the seafloor.
3. Heading: Sea urchins scatter
   Explanation: When a sunflower sea star approaches, urchins begin moving away.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2020: Critically Endangered (CR)
