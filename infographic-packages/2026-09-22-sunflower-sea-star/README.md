## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Pycnopodia helianthoides* (Brandt, 1835).
- Public names: Sunflower Sea Star / ヒマワリヒトデ. The Japanese name is used as a transparent rendering, not claimed as a standardized national-list name.
- Status: Global Critically Endangered (CR) under IUCN criterion A2ace, assessed 3 September 2020 and published in 2021.
- Public claims: juveniles begin with five arms and adults may have up to 24; thousands of tube feet carry the flexible body across the seafloor; sea urchins flee from an approaching sunflower sea star.
- Discovery doorway: a metre-scale sea star can grow up to 24 arms and travel on thousands of tube feet.
