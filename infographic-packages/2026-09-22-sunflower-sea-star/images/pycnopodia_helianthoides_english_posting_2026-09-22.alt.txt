Vertical 2:3 crayon-and-oil-pastel field-notebook poster. On a rocky Northeast Pacific kelp-forest floor, one large orange and muted purple-brown Sunflower Sea Star, Pycnopodia helianthoides, crawls with numerous flexible arms spread from a broad central disc. Three numbered illustrated cards show a five-armed juvenile becoming an adult with as many as 24 arms, thousands of tube feet moving beneath one arm, and sea urchins moving away from an approaching star. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2020: Critically Endangered (CR).
