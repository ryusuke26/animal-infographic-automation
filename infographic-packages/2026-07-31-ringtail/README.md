## Topic Lock

- English common name: Ringtail
- Japanese common name: カコミスル
- Scientific name: *Bassariscus astutus*
- Broad native region: North America
- Lineage: raccoon family, Procyonidae
- Habitat: rocky canyons, talus, woodland, scrub, and riparian habitats from
  the southwestern United States into Mexico
- Curiosity hook: it can turn its hind feet around and descend a rock face
  headfirst while keeping pads and claws against the surface

  after review found the animal and mechanism drawing unnatural.
- The remade Japanese poster uses a stable four-footed walking pose on a broad
  horizontal ledge. All four limbs connect naturally from torso to paw, and
  the tail attaches visibly to the rump.
- Card 3 now explains the 180-degree hind-foot rotation with one complete small
  descending Ringtail rather than detached or floating anatomy.

  first source was rejected at 887x1774; one targeted regeneration produced
  the accepted true 1024x1536 companion.
- Both posters show one unobstructed Ringtail with large dark eyes, pale eye
  rings, rounded ears, a slender tan-grey body, and one long black-and-pale
  ringed tail. Domestic-cat, raccoon-mask, lemur, civet, and extra-animal
  traits are absent.
- Each poster contains exactly three unequal numbered cards. Every card has
  species-specific spot art and the full locked explanatory copy.
- Japanese and English text, including the corrected 2015 assessment year and
  the English colon, comma, and `(LC)` spacing, is accurate and legible at
  phone size.
