Thorny Devil
Moloch horridus

In dry sandy country of Australia, this small lizard walks low to the ground in a coat of thorn-like spines.
It waits beside ant trails and picks up ants one by one with a short sticky tongue.

Observation notes
1. Sandy colors and thorny camouflage
2. A small spiny false head on the neck
3. Skin grooves guide water toward the mouth

Quiet context note
IUCN Red List 2017: Least Concern (LC)
No population number is used; the focus is the desert body plan and water-moving skin grooves.
