## English X Post

A tiny desert lizard wears thorns, then lets water travel through grooves in its skin.  
Thorny Devil  
Moloch horridus

The Thorny Devil lives in dry sandy country and spinifex grasslands of arid Australia. It waits beside ant trails and takes ants one by one with a short sticky tongue.  
Its spines, sandy colors, and small false head are key clues.  
A slightly strange life shaped by sand, ants, and passing drops of water.

IUCN Red List 2017: Least Concern (LC)
