## Image Identity Guidance

- Show a real thorny devil body plan: small squat lizard, short legs, short tail, broad flat body, dense conical spines, two larger spikes above eyes, spiny false head on neck.
- Use dry Australian sand country with sparse spinifex and a small ant trail.
- Include a small water-groove inset only as a diagram; do not create fake water storage sacs.
- Avoid North American horned-lizard traits, especially blood-squirting from the eyes.
- Avoid dragon wings, dinosaur proportions, extra eyes, oversized teeth, or aggressive monster styling.
