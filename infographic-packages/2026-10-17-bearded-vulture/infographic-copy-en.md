Title: Bearded Vulture
Scientific name: *Gypaetus barbatus*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Bones are the main meal
   Explanation: Bones from dead animals make up most of its diet.
2. Heading: Rocks become bone breakers
   Explanation: Large bones are dropped onto rocks to break into smaller pieces.
3. Heading: A rusty feather bath
   Explanation: Bathing in iron-rich muddy water stains white feathers rusty red.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2021: Near Threatened (NT)
