Vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel field-notebook poster titled Bearded Vulture, Gypaetus barbatus. An adult stands on a rocky ledge before snowy mountain ridges. It has folded slate-black wings, a pale head, rusty chest, a black eye mask and beard below the hooked bill, and a red eye ring. Three numbered illustrated notes: 1, Bones are the main meal: bones from dead animals make up most of its diet, with a small bone study. 2, Rocks become bone breakers: large bones are dropped onto rocks to break into smaller pieces, shown by a tiny flying bird, a descending bone and rocky impact. 3, A rusty feather bath: bathing in iron-rich muddy water stains white feathers rusty red, with a contextual study of a bird dipping its breast into an ochre puddle. Footer: IUCN Red List 2021: Near Threatened (NT).
