Vertical gouache painting in deep indigo. A stocky blue-black spotted salamander rests on wet leaves beside a spring woodland pond, with a broad head, short limbs, a long curved tail and two irregular rows of round yellow spots. Three numbered illustrated notes show a subtly green jelly egg mass with algae, a woodland breeding pond and a fallen-log shelter with a moon and earthworm. They explain algae in the egg jelly, spring gathering at ponds and daytime shelter with nighttime foraging. Title: Spotted Salamander; scientific name: Ambystoma maculatum. Footer: IUCN Red List 2020: Least Concern (LC).
