Title: Spotted Salamander
Scientific name: *Ambystoma maculatum*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Green guests in the eggs
   Explanation: Green algae can grow within the jelly surrounding the eggs.
2. Heading: From forest to spring pond
   Explanation: Usually hidden in woods, adults gather at ponds to breed in spring.
3. Heading: Hidden by day, out at night
   Explanation: Adults shelter under rocks or logs by day and forage on the forest floor at night.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2020: Least Concern (LC)
