# Star-nosed Mole Infographic Package

## 1. Selection Rationale

Star-nosed mole / ホシバナモグラ (*Condylura cristata*) is a strong curiosity-first subject because its 22 fleshy nasal rays, wetland tunnels, semi-aquatic life, and underwater bubble-sniffing make ordinary "mole" expectations feel unfamiliar. The topic can be framed as sensory natural history rather than conservation alarm.
