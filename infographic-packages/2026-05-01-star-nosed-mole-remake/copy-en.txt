A wetland mole with a star at its nose.
Star-nosed Mole
Condylura cristata

In wet soils and wetlands of eastern and northern North America,
it uses 22 fleshy rays on its nose to rapidly touch its surroundings.
Underwater, it can blow and re-inhale bubbles to follow scent.
A slightly mysterious life, reading the world with a star-shaped nose.

IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC)
