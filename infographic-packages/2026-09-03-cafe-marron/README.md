## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Ramosmania rodriguesii* Tirveng.
- English public name: Café marron, used by Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew.
- Japanese public name: カフェマロン, recorded as a Japanese vernacular name by Catalogue of Life through GBIF; no Japanese botanical standard-name list was confirmed.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Critically Endangered (CR) under criterion D, assessed 29 June 2025 and published in 2026, record `T33659A164117186`.
- Public claims: it grows in forest on Rodrigues; its juvenile leaves are long and narrow while adult leaves are shorter and broader; its greenish-yellow corollas mature to pure white.
- Discovery doorway: the same plant changes the visible language of both leaf and flower as it matures.
