## Image Identity Guidance

Required traits:

- small frogfish, not a seahorse or generic reef fish
- rounded/globose body
- large mouth
- mottled yellow, brown, green camouflage
- irregular weed-like skin flaps
- pectoral fins like little arms, touching or gripping Sargassum
- floating Sargassum with bead-like gas bladders
- open-ocean surface feeling, no fake map

Avoid:

- coral reef clownfish palette
- dramatic conservation imagery
- plastic pollution or threat imagery unless explicitly sourced
- population numbers
- maps or range silhouettes
