## 1. Selection Rationale

Today's topic is the Sargassum frogfish, Japanese common name ハナオコゼ. It is a small frogfish that lives among floating Sargassum seaweed, using seaweed-like flaps, mottled colors, and arm-like fins to hide and move through a drifting surface habitat.

It was selected because the recent archive already covered a desert plant, a floating sea slug, and a rainforest bird. This topic shifts to a marine fish and a floating open-ocean microhabitat, while keeping the tone curiosity-first: a tiny life waiting inside a drifting seaweed "forest."
