Sargassum Frogfish  
*Histrio histrio*

暖かい海の表面を漂うサルガッサムの中で、海藻に似た体を隠しながら獲物を待ちます。

IUCN Red List 2013: Least Concern (LC)

## English X Post

A tiny fish waits inside a drifting forest of seaweed.  
Sargassum Frogfish  
*Histrio histrio*

Among floating Sargassum at the warm ocean surface, this frogfish hides in plain sight and waits for small fishes or shrimps.  
Its pectoral fins work like little arms, helping it creep through the weed.  
A small drifting-weed life, just strange enough to make you look twice.

IUCN Red List 2013: Least Concern (LC)
