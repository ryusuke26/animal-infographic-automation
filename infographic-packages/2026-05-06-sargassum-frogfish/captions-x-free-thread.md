## Japanese

1. 漂う海藻の森で、じっと待つ小さな魚。ハナオコゼ / *Histrio histrio*。

2. 暖かい海の表面を漂うサルガッサムの中にひそみ、海藻そっくりの体で獲物を待ちます。

3. 胸びれを腕のように使い、海藻の枝をつかむようにのそのそ進みます。

4. IUCN Red List 2013: Least Concern (LC)。Sources: FishBase, GBIF, Smithsonian Ocean.

## English

1. A tiny fish waits inside a drifting forest of seaweed: Sargassum Frogfish, *Histrio histrio*.

2. It lives among floating Sargassum at the warm ocean surface, hiding in plain sight among the weed.

3. Weed-like skin flaps blur its outline, and pectoral fins help it creep through the seaweed like little arms.

4. IUCN Red List 2013: Least Concern (LC). Sources: FishBase, GBIF, Smithsonian Ocean.
