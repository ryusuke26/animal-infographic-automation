## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Micropotamogale lamottei* Heim de Balsac, 1954.
- English public name: Nimba Otter-shrew.
- Japanese public name: ヒメポタモガーレ, listed for this species in the Mammal Society of Japan's standard world-mammal name list.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Vulnerable (VU) under B1ab(i,ii,iii,v), assessed 20 December 2017 and published in 2018, record `T13393A111940150`.
- Public claims: it inhabits forest streams in the Mount Nimba region; it has a small rounded uniformly dark-brown body, short legs, and a long muscular tail without a paddle-like profile; sensitive whiskers and an enlarged upper lip help it find prey underwater.
- Discovery doorway: a small stream hunter moves between wet forest stones and underwater prey without the webbed feet or broad paddle tail expected of a more specialized aquatic mammal.
