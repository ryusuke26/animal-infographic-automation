## English Thread

1. Western Underground Orchid, Rhizanthella gardneri, lives below sandy soil in south-western Western Australia.
2. It has no green leaves, and even its clustered flower head develops underground.
3. As the head matures, pale bracts may reach the surface beneath leaf and bark litter.
4. It links to Melaleuca through a fungus. Australian EPBC Act: Critically Endangered (checked 2026).
