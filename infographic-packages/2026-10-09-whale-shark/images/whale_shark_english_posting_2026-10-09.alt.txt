A vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel observation poster. One dominant slate-blue whale shark cruises in blue ocean water, with a broad flat head, terminal transverse mouth, pale spots and lines, connected pectoral fins, two unequal dorsal fins and a vertical two-lobed tail. Three numbered illustrated cards explain upright feeding, filtering small prey and dives beyond 1 km. The depth and prey studies are qualitative. The title is Whale Shark, the scientific name Rhincodon typus, and the footer gives the 2025 global IUCN Endangered (EN) assessment.
