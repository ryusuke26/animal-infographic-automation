Title: Whale Shark
Scientific name: *Rhincodon typus*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: A meal in an upright pose
   Explanation: It can hang vertically and draw food-rich water into its mouth.
2. Heading: The biggest fish filters small prey
   Explanation: Gill filters collect small crustaceans and fish from the water.
3. Heading: From the surface to beyond 1 km
   Explanation: It feeds near the surface but can also dive more than a kilometre deep.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2025: Endangered (EN)
