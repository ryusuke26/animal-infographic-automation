Vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel poster titled Marine Iguana, with Amblyrhynchus cristatus beneath. A charcoal-grey adult rests on dark lava beside a teal sea. It has a blunt rounded muzzle, a pointed neck-and-back crest, stout body, clawed legs and one long tapering tail. Three numbered illustrated notes explain large individuals diving to graze on attached algae, excess salt expelled through the nose, and dark scales absorbing morning sunlight before sea foraging. The drawings show an underwater grazing iguana, an attached head-and-neck study with a small outward nasal spray, and an iguana basking on lava beneath the sun. Footer: IUCN Red List 2019: Vulnerable (VU).
