Title: Marine Iguana
Scientific name: *Amblyrhynchus cristatus*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Seaweed on the menu
   Explanation: Large individuals dive to graze on algae growing on rocks.
2. Heading: Sneezing out salt
   Explanation: Excess salt taken in with their food is expelled through the nose.
3. Heading: Warming up in the sun
   Explanation: Dark scales absorb sunlight during morning basking before foraging at sea.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2019: Vulnerable (VU)
