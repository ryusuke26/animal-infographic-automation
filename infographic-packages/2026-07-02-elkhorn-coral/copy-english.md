## Full Copy

Elkhorn Coral
*Acropora palmata*

In clear shallow Caribbean water, this reef-building coral spreads broad branches upward.
Golden tan branches with pale tips make a living thicket of tiny polyps.
The spaces between branches can shelter small reef animals.

Conservation note: IUCN Red List Critically Endangered (assessed 2021)
