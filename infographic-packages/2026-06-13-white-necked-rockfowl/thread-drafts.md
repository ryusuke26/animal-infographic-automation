## Japanese Thread

## English Thread

1. White-necked Rockfowl lives in rocky forests of West Africa.
2. It has a bare yellow head, black facial patches, white underparts, and a long tail.
3. It moves on the forest floor and builds deep mud cup nests beneath protected rock.
4. IUCN Red List 2018: Vulnerable (VU). Scientific name: Picathartes gymnocephalus.
