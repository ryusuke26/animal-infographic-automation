## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Romanichthys valsanicola* Dumitrescu, Bănărescu & Stoica, 1957.
- English public name: Asprete; Romanian Darter, Sculpin-perch, and Vilsan Perch are recorded English aliases.
- Japanese public name: アスプレテ. No established standard Japanese common name was confirmed; this is a transparent katakana rendering of the supported English and Romanian name, and the caveat remains in evidence context rather than on the poster.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Critically Endangered (CR) under B1ab(iii), assessed 1 September 2023 and published in the 2024-2 release as assessment `T19740A137285480`.
- Public claims: it lives close to the rocky bottom of the cold, fast Vâlsan River; its small elongated body is brown-grey and irregularly mottled; it hides under stones by day and hunts current-loving insect larvae at night.
- Discovery doorway: a mottled river-bottom fish disappears beneath stones by day, then emerges after dark to search the current for insect larvae.
