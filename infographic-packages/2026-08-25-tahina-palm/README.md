## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Tahina spectabilis* J.Dransf. & Rakotoarin.
- English public name: Tahina Palm, used by the current official IUCN assessment.
- Japanese public name: タヒナ・スペクタビリス, a katakana rendering already used in Japanese specialist horticultural material; no established standard Japanese common name was confirmed.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Critically Endangered (CR) under B1ab(iii); D, assessed 17 December 2010 and published in 2012. The current official page is annotated `Needs updating`.
- Public claims: it grows at the foot of a limestone outcrop in seasonally flooded soil, carries fan leaves up to 5 m across, and flowers once in a huge branching inflorescence before the palm dies.
- Discovery doorway: after years as a crown of enormous fans, one pale candelabra-like flowering tower rises above the whole palm and marks the closing chapter of its life.
