Vertical 2:3 handmade Madagascar field-notebook poster. One complete Tahina Palm, Tahina spectabilis, grows at the foot of a dark limestone outcrop. It has one thick swollen trunk with horizontal leaf scars, hanging dead leaves, and a dense crown of enormous green pleated fans. One pale, branching, candelabra-like inflorescence rises continuously from the crown into the sky. The title and scientific name are integrated at the top. Three unequal numbered illustrated cards show the seasonally flooded limestone foot, a fan leaf up to 5 m across, and the palm's single flowering followed by death. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2010: Critically Endangered (CR).
