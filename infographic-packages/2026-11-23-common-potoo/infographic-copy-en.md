Title: Common Potoo
Scientific name: *Nyctibius griseus*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Becoming a broken branch
   Explanation: When alarmed, it stretches its neck, points its bill straight up and presses its feathers flat, freezing like a snapped branch tip.
2. Heading: Watching with eyes closed
   Explanation: Narrow slits in the upper eyelids let it sense nearby movement even while its eyes look shut.
3. Heading: A nest that is just a dent
   Explanation: Without any nest material, it lays a single white egg in a hollow on a broken branch, and both parents take turns incubating.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2019: Least Concern (LC)
