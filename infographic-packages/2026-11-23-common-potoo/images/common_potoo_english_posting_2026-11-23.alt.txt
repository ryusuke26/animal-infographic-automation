Vertical poster drawn on toned paper in a sketchbook style. Title "Common Potoo" and the scientific name in a frame at the top. In the centre, one slim mottled grey-brown bird stands upright on a broken, lichen-spotted dead branch, bill pointing straight up and eyes closed to slits; five black spots cross its breast and a long dark-banded tail hangs beside the branch. Three numbered figures: 1, a tall oval at upper left, the bird relaxed with an open yellow eye, then, after an arrow, frozen with its bill up like a branch tip; 2, a round close-up at right of the head with the eye shut to a narrow slit; 3, a torn-paper vignette at lower left, one speckled white egg in a hollow on top of a broken stump. Each has a heading and explanation. Footer: IUCN Red List 2019: Least Concern (LC).
