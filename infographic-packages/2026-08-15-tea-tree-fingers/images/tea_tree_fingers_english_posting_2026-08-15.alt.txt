Vertical 2:3 handmade mycology field-notebook poster set in damp tea-tree woodland of southeastern Australia. One thin dead twig crosses the composition diagonally. A single dominant Tea-tree Fingers fungus, Hypocreopsis amplectens, forms a low cinnamon to rust-brown central mass whose thick flattened finger-like lobes clasp the twig. The English title and scientific name sit at the top. Three unequal numbered illustrated cards show an attached side-view fruiting body, the layered relationship with a wood-decay fungus below, and moist woodland in southeastern Australia and New Zealand's South Island. The footer reads IUCN Red List 2019: Critically Endangered (CR).
