## Japanese Post

暗い海底に、白いハープみたいな体。  
Harp sponge  
Chondrocladia lyra

北カリフォルニア沖の深海の泥底に根を張る、肉食性のカイメンです。流れに向けた枝で、小さな甲殻類をからめ取ります。  
ろうそく台のような白い枝と、小さな鉤が目じるし。  
深海の流れを待つ、ちょっと不思議な暮らし。

IUCN Red List: Not Evaluated (NE; checked 2026)

## English Post

A white harp shape waiting in the dark deep sea.  
Harp sponge  
Chondrocladia lyra

This carnivorous sponge is known from deep muddy seafloor off northern California. Its upright branches carry tiny hooks that catch small crustaceans drifting past.  
It looks delicate, but it is built for life in a food-poor current.

IUCN Red List: Not Evaluated (NE; checked 2026)
