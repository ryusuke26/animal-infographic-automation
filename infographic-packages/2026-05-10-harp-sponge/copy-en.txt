Harp sponge
Chondrocladia lyra

A white carnivorous sponge rooted on the dark muddy seafloor off northern California.
It faces deep-sea currents with harp-like branches and tiny hooks that catch small crustaceans.
Its upright branches and small terminal balls make it look more like a quiet instrument than a predator.

IUCN Red List: Not Evaluated (NE; no species assessment located, checked 2026)
