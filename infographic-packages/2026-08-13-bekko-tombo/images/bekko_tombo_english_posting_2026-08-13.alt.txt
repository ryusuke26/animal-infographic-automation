A vertical 2:3 handmade spring-wetland field-notebook poster. One Bekko Tombo, Libellula angelina, rests diagonally on a stout rising reed above a shallow East Asian pond rich in reeds and cattails. Its warm amber body, broad tapering abdomen, clustered gripping legs, and distinct brown markings on all four transparent wings are visible. The common and scientific names sit at the top. Three irregular numbered illustrated cards show the vegetated shallow-pond habitat, a small dorsal adult displaying four marked wings, and a newly emerged adult beside its exuvia and spring buds. A quiet footer gives the IUCN Red List 2006 Critically Endangered (CR) status.
