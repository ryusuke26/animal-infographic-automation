## Evidence route

The official seven-page IUCN assessment PDF and matching official species-page capture supplied by the user directly confirm *Libellula angelina* as Global Critically Endangered (CR) under A3ce, version 3.1, assessed 31 January 2006 and published in 2006 as record `e.T11928A3315484`. Japan's Ministry of the Environment supports the accepted Japanese and scientific names, East Asian range, shallow vegetated pond habitat, brown markings on all four wings, spring emergence, and one generation per year. The Japanese national CR listing remains separate from the global assessment used in public footers.
