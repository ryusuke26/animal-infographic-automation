## Visual identity guidance

- Show one complete immature adult in a natural horizontal perch on a slender reed or cattail stem above shallow water. Use a stable dorsal three-quarter view so the four wings and broad tapered abdomen are readable; do not use a dramatic flight pose.
- The hero is a medium-sized libellulid dragonfly, about 38 mm long with a roughly 7 cm wingspan. Give it a warm amber to tortoiseshell immature body, a sturdy thorax, large compound eyes, and a relatively broad abdomen tapering toward the tip. Do not turn it into a needle-thin damselfly, butterfly, moth, or generic unmarked dragonfly.
- Show exactly four transparent, veined wings attached separately to the thorax: one forewing and one hindwing on each side. Every wing remains complete, distinct, and unobstructed.
