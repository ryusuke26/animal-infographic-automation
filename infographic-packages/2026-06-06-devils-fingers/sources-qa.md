# Sources and QA - Devil's Fingers

## Verdict

Overall: Ready with conservative wording.

The package uses the accepted name *Clathrus archeri* and the English common name Devil's Fingers. デビルズ・フィンガーズ is explicitly treated as a katakana rendering, not a verified standardized Japanese common name.

## Image Identity Guidance

- Ground-level stinkhorn fruit body in damp leaf litter or wood chips.
- A torn white to cream egg-like volva remains at the base.
- Four to seven separate, tapering, coral-red arms curve outward.
- Inner arm surfaces carry irregular dark olive-brown spore slime.
- A few small flies may land on the slime; do not make them the main subject.
- Avoid a closed red lattice/cage, a starfish, an octopus animal, a flower, a horror monster, eyes, teeth, suction cups, or a marine setting.

## Uncertainty Notes

- Common names vary by language and region. The Japanese title is a transparent transliteration.
- The fruiting body is the visible reproductive structure; the broader fungus includes hidden mycelium.
- Number and posture of arms vary. Public copy avoids a fixed exact count.
- Regional red-list or introduced-species labels should not be presented as a global IUCN category.
