Sunda Colugo
Galeopterus variegatus
Order Dermoptera / Family Cynocephalidae

A nocturnal, tree-living mammal that glides through Southeast Asian forests.
It does not flap like a bat; a furred membrane links the neck, limbs, toes, and tail.
It feeds on young leaves, flowers, buds, fruit, nectar, and sap.
Young can cling to the mother's underside, sometimes sheltered by the folded membrane.

IUCN Red List 2008: Least Concern (LC)
