## Japanese post

森の幹に、ひそむ膜。
Sunda Colugo
Galeopterus variegatus

東南アジアの森で、夜に木から木へすべる樹上性の哺乳類。
コウモリでもキツネザルでもなく、手足と尾を包む膜が目印。
木肌に紛れ、夜に枝へ渡る、ちょっと不思議な暮らし。

IUCN Red List 2008: Least Concern (LC)

## English post

A folded cloak hides against the bark.
Sunda Colugo
Galeopterus variegatus

This nocturnal mammal glides between trees in Southeast Asian forests.
It is not a bat or a true lemur; its signature is a furred membrane linking limbs and tail.
A quiet, bark-clinging life with a body made for gliding.

IUCN Red List 2008: Least Concern (LC)
