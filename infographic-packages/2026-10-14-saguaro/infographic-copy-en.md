Title: Saguaro
Scientific name: *Carnegiea gigantea*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Pleats that expand with rain
   Explanation: Pleats spread as water is stored, then contract in dry weather.
2. Heading: White flowers open at night
   Explanation: Each flower opens at night and closes the next afternoon.
3. Heading: A sheltered start
   Explanation: Young plants grow protected by a nurse tree or rock.
