## Evidence route

The preserved official IUCN errata assessment lists *Phelsuma guentheri* as Vulnerable (VU) under D2, assessed 23 April 2018, with Red List publication year 2018 and current errata record `T16926A152274946`; the errata version itself was published in 2019. The assessment records Mauritius as native and describes humid-to-dry forest and open mixed woodland dominated by palms and screwpines, a large cryptically colored gecko active by day and night, and a successful 2010 translocation to Île aux Aigrettes. The public footer therefore remains the confirmed global category and assessment year only.

The matching current-page capture separately shows a Global Green Status assessment of Critically Depleted, last assessed 4 July 2024. That Green Status field is not an IUCN Red List threat category and is excluded from the poster and main-post footer.

The Reptile Database accepts *Phelsuma guentheri* and records Round Island, oviparity, a vertically elliptical pupil, and secondarily nocturnal activity. Mauritius government material supports the large body, Round Island palm trunks, and adhesive toe pads. Public copy does not publish the assessment's population estimate.

## Locked visual concept

An authored Mascarene field notebook follows one muted gray-brown gecko across a diagonal palm trunk. It must not become the bright emerald, red-spotted look of familiar Madagascar day geckos. Visible limbs must attach and articulate coherently, but natural perspective occlusion of the far-side forelimb is correct for the selected view and must not be "repaired." Five adhesive toes on each visible foot and one complete tail remain hard acceptance gates.
