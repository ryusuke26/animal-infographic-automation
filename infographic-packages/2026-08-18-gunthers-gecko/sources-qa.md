## Visual identity guidance

- One large adult gecko is the hero: robust elongated body, broad blunt head, vertically elliptical pupil, muted olive-gray to gray-brown color, subtle darker mottling, anatomically coherent visible limbs, broad adhesive toe pads, and one complete tapering tail.
- Trace every visible limb from its shoulder or hip origin to its endpoint. Natural perspective occlusion of a far-side limb is acceptable when the torso and camera angle explain it; do not force every limb into view. Each visible foot should read as five splayed gecko digits, not fused mittens.
- The setting is palm-and-screwpine woodland on Round Island: rough palm bark, fan palm, screw-pine leaves, volcanic rock, and humid-to-dry island-forest light. It is not a terrarium, mainland rainforest, resort garden, or generic Madagascar scene.
- The nearest false silhouette is a bright emerald *Phelsuma* with red spots or blue bands. Reject that familiar lookalike palette. Also reject chameleon feet, anole body shape, crest, spines, horns, or eyelash fringes.
- Card 1: palm-rich island woodland. Card 2: one communal nest site on the underside of a Latania palm frond or sheltered rock, with several attached egg pairs/clusters and older overlapping egg scars. Card 3: one small gecko moving along a branch between restrained day and night symbols; natural perspective occlusion is allowed.
- Keep exactly one dominant hero and exactly three numbered illustrated cards. Do not add a map, population count, pet enclosure, conservation slogan, or extra anatomy diagram.
