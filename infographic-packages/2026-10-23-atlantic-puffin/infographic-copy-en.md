Title: Atlantic Puffin
Scientific name: *Fratercula arctica*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: A bill that changes
   Explanation: After breeding, outer bill plates are shed and the bill becomes smaller.
2. Heading: Wings work underwater
   Explanation: It beats its wings to dive and uses its feet to steer after fish.
3. Heading: A nest beneath the grass
   Explanation: It digs a burrow on an island slope and usually lays one egg.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2018: Vulnerable (VU)
