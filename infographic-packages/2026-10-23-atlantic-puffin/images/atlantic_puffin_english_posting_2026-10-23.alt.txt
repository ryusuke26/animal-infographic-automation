Vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel poster titled Atlantic Puffin, scientific name Fratercula arctica. A breeding adult with black back, white belly, pale cheeks and a deep orange-and-gray bill stands on a grassy rocky North Atlantic island slope. Three numbered illustrated notes show: 1, attached head studies compare the bright breeding bill with the smaller, duller nonbreeding bill after outer plates are shed; 2, an intact bird swimming below the surface beats its wings and steers with its feet after fish; 3, a continuous burrow beneath grass ends in one pale egg. Footer: IUCN Red List 2018: Vulnerable (VU).
