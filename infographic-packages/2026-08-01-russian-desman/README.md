## Topic Lock

- English common name: Russian Desman
- Japanese common name: ロシアデスマン
- Scientific name: *Desmana moschata*
- Broad native region: Europe
- Lineage: mole family, Talpidae; tribe Desmanini
- Native range: eastern European waterways in Russia and Ukraine, extending
  into Kazakhstan
- Habitat: oxbow lakes, ponds, and slow-flowing rivers with rich water-marsh
  vegetation and vegetated banks
- Curiosity hook: a round-bodied mole relative swims with webbed hind feet and
  a laterally flattened tail, then probes underwater with a long flexible snout
