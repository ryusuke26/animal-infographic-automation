## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Columbina cyanopis* (Pelzeln, 1870); current IUCN and BirdLife usage retained while the proposed return to *Oxypelia* remains a taxonomy note.
- English public name: Blue-eyed Ground Dove.
- Japanese public name: アオメヒメバト, used by BirdLife International Tokyo.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Critically Endangered (CR) under C2a(i,ii), assessed 26 February 2019, record `T22690804A145971767`.
- Public claims: an unfamiliar call led to the 2015 rediscovery after about 75 years; the adult male has a cobalt eye and blue-violet folded-wing spots; it lives in low-grass and scrub Cerrado, including pale white-sand habitat around Botumirim.
- Discovery doorway: a voice in pale Cerrado grass leads to a tiny russet dove whose eye color reappears as small marks on the wing.
