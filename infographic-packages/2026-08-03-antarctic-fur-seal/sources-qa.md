## Evidence Lock

- Accepted scientific name: *Arctocephalus gazella* (Peters, 1875).
- English common name: Antarctic Fur Seal.
- Supported Japanese name: ナンキョクオットセイ.
- Broad native region: Southern Ocean and sub-Antarctic islands; breeding occurs mainly on islands south of the Antarctic Convergence.
- Habitat: land-breeding colonies on dry shingle and rocky sub-Antarctic shores; marine feeding in surrounding Southern Ocean waters.
- Global IUCN category/year: Endangered (EN), 2026; record e.T2058A293563664.
- Lineage: an eared seal in family Otariidae.
- Curiosity doorway: an “eared seal” that uses long foreflippers and forward-rotated hind flippers to walk on all fours over a black-pebble shore.

## Naming and status notes

- The National Museum of Nature and Science pairs ナンキョクオットセイ with *Arctocephalus gazella* and Antarctic Fur Seal/Kerguelen Fur Seal.
- That museum page still displays Least Concern, which predates the official IUCN April 2026 update. Public copy follows the newer IUCN Global Endangered assessment and identifies the older LC display as outdated in the source reply.
- The official IUCN release contains population estimates and a decline explanation. Those figures are intentionally omitted from the poster and main post; the package needs only the directly confirmed global category and year.
- No national legal category is presented as the global IUCN category.

## Visual identity guidance

- One adult female Antarctic Fur Seal on a dark shingle shore: compact dark brown to grey-brown body, paler throat/chest, short broad muzzle, long pale whiskers, two small pointed external ears, long foreflippers, and two hind flippers rotated beneath the body.
- Stable four-footed land posture. Every flipper has a visible shoulder or hip origin, separate path, and separate grounded endpoint with negative space between near and far counterparts.
- Card 3 shows a complete small walking seal, never an isolated flipper.
- Not a true seal lacking external ears, sea lion, elephant seal, leopard seal, Weddell seal, sub-Antarctic fur seal, or New Zealand fur seal. No trunk-like nose, spotted coat, large mane, pup group, sea-ice-only setting, extra animal, or invented anatomy.
