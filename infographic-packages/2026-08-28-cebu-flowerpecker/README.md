## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Dicaeum quadricolor* (Tweeddale, 1878); original combination *Prionochilus quadricolor*.
- English public name: Cebu Flowerpecker, used by IUCN, BirdLife, and current international bird lists.
- Japanese public name: ヨイロハナドリ, supported by Avibase and Japanese bird-name databases.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Critically Endangered (CR) under C2a(i), assessed 5 August 2020 and published in 2021.
- Public claims: it lives in Cebu's tallest remaining limestone-forest patches; the male has a glossy blue-black body with a large scarlet triangular mantle patch and pale yellow-white underparts; it feeds on mistletoe-like plants and small ripe figs.
- Discovery doorway: a four-coloured canopy bird feared lost for much of the twentieth century was rediscovered in a small Cebu forest patch in 1992.
