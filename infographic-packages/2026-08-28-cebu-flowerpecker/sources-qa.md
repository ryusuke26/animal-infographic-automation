## Selected identity and naming

- Accepted scientific name: *Dicaeum quadricolor* (Tweeddale, 1878), retained by IUCN, BirdLife, Catalogue of Life, and current international bird lists.
- Original combination and retrieval alias: *Prionochilus quadricolor* Tweeddale, 1878.
- English public name: Cebu Flowerpecker. Four-colored Flowerpecker, Four-coloured Flowerpecker, and Orange-backed Flowerpecker are secondary English aliases.
- Japanese public name: ヨイロハナドリ, supported by Avibase and Japanese bird-name listings. セブハナドリ is an understandable rendering but is not used publicly here.
- Editorial classification group: Birds.
- Exact lineage: Animalia; Chordata; Aves; Passeriformes; Dicaeidae; *Dicaeum*; *Dicaeum quadricolor*.
- Broad native region: Cebu Island, Philippines / Asia.
- Habitat: the tallest remaining low-elevation forest patches up to about 500 m, mostly on karst limestone, including open- and closed-canopy forest plus secondary or selectively logged areas next to larger native-vegetation patches.
- Curiosity hook: the bird was feared extinct until its 1992 rediscovery; an adult male compresses four bold colours into a tiny canopy body, with the brightest red carried on the back rather than the throat.
