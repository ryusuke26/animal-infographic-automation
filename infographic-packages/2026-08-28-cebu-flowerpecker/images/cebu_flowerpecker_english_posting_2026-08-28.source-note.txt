Source note: IUCN assessment e.T22717507A181042707 lists Dicaeum quadricolor as Global CR under C2a(i), assessed 5 August 2020. The IUCN/BirdLife account supports Cebu's tallest remaining karst-limestone forest patches, the 1992 rediscovery, and feeding on mistletoe-like plants and small ripe figs. The original plate, Cornell illustrations, and a 1994 field sketch support the male's blue-black, scarlet, yellow and white plumage and triangular scarlet back patch.
