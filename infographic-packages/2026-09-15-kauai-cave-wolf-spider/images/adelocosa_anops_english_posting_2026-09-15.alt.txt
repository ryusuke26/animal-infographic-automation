Vertical 2:3 handmade field-notebook poster. One Kauaʻi Cave Wolf Spider, Adelocosa anops, walks over reddish-brown porous lava in a humid cave on Kauaʻi. Its smooth eye-free carapace, pale abdomen and eight orange-brown legs are visible. The common and scientific names sit at the top. Three numbered illustrated cards show the eyeless face, long sensory hairs on the legs and newly hatched young riding on a parent's back. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 1996: Endangered (EN).
