## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Brachiopsilus dianthus* Last & Gledhill, 2009.
- English public name: Pink Handfish.
- Japanese public name: ピンクハンドフィッシュ, a transparent transliteration rather than a claimed standardized Japanese fish-list name.
- Status: Global Endangered (EN) under IUCN criteria B1ab(v)+2ab(v), assessed 30 April 2018 in record `T123376622A123424329` and published in 2020.
- Public claims: the 2021 survey recorded the species after 22 years at about 120 m; hand-like pectoral fins are used to walk along the seafloor; handfishes lack a pelagic larval stage and young settle near the spawning site.
- Discovery doorway: after two decades without a confirmed sighting, a small pink walking fish crossed a deep survey camera.
