## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Natalus primus* Anthony, 1919.
- English public name: Cuban Greater Funnel-eared Bat.
- Japanese public name: キューバオオアシナガコウモリ, from the Mammalogical Society of Japan standard-name list.
- Status: Global Vulnerable (VU) under IUCN criterion D2, assessed 21 October 2014 in record `T136777A22032828` and published in 2016.
- Public claims: a living population was rediscovered in 1992 after the species had been known from fossils and thought extinct; its large ears have rounded rather than pointed tips and lack the posterior notch typical of other *Natalus*; many sampled animals were found in the warm inner cave section where temperature and humidity stayed stable.
- Discovery doorway: a living, round-eared bat emerging from a story that had seemed to end with fossils.
