Vertical 2:3 crayon-and-oil-pastel field-notebook poster. In a warm limestone cave at Cuba's western tip, one yellowish-buff Cuban Greater Funnel-eared Bat, Natalus primus, hangs naturally from the ceiling. Its large rounded ears, long slender legs, folded wings and tail membrane are visible. The title and scientific name sit above. Three numbered illustrated cards show its 1992 rediscovery, rounded ear tips and use of the cave's inner heat trap. The footer reads IUCN Red List 2014: Vulnerable (VU).
