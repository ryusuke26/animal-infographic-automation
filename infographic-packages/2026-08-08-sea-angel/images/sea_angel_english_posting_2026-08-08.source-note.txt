Source note: JAMSTEC BISMaL and Aquarium Fukushima support Clione elegantissima, the Japanese name ハダカカメガイ, its shell-less gastropod identity, wing-like swimming feet, Shiretoko drift-ice-season occurrence, and conical feeding structures. A 2022 Polar Science study supports its winter occurrence in the southern Sea of Okhotsk.
