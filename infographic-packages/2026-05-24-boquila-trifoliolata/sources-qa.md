## Public Wording Decisions

- Use `ボキラ・トリフォリオラータ` as the Japanese main title because no stable Japanese common name was located.
- Use `Chameleon vine` only as an English hook, never as a substitute for the scientific name.
- Avoid `plant vision`, `the plant sees`, `smart plant`, or `chooses its disguise`.
- Avoid exact counts of host species in poster copy; `nearby host leaves` is safer and easier to read.
- Avoid range maps because detailed boundaries are not needed and fake maps are a known visual risk.

## Image QA Checklist

- Vine, not tree, shrub, ivy houseplant, grape, or fern.
- South American temperate rainforest feeling: humid shade, moss, trunks/branches, soft green understory.
- Leaves include ordinary three-leaflet cue and comparison leaves that subtly vary.
- No eyes, faces, magic glow, hands, or cartoon transformation effects.
- No claim that mimicry mechanism is proven.
- Text may be unreliable in Image Gen raster; text-safe backups carry authoritative wording.
