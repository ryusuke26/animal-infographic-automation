## Japanese thread

1. 森の葉に、そっと自分の輪郭を寄せるつる。ボキラ・トリフォリオラータ。南米の温帯林にすむ植物です。 #植物

## English thread

1. A vine that seems to borrow nearby leaf shapes: Boquila trifoliolata, the chameleon vine of South American temperate forests.

2. Its leaves can resemble nearby host leaves in shape, size, color, angle, stalk length, or tiny pointed tips.

3. The mechanism is still being studied, so the safe wording is simple: the leaves can resemble nearby leaves.

4. IUCN Red List checked 2026: Not Evaluated (NE). Sources: Kew POWO, Universidad de Talca, Gianoli 2014/2021.
