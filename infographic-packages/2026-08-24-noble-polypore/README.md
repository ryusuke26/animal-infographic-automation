## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Bridgeoporus nobilissimus* (W.B. Cooke) T.J. Volk, Burdsall & Ammirati, 1996.
- English public name: Noble Polypore, supported by USDA Forest Service material.
- Japanese public name: ノーブル・ポリポア, a transparent rendering of the supported English common name; no established standard Japanese common name was confirmed.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Critically Endangered (CR) under A2c; C2a(i), assessed 22 April 2015; the citation's errata version was published in 2016.
- Public claims: it forms long-lived conks on the bases of very old large firs and stumps, has coarse white surface fibres that darken and can appear green with algae, and can accumulate more than 100 tube layers.
- Discovery doorway: what looks like a mossy doormat at the foot of a giant fir is a perennial fruitbody recording years of growth in stacked pore layers.
