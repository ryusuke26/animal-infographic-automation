Vertical 2:3 handmade Pacific Northwest old-growth fir field-notebook poster. One huge Noble Polypore, Bridgeoporus nobilissimus, forms a thick low shelf attached to the base of one massive fir. Its pale scalloped margin, shaggy white fibres, cinnamon-brown old tissue, and algae- and moss-green surface are visible. The title and scientific name are integrated at the top. Three unequal numbered illustrated cards show attachment to an old fir, algae among white fibres, and a cutaway with more than 100 tube layers. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2015: Critically Endangered (CR).
