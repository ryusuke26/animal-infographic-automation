A small red fish walks on the seabed.

RED HANDFISH
Thymichthys politus

OBSERVATION 1
Lives on shallow reefs
in south-eastern Tasmania

OBSERVATION 2
Uses hand-like fins
to walk on the bottom

OBSERVATION 3
Attaches eggs near algae
and guards them until hatch

Small steps among seaweed
on a temperate seafloor.

IUCN Red List 2020:
Critically Endangered (CR)
