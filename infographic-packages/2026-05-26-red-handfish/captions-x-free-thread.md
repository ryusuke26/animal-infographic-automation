## Japanese Thread

1. 海の底を歩く、赤い小さな魚。レッドハンドフィッシュ *Thymichthys politus*。#世界の知らない生き物

2. タスマニア南東部の浅い岩礁で、海藻や海草の間にくらす小魚。手のようなひれで海底を歩く。

3. 海藻の根元などに卵をつけ、孵化まで見守る。海藻の影をめぐる、ちょっと不思議な暮らし。

4. IUCN Red List 2020: Critically Endangered (CR)。出典: DCCEEW / Fishes of Australia / IUCN。

## English Thread

1. A small red fish walks on the seabed: Red Handfish, *Thymichthys politus*. #NaturalHistory

2. It lives on shallow reefs in south-eastern Tasmania, among seaweed and seagrass.

3. Its hand-like fins move it across the bottom; eggs are attached near algae and guarded until hatch.

4. IUCN Red List 2020: Critically Endangered (CR). Sources: DCCEEW / Fishes of Australia / IUCN.
