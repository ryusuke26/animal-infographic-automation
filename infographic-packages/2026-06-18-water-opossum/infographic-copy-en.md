# English Infographic Copy

Title: Water Opossum

Scientific name: *Chironectes minimus*

Observation labels:

- Forest streams
- Webbed hind feet
- Water-tight pouch

Footer:

IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC)

Core copy:

A swimming marsupial of Neotropical forest streams.

Its broad webbed hind feet and long tail help it move through water.

The female pouch can close during dives, helping keep young dry.
