# Japanese Image Prompt

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: Japanese educational social media infographic, vertical 2:3 poster
Primary request: Create a gentle infographic about the Water Opossum, *Chironectes minimus*, focused on curiosity, natural-history discovery, and education rather than advocacy.
Subject: one Water Opossum in a tropical Neotropical forest stream. Key identity traits: small opossum-like marsupial, gray-white and dark marbled fur, dark face markings, pale band over the eyes, rounded ears, whiskers, long tail used while swimming, broad webbed hind feet, small non-webbed forefeet. It should not look like an otter, beaver, rat, generic mouse, or ordinary terrestrial opossum.
Style/medium: childlike crayon and oil pastel field-notebook poster, textured warm paper, handmade but polished and readable.
Composition/framing: top title and scientific name; center one large animal partly in shallow clear stream water beside a forested bank; three short observation labels placed around the animal; bottom quiet conservation/status footer; generous whitespace.
Color palette: clear stream blue-green, wet stones, tropical forest greens, warm paper, soft gray and dark fur markings.
Text, verbatim:
ミズオポッサム
Chironectes minimus
森の小川にすむ
後ろ足に水かき
ぬれにくい育児嚢
IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC)
Constraints: Discovery-first, not moralizing. Accurate legible text. Do not invent population numbers. Do not imply blame. No logos, no watermark. No fake maps.
Avoid: crowded poster, tiny text, fake map, unsupported threats, rescue slogans, species misidentification, misspelled names, extra animals, dramatic pouch cutaway.
