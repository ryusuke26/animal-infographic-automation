## Japanese

1. 川の暗がりを泳ぐ有袋類、ミズオポッサム。後ろ足には水かき、長い尾は水中の舵になります。
2. 中南米の森の小川や湖岸にすみ、夜の水辺で小さな水生動物を探します。
3. 子を入れる育児嚢は水に入るとき閉じられるしくみ。水辺で子を運ぶ、ちょっと不思議な暮らし。
4. IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC)。個体数の数字は使わず、暮らしの形を中心に紹介しています。

## English

1. Meet the water opossum, a marsupial that swims through Neotropical forest streams with webbed hind feet and a long tail.
2. It lives around freshwater streams and lakeshores, often using stream-bank dens near cover.
3. The female pouch can close during dives, helping keep young dry while the animal moves through water.
4. IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC). This post avoids population numbers and threat-heavy framing.
