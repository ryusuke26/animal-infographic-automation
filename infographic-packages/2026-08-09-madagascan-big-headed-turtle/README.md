## Evidence route

The IUCN/TFTSG assessment accepts *Erymnochelys madagascariensis* and records Global Critically Endangered under A4d, assessed 15 January 2008. The current errata citation uses record `e.T8070A97396666`; the assessment year, rather than the 2016 errata-publication context, is used in public footers. The 2025 IUCN SSC Tortoise and Freshwater Turtle Specialist Group references support the accepted lineage, western Madagascar river-and-lake habitat, large casque-covered head, and current CR context. Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry supports the public Japanese name マダガスカルヨコクビガメ.
