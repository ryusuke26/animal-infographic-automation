Vertical 2:3 handmade Torricelli mountain-forest field-notebook poster. One black Tenkile, Dendrolagus scottae, walks quadrupedally through mist, mossy roots, ferns and wet leaf litter toward a slanting trunk. Its shoulder hair whorl, rounded ears, elongated muzzle, four connected limbs and thick pelvis-connected tail are visible. The title and scientific name are integrated at the top. Three unequal numbered illustrated cards show Torricelli montane forest, the black coat and shoulder whorl, and a complete Tenkile walking on the ground. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2015: Critically Endangered (CR).
