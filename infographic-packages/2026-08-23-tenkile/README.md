## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Dendrolagus scottae* Flannery & Seri, 1990.
- English public name: Tenkile; Mammal Diversity Database also records Scott's Tree Kangaroo and Tenkile Tree Kangaroo.
- Japanese public name: スコットキノボリカンガルー, supported by the Mammal Society of Japan's 2018 world-mammal name list.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Critically Endangered (CR) under A2d, assessed 30 September 2015 and published in 2019.
- Public claims: it lives in Torricelli mid-montane rainforest, has black fur with a shoulder hair whorl, and spends a high proportion of time on the ground despite being a tree-kangaroo.
- Discovery doorway: a dark tree-kangaroo steps through mossy mountain-forest leaf litter as naturally as it grips the surrounding trunks.
