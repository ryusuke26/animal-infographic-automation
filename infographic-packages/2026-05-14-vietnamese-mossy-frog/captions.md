苔の岩かと思ったら、目だけがこちらを見ている。
Mossy Frog
Theloderma corticale

ベトナム北部などの湿った石灰岩の森で、渓流や洞窟の水辺にひそむ小さなカエル。
でこぼこした緑と茶色の皮ふが、苔のかたまりみたいに岩へ溶けこむ。
水辺のすきまで息をひそめる、ちょっと不思議な暮らし。

IUCN Red List 2017: Least Concern (LC)

## English X Post

At first it looks like wet moss. Then the moss has eyes.
Mossy Frog
Theloderma corticale

This small frog lives around wet limestone forests, rocky streams, and flooded cave pools in northern Vietnam and nearby regions.
Its bumpy green-brown skin lets it disappear into mossy stone.
A quiet, almost-rock kind of life.

IUCN Red List 2017: Least Concern (LC)

#MossyFrog

ALT: Educational poster of a mossy frog on wet moss-covered rock beside a stream or cave pool. The frog has mottled green, brown, and black bumpy skin, large eyes, and sticky toe pads. Short labels describe moss-like skin, stream caves and wet rock, and night insect hunting.

Sources checked: Smithsonian National Zoo, Amphibian Species of the World, IUCN Red List 2017, and Gaia Note. No population number is used.
