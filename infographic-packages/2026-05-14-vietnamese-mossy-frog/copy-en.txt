Mossy Frog
Theloderma corticale

A small frog of wet limestone forests, rocky mountain streams, and flooded cave pools.
Its green, brown, and black bumpy skin makes it look like a clump of moss on stone.
At night, it searches the waterside crevices for insects and other small prey.

It survives by becoming almost part of the rock.

IUCN Red List 2017: Least Concern (LC)
