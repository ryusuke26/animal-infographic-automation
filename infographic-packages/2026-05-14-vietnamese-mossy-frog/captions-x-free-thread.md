## English 140-character thread draft

1.
At first it looks like wet moss. Then the moss has eyes.
Mossy Frog
*Theloderma corticale*

2.
A small frog of wet limestone forests, rocky streams, and flooded cave pools in northern Vietnam and nearby regions.

3.
Its bumpy green, brown, and black skin helps it vanish into mossy stone.

4.
IUCN Red List 2017: Least Concern (LC)
Sources: Smithsonian, ASW, IUCN
