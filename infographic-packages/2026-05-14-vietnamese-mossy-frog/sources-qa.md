## Notes

- IUCN status is cited as the 2017 Red List assessment. The DOI page was not text-readable in this run, so status wording was cross-checked against Smithsonian and the published IUCN DOI metadata known for this species.
- ASW records a broader distribution including Vietnam, China, and Laos. Public copy uses conservative "ベトナム北部など" / "northern Vietnam and nearby regions" rather than a fake map or over-specific range claim.
- No population numbers are used.
- Image identity guidance: mottled moss-green/dark-brown bumpy frog, tubercles/spines, large eyes, adhesive toe pads, wet limestone rock/cave-stream habitat; avoid generic smooth tree frog or bright poison-frog styling.
