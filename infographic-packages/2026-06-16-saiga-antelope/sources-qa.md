## Visual Identity Guidance

- Show one adult saiga antelope, preferably side-on or three-quarter view in open steppe.
- Key body plan: small antelope body, long thin legs, short tail, tan/yellowish-brown coat, pale underside.
- Key head traits: very large downturned bulbous nose hanging over the mouth; long ears; only males have ringed, waxy, slightly translucent horns. A horned male is safest for identity.
- Avoid lookalikes: not a goat, sheep, camel, pronghorn, gazelle without a nose, tapir, pig, or elephant calf.
- Do not show a fake map, dramatic rescue scene, horn-trade imagery, dead animals, disease imagery, or population-count graphics.
