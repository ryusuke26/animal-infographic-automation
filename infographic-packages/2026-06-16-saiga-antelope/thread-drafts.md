## English Thread

1. A dry-steppe antelope with a nose like a tiny weather balloon: Saiga Antelope, *Saiga tatarica*.
2. It lives across Central Asian steppe and semi-desert, moving in herds through open country.
3. The downturned, inflated-looking nose is the clue: this is a saiga, not an ordinary gazelle.
4. IUCN Red List 2023: Near Threatened (NT). No population number here; the category is the quiet footer.
