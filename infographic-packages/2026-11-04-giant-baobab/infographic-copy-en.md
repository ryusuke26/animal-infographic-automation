Title: Giant Baobab
Scientific name: *Adansonia grandidieri*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Stored water grows new leaves
   Explanation: Water stored in the trunk supports the growth of new leaves.
2. Heading: A season without leaves
   Explanation: Leafy in the wet season, it stands bare through much of the dry season.
3. Heading: One leaf, many leaflets
   Explanation: An adult leaf has narrow leaflets radiating from one shared point.
