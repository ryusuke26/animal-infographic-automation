# Thread Drafts - Maned Wolf

## Japanese Short Thread

```text
草の海から、細い脚だけが先に見える。タテガミオオカミ / Chrysocyon brachyurus。
```

```text
南米の草原やサバンナにすむ、ひとり歩きのイヌ科です。
```

```text
キツネにもオオカミにも見えるけれど、別の属。果実も小動物も食べます。
```

```text
IUCN Red List 2015：準絶滅危惧（NT）。出典はADWとSmithsonian。
```

## English Short Thread

```text
A tall shadow in South American grass: the maned wolf, Chrysocyon brachyurus.
```

```text
It is a solitary canid of open grasslands and savannas.
```

```text
It looks foxlike, but it is neither fox nor wolf, and it eats fruit as well as small animals.
```

```text
IUCN Red List 2015: Near Threatened (NT). Sources: ADW and Smithsonian.
```
