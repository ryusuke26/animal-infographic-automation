# Japanese Image Prompt - Maned Wolf

Use case: infographic-diagram

Asset type: Japanese educational social media infographic, vertical 2:3 poster.

Primary request: Create a gentle curiosity-first natural-history infographic about the maned wolf, focused on discovery and education rather than advocacy.

Subject: タテガミオオカミ, *Chrysocyon brachyurus*, one tall slender South American canid in open Cerrado-like grassland. Key identity traits: reddish-golden coat, very long thin black legs, large upright ears, narrow muzzle, black mane along neck/shoulders, subtle pale throat/chest and tail tip. It is not a gray wolf, not a red fox, not a coyote, not a domestic dog, and not a jackal.

Style/medium: childlike crayon and oil-pastel field notebook, textured warm paper, handmade educational tone with adult art direction, polished and readable.

Composition/framing: vertical 2:3 canvas. Top title and scientific name. Center one large hero animal in tall grass. Place exactly three short observation callouts around the hero. Bottom quiet status footer. Generous whitespace. No maps, no population graphics, no rescue or threat scene.

Text (verbatim, Japanese poster only):

```text
タテガミオオカミ
Chrysocyon brachyurus

南米の草原にすむ
長い黒い脚と大きな耳
果実も小動物も食べる

IUCN Red List 2015：準絶滅危惧（NT）
```

Constraints: Accurate legible text. Keep the title and scientific name at the top. Exactly three observation callouts. Discovery-first, not moralizing. Do not invent population numbers. Do not imply blame. No logos, no watermark.

Avoid: Crowded poster, tiny text, fake maps, roadkill, zoo fence, pack scene, pups, gray wolf body, red fox body, coyote, domestic dog, jackal, threat icons, aggressive slogans, misspelled names.
