## Visual Identity Guidance

Show one tall, slender South American canid in open Cerrado-like grassland, with:

- reddish-golden coat;
- very long thin black legs;
- large upright ears;
- narrow foxlike muzzle but not a red fox;
- black mane along neck/shoulders;
- white throat/chest patch and pale tail tip may be subtle;
- solitary standing or slow-walking posture in tall grass;
- optional small yellow-orange wolf-apple-like fruit on ground is acceptable, but no feeding scene is required.

Avoid:

- gray wolf, red fox, coyote, jackal, domestic dog, hyena-like body, pack scene, puppies, zoo fence, fake map, roadkill, rescue framing, population numbers, threat icons.
