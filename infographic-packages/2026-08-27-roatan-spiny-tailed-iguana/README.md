## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Ctenosaura oedirhina* de Queiroz, 1987.
- English public name: Roatán Spiny-tailed Iguana, used by IUCN and the IUCN SSC Iguana Specialist Group.
- Japanese public name: ロータントゲオイグアナ, supported by WWF Japan's Japanese CITES CoP18 proposal summary.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Endangered (EN) under B1ab(v)+2ab(v), assessed 5 June 2018 and published in 2019.
- Public claims: it uses habitats from dry forest and mangroves to beaches and karst limestone; the blunt rounded snout, short low crest, lack of a pendulous dewlap, and ringed tail spines distinguish it; it is primarily herbivorous but also takes varied animal items.
- Discovery doorway: on a Caribbean limestone edge, the island's dark iguana is recognizable not by a giant crest but by a rounded nose and a tail built from rings of spines.
