## Selected identity and naming

- Accepted scientific name: *Ctenosaura oedirhina* de Queiroz, 1987. ITIS, the Reptile Database, and the IUCN SSC Iguana Taxonomy Working Group retain the species.
- Retrieval caveats: older literature sometimes treated material as *Ctenosaura bakeri* or *Enyaliosaurus bakeri*; `Ctenosaura oederhina` is an incorrect subsequent spelling.
- English public name: Roatán Spiny-tailed Iguana. De Queiroz's Spiny-tailed Iguana and Wishywilly are secondary names.
- Japanese public name: ロータントゲオイグアナ, the form used by WWF Japan in its Japanese CITES CoP18 summary.
- Editorial classification group: Reptiles.
- Exact lineage: Animalia; Chordata; Reptilia; Squamata; Iguanidae; Iguaninae; *Ctenosaura*; *Ctenosaura oedirhina*.
- Broad native region: Roatán, Barbareta, and nearby cays, Bay Islands, Honduras / Central America and the Caribbean.
- Habitat: seasonally dry tropical forest, mangroves, beach, karst limestone, scrub forest, rocky shore, and some altered landscapes across the island range.
- Curiosity hook: this dark island iguana is named for a visibly swollen-looking rounded snout, while the more dramatic armor runs in rings down its long tail.
