Title: Western European Hedgehog
Scientific name: *Erinaceus europaeus*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Winter rest has breaks
   Explanation: Hibernation is interrupted by occasional awakenings.
2. Heading: Spines are modified hairs
   Explanation: Spines cover the back, while coarse fur covers the face and belly.
3. Heading: Leaves make a winter nest
   Explanation: Densely packed plant material insulates winter nests better than summer nests.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2023: Near Threatened (NT)
