## Visual identity guidance

- Show one adult Water Chevrotain standing naturally on damp leaf litter beside a narrow forest stream in moist West/Central African tropical forest, preferably in a calm side or three-quarter profile.
- Diagnostic silhouette: compact heavy rounded body, short thick neck, short slender legs, hindquarters visibly larger and slightly higher than the shoulders, small pointed head, small inconspicuous rounded ears, no antlers and no horns.
- Diagnostic coat: brown to reddish brown; two pale horizontal stripes along each flank; rows of pale/white spots across the upper back and sides; two diagonal pale throat stripes. Keep the pattern crisp enough to distinguish it from duikers and Asian mouse-deer.
- Males can have elongated upper canines, but do not make tusks a required poster feature; exaggerated fangs would increase lookalike and anatomy risk.
- Card icons: 1 a shaded rainforest leaf plus stream; 2 a rounded reddish-brown body with white stripe/spot swatch; 3 a small ripple/refuge icon. The third icon must not imply permanent aquatic life or long-distance swimming.
- Avoid: generic slender deer, duiker, Asian mouse-deer, muntjac, antlers, horns, hooves enlarged as a focal point, mouse-like rodent body, pig snout, long rabbit ears, long legs, tall shoulders, uniformly spotted fawn coat without horizontal stripes, zebra-like stripes, swimming mid-river, underwater scene, aquatic hooves, duplicate adults, young/fawn, predator attack, hunting scene, fake map, rescue imagery, population graphic, or printed claims beyond Copy Lock.
