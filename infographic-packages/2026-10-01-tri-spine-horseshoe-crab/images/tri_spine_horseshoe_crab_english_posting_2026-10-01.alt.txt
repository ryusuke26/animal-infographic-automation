A vertical 2:3 crayon and oil pastel field notebook poster shows one tri-spine horseshoe crab from above on a sheltered sandy tidal shore. Its rounded front shield, broad rear shield and single attached tail are visible. Three numbered illustrated cards show beach eggs and a young horseshoe crab on a nearby flat, a close view of three short tail-base spines, and a comparison of chelicerates with true crabs.
