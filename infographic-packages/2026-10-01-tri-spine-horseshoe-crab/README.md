## Locked scope

*Tachypleus tridentatus*, Tri-spine Horseshoe Crab / カブトガニ. Global IUCN Endangered (EN), assessed 22 July 2018; published 2019. One adult, sandy beach plus nursery tidal flat, three fixed tail-base spines and chelicerate identity. Exactly three illustrated explanatory cards per language.

## Production result

Evidence Lock and bilingual cards-v2 Copy Lock passed pre-image validation after final metadata corrections. The first Japanese and English posters passed the exact 2:3 direct-source gate but their Card-2 studies conflated the diagnostic tail-base spines with lateral marginal spines. The initial Japanese retry also failed the visual gate. The user then supplied a close-up with three arrows locating the short fixed spines. Its original and local copy share SHA-256 `13BED268BDDBCBF546096245A845730CAFEC1C704A880755D017436449873243`. This new reference allowed one focused correction per language. The selected Japanese and English Card-2 studies show three distinct short projections at the root of one tail, separate from the wider margins. The accepted images are educational sketches; the photo and cited biological sources remain the anatomy evidence.

Both selected direct images again passed the 1024x1536 exact-2:3 source gate. Whole-canvas review checked the shield hierarchy, one attached tail, natural occlusion, all nine locked strings, exactly three explanatory illustrated cards and visible crayon/oil-pastel pigment in hero and cards. A [342x513 phone-size comparison](audit/phone-review-selected-2026-10-01.png) passed manual legibility review. The posting PNGs are each 1024x1536 and pixel-identical to the respective selected direct source; eight copy-ready sidecars synchronize from the primary X sets. `validate_package.py` passes. The user explicitly adopted the selected artwork with “これで行こう” on 1 October 2026. At the user's request, six failed/provisional package PNGs and their three matching failed generation-cache originals were moved to the Windows Recycle Bin; all 17 protected posters, sidecars, references and accepted cache sources retained their SHA-256 hashes. The [cleanup manifest](audit/cleanup-manifest-2026-10-01.json) records the exact paths and hashes. Accepted audit copies and selected QA images remain. Nothing was posted to X.

The package and INDEX were published to GitHub in content commit `2a00b1bae4498eec21641f11894ec5a4a69efd86` by a direct push to `origin/master`. Remote `refs/heads/master` was verified at that commit before this published-state metadata update.
