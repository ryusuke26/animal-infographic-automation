Title: Tri-spine Horseshoe Crab
Scientific name: *Tachypleus tridentatus*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: From beach to tidal flat
   Explanation: Eggs are laid on sandy beaches, while young grow on nearby tidal flats.
2. Heading: Three spines by the tail
   Explanation: Three fixed spines at the tail base help distinguish this species.
3. Heading: Not a true crab
   Explanation: Despite its name, it belongs to the chelicerates, not the crustaceans.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2018: Endangered (EN)
