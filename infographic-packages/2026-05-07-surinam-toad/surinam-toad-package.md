## 1. Selection Rationale

Today's topic is the Surinam toad, Japanese common name コモリガエル, scientific name *Pipa pipa*. The hook is not rarity or crisis, but a quietly strange amphibian life: a flat, leaf-like aquatic frog whose young develop in pockets on the female's back.

This shifts away from the recent sequence of floating fish, African grassland mammal, New Guinea bird, sea slug, and desert plant by moving to a South American freshwater amphibian.
