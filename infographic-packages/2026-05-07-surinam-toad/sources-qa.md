## Publication-Safe Fact Notes

- Do not describe the birth behavior with graphic "erupts" or "bursting" language in public copy.
- Do not include population numbers.
- Do not use a fake map; use habitat cues only.
- Keep the taxonomy caveat out of the poster unless a long explainer is requested.
- Use "Surinam toad" in English; "Suriname toad" is a source variant.
- Use コモリガエル in Japanese; ピパ/ピパピパ appear in some references but are not the poster title.

## Image Identity Checklist

- Very flat leaf-like brown aquatic frog/toad.
- Triangular flat head and tiny eyes.
- Mottled rough brown skin.
- Webbed hind feet.
- Front fingers with small star-like sensory tips.
- Muddy slow-water bottom, leaf litter, and soft aquatic plants.
- Back-brooding shown gently as non-graphic pockets or small round marks.
- Not a green tree frog, not a land toad, not a poison frog, not horror.
