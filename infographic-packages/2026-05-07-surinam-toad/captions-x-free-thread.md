## Japanese

1. 水底の枯れ葉みたいなカエル、コモリガエル。背中で子ガエルが育つ。Surinam toad / *Pipa pipa*

2. 南米のゆるやかな川や沼にすむ、完全に水の中で暮らすピパ科のカエルです。

3. 星形の指先で獲物を探し、卵は雌の背中のくぼみで育ちます。

4. IUCN Red List 2023: Least Concern (LC). Sources: IUCN, ASW, USGS, ADW.

## English

1. A leaf-flat frog with young growing on its back: Surinam toad, *Pipa pipa*.

2. It lives in slow waters and muddy ponds across northern South America and Trinidad.

3. Star-tipped fingers help it feel for prey; young develop in pockets on the female's back.

4. IUCN Red List 2023: Least Concern (LC). Sources: IUCN, ASW, USGS, ADW.
