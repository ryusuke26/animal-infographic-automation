水底の枯れ葉みたいに、背中で小さな命を待つカエル。  
Surinam toad  
*Pipa pipa*

南米のゆるやかな川や沼の底で、平たい体を沈めて暮らします。  
星形の指先で獲物を探し、舌を使わずに小さな水生動物を吸いこみます。  
背中のくぼみで子ガエルが育つ、ちょっと不思議な暮らし。

IUCN Red List 2023: Least Concern (LC)

## English X Post

A leaf-flat frog waits on the muddy bottom, carrying the next generation on her back.  
Surinam toad  
*Pipa pipa*

In slow rivers, canals, ponds, and swamps of northern South America and Trinidad, this fully aquatic frog lives close to the bottom.  
Star-tipped fingers help it feel for small prey.  
Young develop in pockets on the female's back: a quiet, strange freshwater life.

IUCN Red List 2023: Least Concern (LC)
