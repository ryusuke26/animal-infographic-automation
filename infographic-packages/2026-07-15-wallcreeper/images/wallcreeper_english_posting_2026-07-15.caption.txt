A tiny bird for giant cliffs—look for the red wings.
Wallcreeper
Tichodroma muraria

In summer it searches high mountain rock faces near the tree line. In winter, it may visit lower walls and quarries.
Its curved bill reaches into deep cracks for insects and spiders.
Crimson wings flicker over the cliff face: a very strange little life.

IUCN Red List 2019: Least Concern (LC)
