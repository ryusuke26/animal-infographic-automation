## Visual identity guidance

- Show one small Wallcreeper, not a generic red bird: slate-grey body, black flight and tail feathers, crimson wing coverts with visible white spots, long thin black curved bill, short tail, and two small dark legs.
- Use a safe natural posture on a near-vertical pale limestone or dark alpine cliff, with one wing partly open or flicking; the bird should still read as a passerine, not a woodpecker or butterfly.
- Background: high mountain rock face, sparse alpine plants, quiet cool sky; a small lower-elevation wall hint is optional but not required.
- Avoid: woodpecker tail-bracing, nuthatch shape, swallow wings, hummingbird hovering, a red butterfly, a generic red-and-black songbird, duplicate birds, fake range maps, crowded town scenes, captive cages, or conservation action imagery.
