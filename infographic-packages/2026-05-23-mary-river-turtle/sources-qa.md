## QA Notes

- No population number is used.
- IUCN status is written as `IUCN Red List 1996: Endangered (EN)`. Australian national EPBC status is treated separately in README notes because later domestic listings can differ from the global IUCN assessment.
- The Japanese common name `カクレガメ` is supported by Japanese reptile/pet-trade references, but public copy also includes the English common name and scientific name.
- Conservation stays as a quiet footer; the poster leads with river habitat, algae growth, and cloacal respiration.
- Image identity target: low streamlined freshwater turtle, moderately short neck, small head, broad webbed feet, very long tail, green filamentous algae on head/shell, clear freshwater river stones and aquatic plants.
- Avoid visual mistakes: sea turtle flippers, tortoise legs, snapping turtle head, fake distribution map, mohawk/punk caricature, population icons.
