## Image Identity Guidance

Show a freshwater turtle underwater, not a sea turtle, tortoise, or snapping turtle. Key cues: low streamlined shell, moderately short neck, small head, large webbed feet, very long tail extending behind the shell, and soft green filamentous algae on the head and shell. Use clear river water, rounded stones, and aquatic plants. Avoid fake maps, punk-rock caricature, horror styling, and population-count visuals.
