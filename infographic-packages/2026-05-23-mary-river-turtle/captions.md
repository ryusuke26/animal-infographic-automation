## Japanese

川底で、緑の髪みたいな藻をまとっている。
Mary River Turtle
Elusor macrurus

オーストラリア・クイーンズランド州のメアリー川だけにすむ淡水ガメです。
長い尾と、藻をまとった姿が目印。
しっぽ側の器官で水中の酸素も使う、ちょっと不思議な暮らし。

IUCN Red List 1996: Endangered (EN)

## English

In one Queensland river, a freshwater turtle can wear algae like green hair.
Mary River Turtle
Elusor macrurus

This turtle is known only from the Mary River system in Queensland, Australia.
Its long tail, webbed feet, and algae-covered shell help make it unmistakable.
It also uses cloacal glands to take oxygen from the water: a quietly unusual river life.

IUCN Red List 1996: Endangered (EN)
