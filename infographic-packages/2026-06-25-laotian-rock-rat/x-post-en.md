In Lao limestone karst, a small dark rodent carries a fossil-known lineage into the present.
Laotian Rock Rat / Kha-nyou
Laonastes aenigmamus

It lives among limestone rocks, with long whiskers and a thick furry tail.
Genetic work found many local lineages across separate karst blocks.

IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC)
