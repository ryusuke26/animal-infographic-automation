- Accepted scientific name: *Myobatrachus gouldii* (Gray, 1841).
- Taxonomy caveat: historical names include *Breviceps gouldii*, *Myobatrachus paradoxus*, and *Chelydobatrachus gouldii*. Public copy uses the accepted current name.
- Native range: southwestern Western Australia, from near Geraldton south and east through the southwest; public copy avoids a map and uses the broader safe wording.
- Habitat: deep sandy soils, often near termite colonies; the species is largely subterranean.
- Distinctive traits and behavior: globular body, very small head and eyes, short muscular forelimbs, forward/headfirst burrowing, largely termite-feeding ecology.
- Reproduction: eggs are deposited in moist sand in burrows and complete development inside the egg capsule; froglets hatch without a free-living tadpole stage.
