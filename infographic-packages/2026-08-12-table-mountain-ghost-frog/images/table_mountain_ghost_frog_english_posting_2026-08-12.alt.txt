Vertical 2:3 handmade Cape-mountain field-notebook poster. One Table Mountain Ghost Frog, Heleophryne rosei, crouches low on wet sandstone beside a tea-colored torrent. The complete adult has a flattened pale-green body with purple-brown mottling, large eyes with vertical pupils, four separate long limbs, broad terminal toe discs, and webbed hind feet. The English title and scientific name sit at the top. Three unequal numbered illustrated cards show the Table Mountain stream, one complete small adult gripping wet rock, and one complete tadpole clinging to stone with its sucker-like mouth. The footer reads IUCN Red List 2024: Endangered (EN).
