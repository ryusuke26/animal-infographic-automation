## Locked visual concept

One complete adult crouches naturally on a wet sandstone ledge beside a tea-colored Table Mountain torrent. Its low flattened body, pale-green ground color, reddish-purple to brown mottling, vertical pupils, broad terminal toe discs, and webbed hind feet remain readable. Three unequal stream-shaped field-note cards follow the water and rock contours: the shaded perennial cascade, one complete mini adult gripping wet rock, and one complete tadpole clinging to a stone with its sucker-like oral disc. The composition uses sandstone rubbings, fern silhouettes, fynbos marks, and current lines rather than equal software panels.
