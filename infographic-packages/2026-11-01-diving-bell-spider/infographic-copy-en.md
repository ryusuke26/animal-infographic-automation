Title: Diving Bell Spider
Scientific name: *Argyroneta aquatica*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Oxygen enters the bubble
   Explanation: Dissolved oxygen moves from the water into the air-filled bell.
2. Heading: Carrying air on body hairs
   Explanation: Air from the surface clings to abdominal hairs for the trip to the bell.
3. Heading: A silk room among plants
   Explanation: The air-filled silk retreat is a place for feeding and egg-laying.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2023: Near Threatened (NT)
