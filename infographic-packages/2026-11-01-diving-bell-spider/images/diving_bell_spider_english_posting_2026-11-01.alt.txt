Vertical gouache poster of a deep teal freshwater pond. A large diving bell spider grips aquatic plants, with a brown anterior body and jointed legs and an oval abdomen covered by a silvery air film. Title and scientific name Argyroneta aquatica at top. Illustrated note 1 shows a silk-supported air bell open underneath, with arrows from the surrounding water into the air space to represent dissolved oxygen transfer. Note 2 shows a small spider just below the surface carrying air on its abdomen and a connected hair detail. Note 3 shows a silk retreat among plants with a small spider feeding at its lower opening. Each has a number, heading and explanatory sentence. Footer: IUCN Red List 2023: Near Threatened (NT). Arrows and magnification are schematic.
