## Taxonomy And Naming Caveats

- AMNH Amphibian Species of the World 6.2 accepts *Calotriton arnoldi* Carranza and Amat, 2005.
- AMNH/ASW lists "Montseny Brook Newt" as the original-publication common name and "Montseny Newt" as another English common name.
- AMNH/ASW records two subspecific names from 2024: *Calotriton arnoldi arnoldi* and *Calotriton arnoldi laietanus*. This package stays at species level and does not depict or discuss either subspecies separately.
- Japanese title `モンセニー渓流イモリ` is a safe descriptive rendering, not a claim of a standardized Japanese common name.

## Visual Identity Guidance

- Show one small dark chocolate-brown newt/salamander-like amphibian in a shallow, cold, rocky mountain stream.
- Body: low, slightly flattened head and body; long laterally flattened tail; four small limbs; four front toes and five hind toes if toes are visible.
- Skin: dark brown/chocolate tone, subtle rough texture, possible tiny pale speckles but no bright alpine-newt orange belly display.
- Posture: calm natural crawling/resting posture among wet stones; no dramatic rescue scene or handling pose.
- Avoid: generic lizard, fish, axolotl gills, salamander with external gills, bright crested newt, orange-bellied alpine newt, fire salamander yellow patches, duplicate animals, fake map, population count, threat icons, or advocacy slogan.
