Title: Ginkgo
Scientific name: *Ginkgo biloba*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: A seed dressed like fruit
   Explanation: The yellow flesh is a seed coat, not fruit flesh.
2. Heading: Veins that fork in two
   Explanation: Fine veins divide in two across each fan-shaped leaf.
3. Heading: Gold beneath the tree
   Explanation: Leaves turn yellow, then fall to carpet the ground.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 1998: Endangered (EN)
