Vertical natural-history plate in the style of a hand-coloured copperplate engraving. A female Wallace's Giant Bee stands in three-quarter front view on a brown termite nest built on a tree trunk: glossy black body, huge sickle-shaped stag-beetle-like jaws, large dark-brown eyes, one pale cream band at the front of the abdomen and amber wings. Three numbered figures surround her: 1, a large cut-away of the nest with a tunnel lined in amber resin and tiny termites; 2, a round medallion of her face holding a ball of resin in her jaws; 3, a size comparison with a much smaller honeybee. Each has a heading and explanation. Footer: IUCN Red List 2013: Vulnerable (VU).
