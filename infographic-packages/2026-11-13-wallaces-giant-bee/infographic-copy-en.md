Title: Wallace's Giant Bee
Scientific name: *Megachile pluto*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Living inside a termite nest
   Explanation: She digs tunnels into a tree termite nest and lines them with resin that hardens and keeps termites out.
2. Heading: Jaws only females carry
   Explanation: Stag-beetle-like jaws gather tree resin into a ball and carry it back to the nest.
3. Heading: The largest known bee
   Explanation: A female is about four times the size of a honeybee; males are much smaller and lack the big jaws.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2013: Vulnerable (VU)
