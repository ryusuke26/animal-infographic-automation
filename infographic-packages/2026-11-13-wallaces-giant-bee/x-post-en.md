## Story reply
```text
Wallace collected just one female on Bacan. What the huge jaws were for stayed unknown until Adam Messer watched nests on Halmahera in 1981. In January 2019, after five days of searching, a team found a female at a termite nest and filmed it alive for the first time.
```
