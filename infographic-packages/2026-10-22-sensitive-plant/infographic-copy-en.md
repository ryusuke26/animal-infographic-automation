Title: Sensitive Plant
Scientific name: *Mimosa pudica*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Water powers the fold
   Explanation: Water pressure changes at leaflet bases, folding the leaflets shut.
2. Heading: A ball of tiny flowers
   Explanation: Many small flowers form a pink head, with fine stamens on show.
3. Heading: Night is another cue
   Explanation: The leaflets also close at night, even without being touched.
