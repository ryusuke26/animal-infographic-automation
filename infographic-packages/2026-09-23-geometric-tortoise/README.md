## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Psammobates geometricus* (Linnaeus, 1758).
- Public names: Geometric Tortoise / ホシヤブガメ. The Japanese name is database-backed but not claimed as a standardized Japanese national-list name.
- Status: current IUCN errata record `T18398A173894530`, Global Critically Endangered (CR) under A4ace, assessed 13 June 2017, published 2018; errata version published in 2020.
- Public claims: yellow rays meet across dark shell scutes in a geometric network; the pattern is well camouflaged among low shrub shadows; during hot dry summer the tortoise shelters in slightly damp microhabitats beneath dense vegetation.
- Discovery doorway: a shell that looks covered in bright yellow stars can almost disappear in low renosterveld.
