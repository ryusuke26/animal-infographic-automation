Vertical 2:3 crayon-and-oil-pastel natural-history poster. One Geometric Tortoise, Psammobates geometricus, walks through low Western Cape renosterveld. Its high rounded black-brown shell carries golden-yellow rays that meet across scute seams. Three numbered illustrated cards show the connected shell pattern, camouflage among shrub shadows, and shelter in slightly damp ground beneath dense plants during hot dry summer. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2017: Critically Endangered (CR).
