Title: Common Flying Dragon
Scientific name: *Draco volans*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Ribs hold the membrane
   Explanation: Elongated ribs support the skin membranes on both sides.
2. Heading: Ants on the menu
   Explanation: This tree-dwelling lizard feeds mainly on ants.
3. Heading: A small blue throat flap
   Explanation: Females have a small bluish flap; males have a yellow one.
