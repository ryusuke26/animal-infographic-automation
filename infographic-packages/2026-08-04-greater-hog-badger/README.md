## Discovery doorway

夜の森で、長いブタのような鼻先が落ち葉を押し分ける。その鼻と大きな前爪がたどる地面から、ブタバナアナグマの暮らしへ入る。

At night, a long pig-like snout parts the leaf litter. That nose and the large foreclaws following the forest floor are the doorway into the Greater Hog Badger's life.

It was originally published in 2016 and republished as a 2024 amendment that changes only one assessor's initials. The poster and main-post footers correctly use the field-level assessment year 2015.
