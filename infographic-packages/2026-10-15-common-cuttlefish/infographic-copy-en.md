Title: Common Cuttlefish
Scientific name: *Sepia officinalis*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Skin changes shape, too
   Explanation: Color, pattern and skin bumps change to help it hide.
2. Heading: A shell that helps it float
   Explanation: A white internal shell holds gas and helps control buoyancy.
3. Heading: Eggs like black grapes
   Explanation: Inky eggs are laid in clusters on seaweed and other supports.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2009: Least Concern (LC)
