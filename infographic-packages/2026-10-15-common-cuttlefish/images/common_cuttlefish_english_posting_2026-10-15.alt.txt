Vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel observation poster. A brown mottled common cuttlefish rests over a sandy, pebbly seabed. A thin fin skirt borders its broad oval mantle; its head faces left, with arms folded together in front. Three numbered cards illustrate changing skin pattern and bumps, a white internal cuttlebone used in buoyancy control, and black egg clusters attached to seaweed. Each has a heading and explanatory sentence. The title and Sepia officinalis appear above; the footer gives the 2009 global IUCN assessment, Least Concern (LC).
