## Locked identity

- English common name: Cookiecutter Shark
- Japanese common name: ダルマザメ
- Accepted scientific name: *Isistius brasiliensis* (Quoy & Gaimard, 1824)
- Lineage: Chondrichthyes, Squaliformes, Dalatiidae
- Broad native region: Ocean/Global; a circumtropical oceanic species recorded across the Atlantic, Pacific, and Indian oceans.
- Habitat: pelagic ocean waters, from near the surface to deep water; the documented range reaches about 3,700 m, with deep daytime and shallower nighttime movement.
- Visual identity guidance: small cigar-shaped shark, short bulbous snout, dark collar around the throat, pale underside, two small close-set dorsal fins far back on the body, large nearly symmetrical tail, and no anal fin. Do not depict a large generic shark, a reef shark, a cookie, a gore scene, a bite close-up, or the related Largetooth Cookiecutter Shark (*Isistius plutodus*).
