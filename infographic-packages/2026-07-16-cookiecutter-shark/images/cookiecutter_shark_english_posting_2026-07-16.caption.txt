A shark that glows blue—and leaves cookie-shaped bites.
Cookiecutter Shark
Isistius brasiliensis

In warm oceanic waters, it dives deep by day and rises at night.
Its belly glows blue; it is a small shark, about 50 cm long.
It can take a round plug of flesh from larger fish and whales—a slightly strange way to live.

IUCN Red List 2017: Least Concern (LC)
