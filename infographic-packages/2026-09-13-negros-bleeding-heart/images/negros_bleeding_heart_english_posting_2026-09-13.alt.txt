Vertical 2:3 handmade field-notebook poster. In dense Philippine forest, one adult Negros Bleeding-heart, Gallicolumba keayi, walks over moss and leaf litter. Its dark emerald head and upper body, white face and belly, narrow vertical red breast patch, two white wing bars, chestnut rear wing and long pink-red legs are visible. The common and scientific names sit at the top. Three numbered illustrated cards show the distinctive narrow breast mark, leaf-litter foraging and its association with dense understorey and canopy. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2020: Critically Endangered (CR).
