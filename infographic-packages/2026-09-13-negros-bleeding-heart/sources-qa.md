## Selected identity and naming

- Accepted public scientific name: *Gallicolumba keayi* (Clarke, 1900), used by current IUCN, BirdLife and IOC-linked taxonomy sources.
- Historical combination for duplicate screening: *Phlogoenas keayi* Clarke, 1900.
- English public name: Negros Bleeding-heart; Negros Bleeding-heart Pigeon is a supported expanded form.
- Japanese public name: ネグロスヒムネバト, a database-backed Japanese rendering exposed by Cornell BirdNET taxonomy. It is not presented as a formally standardized Japanese national bird-list name.
- Editorial classification group: Birds.
- Exact lineage: Animalia; Chordata; Aves; Columbiformes; Columbidae; *Gallicolumba*.
- Broad native region: Panay and Negros, Western Visayas, Philippines / Southeast Asia.
- Habitat: terrestrial tropical lowland forest, especially sites with dense understorey and dense canopy; the species spends much of its time on the forest floor.
- Curiosity hook: within white breast feathers, the namesake red mark is not a broad heart but a narrow vertical line that flashes as the bird walks through dense forest-floor shade.

- Diagnostic features: (1) stocky ground-dove body with small rounded head, short dark bill and relatively long pink-red walking legs; (2) dark emerald-green crown, nape, mantle and upper breast beside a clean white lower face, throat and belly; (3) a single narrow vertical blood-red breast patch, about 5 mm wide and roughly 26 mm long in Curio's live birds, centered within white breast feathers; (4) two bold white arcs/bars across otherwise dark green-black folded wing coverts; (5) a chestnut-brown rear wing panel and one short dark tapered tail.
- Natural pose: eye-level three-quarter side view walking on mossy leaf litter beneath dense tropical understorey. Both legs and the near folded wing are readable; the far wing and part of the far flank may be naturally occluded by the body.

- Uncertainty: fine feather counts and sexual differences are not turned into visual claims. The BirdLife photograph is 750x750 and slightly soft but clearly resolves all five locked feature zones; Curio's measured description closes the breast-patch and wing-bar details.
