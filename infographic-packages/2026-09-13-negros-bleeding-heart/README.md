## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Gallicolumba keayi* (Clarke, 1900); original combination *Phlogoenas keayi* is retained for duplicate screening.
- English public name: Negros Bleeding-heart.
- Japanese public name: ネグロスヒムネバト, used as a database-backed rendering rather than a claimed standardized national-list name.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Critically Endangered (CR) under C2a(i), assessed 11 August 2020, record `T22690988A181469833`.
- Public claims: the red breast patch is unusually narrow among bleeding-hearts; the bird tosses leaf litter aside while foraging; surveys found it more often where both understorey and canopy cover were dense.
- Discovery doorway: a slim red line appears between white breast feathers as a green ground dove steps through deep forest shade.
