# Sources and QA - Green-banded Broodsac

## Claim Check

| Claim | Verdict | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| *Leucochloridium paradoxum* Carus, 1835 is the target species. | accurate | GBIF and NCBI agree on the species name. |
| It is a trematode flatworm in Leucochloridiidae. | accurate | GBIF gives Animalia > Platyhelminthes > Trematoda > Diplostomida > Leucochloridiidae. |
| It uses succineid/amber snails and birds in its life cycle. | accurate | ADW and recent sporocyst-development literature support snail and bird host framing. |
| The visible green-banded broodsac can occur in the snail tentacle/eyestalk. | accurate | ADW describes colored pulsating tentacles; green-banded broodsac literature supports the public common-name hook. |
| Range includes Europe and North America. | accurate with caveat | ADW gives Europe/North America via host range. Hokkaido green-banded *Leucochloridium* records may not be identical to *L. paradoxum*, so Japan is excluded from public copy. |
| The broodsac mimics a caterpillar. | needs context | Usable as source context, but public copy says only `鳥の目にとまりやすい姿` to avoid overclaiming. |
| Conservation status is Not Evaluated. | needs context | ADW displays IUCN Not Evaluated. Direct public IUCN search did not locate a formal species assessment on 2026-05-29. |

## Public Wording Decisions

- Use `緑しまのブルードサック` as a descriptive Japanese title because a standardized Japanese common name was not strongly confirmed.
- Avoid `ゾンビ`, `脳を乗っ取る`, `目を食べる`, and other sensational phrases.
- Avoid Japan range wording because checked Hokkaido material is reported as green-banded *Leucochloridium* and may not equal *L. paradoxum*.
- Keep the status footer quiet and short.
