Green-banded Broodsac
Leucochloridium paradoxum
Trematode flatworm

In damp woods and grassy places, this small parasite links the lives of amber snails and birds.

Its larval broodsac can appear inside a snail eyestalk as green and brown bands.

That conspicuous little signal is part of a life cycle that continues when a bird eats the infected snail or broodsac.

A tiny, strange life on wet leaves.

IUCN Red List: Not Evaluated (NE)
