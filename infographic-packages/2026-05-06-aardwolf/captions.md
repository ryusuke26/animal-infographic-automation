アフリカ東部と南部の乾いた草原で、昼は穴にひそみ、夜に歩きだします。

## English X Post

A hyena relative walks the night grassland for termites.  
Aardwolf  
*Proteles cristatus*

In dry open country of eastern and southern Africa, the aardwolf rests in burrows by day and forages after dark.  
With a long sticky tongue, it laps termites instead of chasing large prey.  
A quiet termite-trail life, just strange enough to make you look twice.

IUCN Red List: Least Concern (LC)
