## 1. Selection Rationale

This is a redo of today's daily task. The earlier 2026-05-06 Sargassum frogfish package is left intact, and this package is created as a separate redo package.

Today's redo topic is the aardwolf, Japanese common name アードウルフ, also called ツチオオカミ. It looks like a small striped hyena, but it mainly eats termites with a long sticky tongue. This makes it a good curiosity-first topic: familiar hyena-like outline, unexpectedly quiet insect-eating life.

It also shifts away from the recent sequence of desert plant, sea slug, toxic bird, and floating fish by moving to a dry African grassland mammal.
