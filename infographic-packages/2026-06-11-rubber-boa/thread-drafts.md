## English Thread

1. Rubber Boa, *Charina bottae*, is a small boa native to western North America.
2. It shelters under rocks, logs, leaf litter, and in burrows, often in forested or rocky cover.
3. Its smooth tube-like body ends in a short blunt tail that can be presented defensively.
4. IUCN Red List 2019: Least Concern (LC). No population number or threat claim is used here.
