# Rubber Boa Infographic Package

Date: 2026-06-11
Topic: Rubber Boa / ラバーボア
Scientific name: *Charina bottae*
Broad native region: North America
State: local-ready, not published

## Why This Topic

North America had no appearances among the previous eight completed packages, and the previous run was Ocean/Global. A small terrestrial boa in cool forest-floor cover changes the lineage, habitat, and visual hook from recent oceanic, plant, mammal, fungal, and amphibian topics.

## Completion Notes

- Evidence Lock completed before Image Gen.
- Japanese and English Copy Lock completed before Image Gen.
- Scientific name, taxonomy caveat, native region, three public claims, exact footer, and visual identity guidance are settled.
- Japanese title is a safe katakana rendering, not a verified standardized Japanese common name.
- Public copy omits population numbers, threat claims, fake maps, and head-mimic certainty.
- The first direct Japanese and English Image Gen posters were rejected during user review.
- Japanese v1 has an overly pointed upright tail that reads like a second head, and its tight coil makes the head-to-tail path difficult to trace.
- English v1 has a segmented, pointed upright tail that reads like a rattle or horn rather than the short rounded tail of a rubber boa.
- Both v1 posters exaggerate scale size and body mass, making the animal read closer to a heavy python than a small smooth-scaled rubber boa.
- Text matched Copy Lock, but correct text does not override failed anatomy and identity QA.
- Japanese replacement candidate v2 removes the tight coil and shows one traceable side-on body with a flat rounded tail. It is anatomically coherent, but the taper toward the tail is longer and slimmer than ideal.
- English replacement candidate v2 was rejected for adding unapproved explanatory text.
- English replacement candidate v3 has exact locked text and a traceable head-to-tail body with a flat rounded tail. It is coherent, but the body remains somewhat stout.
- A matched-layout Japanese localization was created from English candidate v3. It preserves the exact same snake, background, framing, inset images, and footer; only the title and three observation labels are replaced locally with checked Japanese text.
- The matched-layout Japanese file is deterministic localization, not a separate direct Japanese Image Gen poster, so it solves design consistency but does not by itself satisfy the automation's direct-bilingual-generation completion rule.
- A later direct Japanese edit removed the duplicated upper `岩や倒木の下にひそむ` callout. The first edit incorrectly changed the lower-left label and was rejected; corrected v4 restores the lower-left label and leaves no duplicate upper callout.
- Side-by-side review of corrected Japanese v4 and English v3 confirms that each of the three observation labels appears once and no extra callout remains.
- End-of-day review accepted corrected Japanese v4 and English v3 as the final direct posters.
- The corrected files were promoted to the canonical Japanese and English direct-poster filenames.
- Final visual QA passed: one continuous side-on snake, one small blunt head, one flat rounded tail, no second-head/rattle reading, no duplicated upper callout, and each locked observation label appears once.
- Text-safe Japanese and English SVG/PNG backups exist.
- Direct Image Gen posters are 1023 x 1537 (Japanese) and 1024 x 1536 (English). Text-safe PNGs are 1200 x 1500.
- Optional `generated_images/animal_img` mirror was not attempted.
