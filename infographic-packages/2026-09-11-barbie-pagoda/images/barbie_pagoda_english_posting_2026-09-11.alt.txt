Vertical 2:3 handmade field-notebook poster. In humid southern New Caledonian forest, one under-10-cm fruit body of the Barbie Pagoda, Podoserpula miranda, rises from the base of a dark oak gum trunk. Five to six thin funnel-shaped caps become smaller toward the top along one continuous pink-lilac stem. Their upper surfaces are pale and cottony, while the exposed undersides are vivid pink-lilac with radiating wrinkles. The common and scientific names sit at the top. Three numbered illustrated cards show the tiered caps, emergence during wetter El Niño events, and the trunk-base association. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2019: Critically Endangered (CR).
