## Selected identity and naming

- Accepted public scientific name: *Podoserpula miranda* Buyck, Duhem, Eyssartier & Ducousso, validly described in 2012 and registered as MycoBank MB 802706.
- Duplicate-name coverage: *Podoserpula miranda* nom. prov. in the 2009 preliminary report; no accepted taxonomic synonym was found.
- English public names: Barbie Pagoda; Barbie Pagoda Fungus; Pagoda Barbie in French local material.
- Japanese public name: バービー・パゴダ, a transparent transliteration rather than a claimed standardized Japanese fungus-list name.
- Editorial classification group: Fungi and lichens.
- Exact lineage: Fungi; Basidiomycota; Agaricomycetes; Amylocorticiales; Amylocorticiaceae; *Podoserpula*. The GFRLI/IUCN mirror displays the older order placement Boletales; the species description and later phylogenetic literature support Amylocorticiales.
- Broad native region: southern Grande Terre, New Caledonia / Oceania.
- Habitat: humid evergreen forest, at the base of endemic oak gum (*Arillastrum gummiferum*) trunks.
- Curiosity hook: an under-10-cm pink pagoda rises in tiers when the forest gets an unusually wet season.
