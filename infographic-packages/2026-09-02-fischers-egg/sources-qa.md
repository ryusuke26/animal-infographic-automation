## Selected identity and naming

- Accepted scientific name: *Claustula fischeri* K.M.Curtis.
- English public name: Fischer's Egg, used by the IUCN assessment and Atlas of Living Australia. Australian alias: Bunyip Egg.
- Japanese public name: フィッシャーズ・エッグ, a transparent rendering of Fischer's Egg. No established standard Japanese common name was confirmed.
- Editorial classification group: Fungi and lichens.
- Exact lineage: Fungi; Basidiomycota; Agaricomycetes; Phallales; Claustulaceae; *Claustula*.
- Broad native region: Tasmania, Australia, and New Zealand / Oceania.
- Habitat: damp ground in native forest, with records associated with *Eucalyptus*, *Nothofagus*, *Leptospermum*, or *Kunzea* forest.
- Curiosity hook: the small white hollow fruitbody resembles an egg emerging from a brown shell-like layer, but its centre holds a dry brown spore mass.

## Visual identity lock

- Reference role: the two official IUCN photographs are species-identity references only, never edit targets. They show intact, emerging, and cut specimens against a laboratory background; the poster must place the form naturally in habitat.
- Hero: exactly one dominant mature fruitbody partly above damp forest litter, 2–3 cm scale, globose to ovoid, with a smooth matte ivory-white hollow body emerging through an irregular earthy brown outer layer at the base and one side.
- Interior: one small natural tear may reveal a dry chocolate-brown spore mass; no liquid, yolk, seeds, gelatin, stinkhorn slime, or bright gleba.
- Habitat: cool damp Tasmanian or New Zealand native forest floor with wet brown leaves, fine roots, moss, small twigs, soft fern shadows, and restrained *Nothofagus* or eucalypt cues. No farm nest, kitchen, plate, basket, garden lawn, fantasy forest, or fake map.
- Card art: card 1 shows a forest-floor habitat vignette; card 2 shows a small emergence sequence with the brown outer layer parting around a white ovoid body; card 3 shows a clean scientific cutaway of the thick white wall and dry brown internal spore mass.
- False silhouettes to avoid: chicken or bird egg, cracked food egg, yolk, puffball, earthball, truffle, potato, bulb, seed, stinkhorn egg with gelatinous layers, mushroom cap and stem, nest, shell fragments, animal embryo, glossy ceramic sphere, or multiple dominant fruitbodies.
