## 1. Selection rationale

Today this package uses *Glaucus atlanticus*, a small pelagic nudibranch commonly called the blue glaucus or blue dragon, and in Japanese アオミノウミウシ.

It was selected because it changes lineage and habitat from the recent archive: after Welwitschia, Yeti crab, Long-eared Jerboa, Star-nosed Mole, Hispaniolan Solenodon, and Olm, this run shifts to a surface-drifting marine gastropod. Its upside-down life at the ocean surface, blue/silver countershading, and use of prey stinging cells create a strong natural-history hook without needing population numbers or urgency framing.
