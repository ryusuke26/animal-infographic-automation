## Visual Identity Guidance

Show a small semi-aquatic desman in a clear, cold mountain stream. Key identity cues: elongated flexible trunk-like snout, tiny eyes, brown-grey fur, large hind feet adapted for swimming, long tail with stiff hairs, wet stones, shallow riffle water. Avoid making it an otter, mouse, rat, platypus, elephant shrew, star-nosed mole, shrew with a sharp beak, beaver, or generic cartoon mole. Do not add fake maps, population counters, rescue slogans, or human-caused blame imagery.
