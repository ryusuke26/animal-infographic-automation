## Evidence route

Kew Plants of the World Online accepts *Strongylodon macrobotrys*, lists *S. megaphyllus* and *S. warburgii* as synonyms, and records the Philippines as its native range and wet tropical forest as its biome. Kew's current Jade Vine page supports the English and Japanese common names, morphology, and bat-pollination story and displays IUCN Red List status Not Evaluated. Exact official IUCN searches under the accepted name and both synonyms did not surface a global assessment on 2026-08-07, so public copy uses the conservative completed-search wording rather than `IUCN NE`.

## Locked visual concept

One connected woody vine climbs a mossy Philippine rainforest tree and drops one dominant, long jade-blue flower truss through the center. Three unequal hand-drawn observation cards follow the vine's story: rainforest home, one attached double-hooked flower, and one complete small bat visiting a flower at night. The palette and card rhythm are composed around the hanging botanical silhouette rather than equal software panels.
