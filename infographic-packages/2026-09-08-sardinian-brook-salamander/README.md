## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Euproctus platycephalus* (Gravenhorst, 1829); *Euproctus rusconii* and *Molge platycephala* are retained for duplicate screening.
- English public name: Sardinian Brook Salamander, following the current IUCN assessment and Amphibian Species of the World; Sardinian Brook Newt, Sardinian Mountain Newt, Sardinian Newt and Flat-headed Salamander are supported aliases.
- Japanese public display name: サルデーニャ・イモリ. No established standard Japanese common name was confirmed; this is a transparent short rendering of the supported English and Italian names.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Endangered (EN) under B2ab(iii,iv), assessed 20 February 2023, record `T8371A223758127`.
- Public claims: an adult has a strongly flattened head and broad snout; it walks along calm stream bottoms; mating pairs overlap their tail bases in running water; females attach eggs beneath stones and in crevices.
- Discovery doorway: in a Sardinian mountain stream, one flat-headed newt turns its whole body and tail into a brace for mating in moving water.
