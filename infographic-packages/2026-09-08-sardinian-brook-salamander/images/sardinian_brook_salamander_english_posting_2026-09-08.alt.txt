Vertical 2:3 handmade field-notebook poster. One Sardinian Brook Salamander, Euproctus platycephalus, walks along the stony bottom of a cool Sardinian mountain stream. The adult has a flattened broad head, short lower jaw, small gold-ringed eyes, irregular olive-brown mottling, four short limbs and a laterally compressed tail. The common and scientific names sit at the top. Three numbered illustrated cards show the male gripping the female's trunk with his jaws, eggs beneath stones and in crevices, and the adult's flat head and bottom-walking posture. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2023: Endangered (EN).
