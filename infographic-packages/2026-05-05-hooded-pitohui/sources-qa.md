## Publication-safe notes

- No population number is used.
- No global threat or urgency claim is used.
- Conservation/status is kept as a quiet footer: IUCN Red List 2018: Least Concern (LC).
- Toxin language is phrased as "can contain" and "thought to come from diet" to avoid overstating individual toxicity or mechanism.
- *Choresine* beetles are described as a putative/likely source, not as the only proven source for every bird.
