## 1. Selection rationale

Today this package uses *Pitohui dichrous*, the Hooded Pitohui, called ズグロモリモズ in Japanese.

It was selected because it avoids the completed archive topics and shifts the recent lineage/habitat pattern. The most recent completed topics were a pelagic sea slug, a desert plant, a deep-sea crustacean, a desert rodent, wetland/subterranean mammals and amphibians, and an armadillo. This run moves to a New Guinea forest songbird with a clear natural-history hook: a black-and-chestnut bird whose skin and feathers can contain batrachotoxin-family compounds, likely acquired through diet.

The topic works for the series because it invites curiosity about food, chemistry, feathers, and rainforest ecology without needing population numbers, urgency slogans, or rescue framing.
