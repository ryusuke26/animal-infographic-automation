## Japanese

森の色の鳥が、森の食べものから毒をまとう。
Hooded Pitohui
*Pitohui dichrous*

ニューギニアの森林にすむ、黒い頭と赤茶色の体の鳴禽。果実や昆虫などを食べ、群れで動くこともあります。
羽と皮ふにはバトラコトキシン系の毒があり、食べもの由来と考えられています。
小さな甲虫の味を羽に残す、ちょっと不思議な暮らし。

IUCN Red List 2018: Least Concern (LC)

#世界の知らない生きもの

## English

A forest-colored bird wears chemistry from its food.
Hooded Pitohui
*Pitohui dichrous*

This New Guinea forest songbird has a black hood and warm chestnut body. It eats fruit and insects and may move with mixed foraging flocks.
Its skin and feathers can contain batrachotoxin-family compounds, probably obtained through diet.
A small beetle-flavored mystery, carried in feathers.

IUCN Red List 2018: Least Concern (LC)
