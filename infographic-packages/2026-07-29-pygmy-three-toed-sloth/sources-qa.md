## Locked public claims

1. Habitat: an island forest of red mangroves and dense tropical woodland.
2. Visible identity: the smallest sloth, with shaggy fur that can look green
   because of algae.
3. Behavior: it moves through branches, and swimming has been recorded.

## Visual identity guidance

- One small adult three-toed sloth, not a baby and not a two-toed sloth.
- Compact rounded body; small rounded head; buff-tan face with a dark eye mask;
  shaggy grey-brown coat with a restrained algae-green cast.
- Long forelimbs and hindlimbs ending in three long curved hook claws; all
  limbs visibly attached and anatomically plausible.
- Natural clinging or hanging posture on a red-mangrove branch above shallow
  coastal water, with mangrove prop roots and dense island forest.
- Keep the face, all four limbs, and diagnostic claws unobstructed.

## Negative constraints

- No two-clawed forelimbs, bear-like paws, primate hands, monkey tail, visible
  long tail, detached claws, duplicated limbs, extra sloths, or infant-only
  depiction.
- No generic mainland rainforest, fake map, zoo scene, rescue scene, human,
  slogan, population number, logo, or watermark.
- Do not claim mangroves are the only habitat; later work records animals in
  interior forest too.
