Title: Wood Frog
Scientific name: *Lithobates sylvaticus*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Frozen solid all winter
   Explanation: Under the leaf litter it freezes, and its heart and breathing stop until spring.
2. Heading: Sugar from the liver
   Explanation: As freezing begins, the liver releases stored sugar into the blood, helping keep the inside of its cells from freezing.
3. Heading: First to call in spring
   Explanation: Once thawed, it heads to woodland pools and starts calling with duck-like quacks, often before the ice has fully melted.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2020: Least Concern (LC)
