Vertical poster in a cut-paper collage style. Title and scientific name on a paper label at the top. The centre is a cutaway of a snowy forest floor: among leaves and soil, a brown frog crouches with legs folded, dusted with frost and ice crystals, showing a dark mask through the eye, a white upper-lip stripe, a ridge along the back and barred hind legs. Three numbered figures: 1, a round frame at upper right, the frog half-closing its eye under frosted leaves; 2, an oval at left, a blue frog silhouette whose liver sends yellow sugar dots through red vessels into round cells, with ice crystals only outside the cells; 3, lower right, a frog calling head-up in a spring pool with ice at its edge and an egg mass underwater. Each has a heading and explanation. Footer: IUCN Red List 2020: Least Concern (LC).
