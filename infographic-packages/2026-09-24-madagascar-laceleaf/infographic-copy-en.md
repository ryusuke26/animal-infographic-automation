# English Copy Lock

Copy format: cards-v2

Title: Madagascar Laceleaf
Scientific name: *Aponogeton madagascariensis*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Leaves turn themselves into lace
   Explanation: Cells between the veins die in a set pattern, opening holes outward.
2. Heading: First leaves stay whole
   Explanation: The first three or four leaves from the corm mature without perforations.
3. Heading: A thin net under water
   Explanation: Mature leaves keep a grid of lengthwise and crosswise veins.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC)
