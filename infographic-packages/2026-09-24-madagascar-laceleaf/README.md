## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Aponogeton madagascariensis* (Mirb.) H.Bruggen.
- Public names: Madagascar Laceleaf / レースソウ. The Japanese title is a documented horticultural name and is not claimed as a standardized national plant-list name.
- Status: IUCN record `T88495173A177456553`, Global Least Concern (LC), assessed 28 June 2016; amended assessment published in 2020.
- Public claims: adult leaf perforations form by developmentally regulated programmed cell death between longitudinal and transverse veins; the first three to four juvenile leaves do not perforate; mature submerged leaves retain a thin vein lattice.
- Discovery doorway: a living underwater leaf sculpts itself into lace as it grows.
