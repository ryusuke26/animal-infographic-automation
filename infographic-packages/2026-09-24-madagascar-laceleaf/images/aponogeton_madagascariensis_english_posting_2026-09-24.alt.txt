Vertical 2:3 crayon-and-oil-pastel natural-history poster. A single Madagascar Laceleaf, Aponogeton madagascariensis, grows from one corm in fresh water, its long green leaves bending with the current. Mature blades contain repeated openings bordered by lengthwise and crosswise veins. Three numbered illustrated cards show holes opening outward between veins, solid first leaves beside later perforated leaves, and a close study of the thin submerged vein lattice. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC).
