# Japanese Direct Poster Prompt — Red River Hog

Create one complete true vertical 2:3 Japanese educational infographic poster,
designed as a handmade dusk-forest naturalist's field-notebook page around the
Red River Hog's low brick-red silhouette and long pale ear tassels. The forest
path, hero, title, three observation cards, and footer must form one authored
composition, not three equal software panels.

## Subject and scene

Show exactly one identifiable adult Red River Hog, *Potamochoerus porcus*, in
natural three-quarter side view, walking through damp leaf litter in a West or
Central African forest just after sunset. Its head is slightly lowered as if
smelling for roots. Show a compact deep-chested pig body, brick-red to chestnut
coat, dark lower legs and muzzle, narrow white lines around the eyes and along
the cheeks, a white mane line down the back, long pointed ears with conspicuous
pale cream tassels, cloven hooves, and a short tufted tail.

Do not make a warthog, bushpig, domestic pig, wild boar, peccary, or babirusa.
No huge tusks, facial warts, grey hairless body, curled domestic-pig tail,
trunk, antlers, extra legs, detached hooves, duplicated ears, extra hogs,
humans, hunting scene, fake map, logo, signature, or watermark.

## Layout and visual hierarchy

Use warm fibrous paper, graphite, colored pencil, muted gouache, and a palette
of humid forest green, leaf-brown, brick red, charcoal, and pale cream. Let a
curved trail of disturbed leaves lead the eye from the title toward the
snout. Place exactly three unequal irregular numbered observation cards around
the hero without covering its ears, face, legs, or dorsal mane:

1. a small canopy-edge and deep-thicket habitat cutaway;
2. a close study of one pointed tasselled ear, white face lines, and red coat;
3. a snout turning leaf litter beside roots and fallen fruit, with a few
   subtle group tracks rather than extra animals.

Each card must have one clear number, one species-specific spot illustration,
and its full explanatory copy. No three equal boxes. Keep the hero dominant
and unobstructed. Integrate the quiet footer at the bottom. Make every line
legible on a phone.

## Text accuracy

Render only the following Japanese text exactly. Do not translate,
paraphrase, shorten, respell, repeat, or add words. Line wrapping is allowed
inside an item; preserve every character and order. Each observation appears
exactly once inside its numbered card.

Text, verbatim:
"アカカワイノシシ"
"Potamochoerus porcus"
"森と深いやぶ 西・中央アフリカに暮らす"
"赤い毛、白い線 耳先の長い房毛が揺れる"
"夕暮れから森を探る 群れで根や落ちた実を食べる"
"IUCN Red List 2016：低懸念（LC）"

Use clear correctly formed Japanese glyphs. Exactly three numbered illustrated
cards; no extra labels, captions, badges, decorative text, logos, or
watermarks.
