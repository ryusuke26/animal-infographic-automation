## Evidence Lock

- Accepted scientific name: *Potamochoerus porcus* (Linnaeus, 1758)
- English common name: Red River Hog
- Japanese common name: アカカワイノシシ
- Broad native region: Africa
- Lineage: pig family, Suidae
- Native range: West and Central Africa
- Habitat: moist lowland forest, forest edges, and deep thickets
- Global IUCN category: Least Concern (LC)
- Scope: Global
- Assessment/publication year: 2016
- Assessment identifier: `e.T41771A100469961`
- Official assessment DOI:
  https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2016-1.RLTS.T41771A44141118.en
- IUCN check: the formal Red List assessment remains the 2016 Global LC
  assessment. A February 2026 IUCN SSC meeting suggested retaining LC with a
  decreasing trend, but that recommendation was still awaiting submission and
  publication; it is not used as the poster category or year.

## Locked Public Claims

1. Habitat: forest, forest edge, and deep thickets in West and Central Africa.
2. Visible identity: red-brown coat, white face and dorsal lines, and long pale
   tassels on the pointed ears.
3. Behavior: groups become active mainly after dusk and forage for roots,
   rhizomes, and fallen fruit.

Public copy omits population numbers, legal-status claims, threat ranking,
blame, rescue framing, and the not-yet-published 2026 reassessment proposal.

## Visual Identity Guidance

- Show exactly one adult Red River Hog in natural three-quarter side view,
  walking through humid forest leaf litter after sunset with its head slightly
  lowered as if smelling for food.
- Compact deep-chested pig body; brick-red to chestnut coat; darker lower legs
  and muzzle; narrow white markings around the eyes and along the cheeks;
  white mane line along the back.
- Long pointed ears ending in conspicuous pale cream tassels.
- Short tail with a tuft, cloven hooves, and a normal pig snout.
- Males may have subtle cheek bumps, but avoid large warthog-like tusks or
  facial warts.

## Negative Constraints

- Not a warthog, bushpig, domestic pig, wild boar, peccary, or babirusa.
- No huge tusks, facial warts, grey hairless body, curled domestic-pig tail,
  trunk, antlers, extra legs, detached hooves, duplicated ears, extra hogs,
  fake map, humans, bait, hunting scene, logo, signature, or watermark.
