## Topic Lock

- English common name: Red River Hog
- Japanese common name: アカカワイノシシ
- Scientific name: *Potamochoerus porcus*
- Broad native region: Africa
- Lineage: pig family, Suidae
- Habitat: forest, forest edge, and deep thickets across West and Central
  Africa
- Curiosity hook: pale ear tassels move above the leaf litter before the
  animal's brick-red body emerges at dusk
