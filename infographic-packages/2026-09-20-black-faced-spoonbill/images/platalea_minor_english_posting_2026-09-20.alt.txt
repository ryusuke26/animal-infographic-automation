Vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel field-notebook poster. One white Black-faced Spoonbill, Platalea minor, feeds in a shallow East Asian winter tidal flat, sweeping its long black spoon-shaped bill sideways. Bare black skin links its eye to the bill, and two long black legs stand in rippled water. Three numbered illustrated cards explain tactile searching for fish and shrimp, the black face and rounded bill tip, and coastal migration across the Yellow Sea. The footer reads IUCN Red List 2025: Vulnerable (VU).
