## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Platalea minor* Temminck & Schlegel, 1849.
- Public names: Black-faced Spoonbill / クロツラヘラサギ.
- Status: Global Vulnerable (VU) under IUCN criterion C2a(ii), assessed 18 June 2025.
- Public claims: tactile side-to-side feeding for fish and shrimp, continuous black face-to-spoon-bill identity, and coastal migration between Yellow Sea breeding areas and East Asian winter wetlands.
- Discovery doorway: a black spoon feeling through shallow water for prey the bird cannot see.
