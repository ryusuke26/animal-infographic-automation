Title: Banded Archerfish
Scientific name: *Toxotes jaculatrix*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Water jets bring insects down
   Explanation: It spits water at insects resting above the surface.
2. Heading: A jump for closer prey
   Explanation: It can leap out of the water to reach prey on low branches.
3. Heading: Where river meets sea
   Explanation: It hunts near the surface in brackish mangrove waters.
