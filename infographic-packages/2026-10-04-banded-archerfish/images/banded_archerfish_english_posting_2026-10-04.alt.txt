A vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel observation poster. One large, silver, deep-bodied Banded Archerfish swims beneath mangrove water, with dark marks descending from its upper flank, a compact upturned mouth, a large round eye, and dorsal and anal fins toward the rear. Three numbered illustrated cards explain water jets that knock down insects above the surface, jumps toward prey on low branches, and life in brackish mangrove waters. The quiet footer states IUCN Red List 2011: Least Concern (LC).
