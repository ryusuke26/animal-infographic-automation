The complete fish has a very thin, laterally compressed but deep silver body, olive-gray back, reddish-purple lower belly, small head without barbels, complete lateral line, large dorsal and anal fins, paired fins, and a modest forked tail. The English title and scientific name sit at the top. Three unequal numbered illustrated cards show Japanese river backwaters and channels, the autumn male color, and one complete small female beside a freshwater mussel containing eggs, with young emerging around May.
