Vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel observation poster. A female okapi stands in shaded eastern DRC rainforest, with a deep brown torso, pale-and-dark stripes across the rump and legs, large upright ears and four coherent hoofed limbs. Its dark tongue reaches leafy foliage. Three numbered illustrated cards explain its giraffe-family identity despite zebra-like stripes, leaf gathering with the tongue, and independently rotating ears. A quiet footer gives the IUCN global Endangered assessment from 2015.
