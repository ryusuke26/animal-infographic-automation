Title: Okapi
Scientific name: *Okapia johnstoni*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Stripes, but a giraffe relative
   Explanation: Its striped hindquarters recall a zebra, yet the okapi belongs to the giraffe family.
2. Heading: A tongue that gathers leaves
   Explanation: Its long dark tongue draws leaves from branches toward its mouth.
3. Heading: Ears that turn separately
   Explanation: Each large ear can rotate on its own to listen through the forest.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2015: Endangered (EN)
