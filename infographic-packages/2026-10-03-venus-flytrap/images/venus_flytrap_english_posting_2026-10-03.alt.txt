A vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel field-notebook poster. One Venus flytrap rosette grows in damp grass, with flat green leaf stalks ending in paired reddish trap lobes edged with fine bristles. Three numbered illustrated notes explain rapid closure after two quick trigger-hair touches, nutrients from prey alongside energy from photosynthesis, and white flowers on a long stalk above the low traps. The footer gives the 2000 IUCN assessment as Vulnerable (VU).
