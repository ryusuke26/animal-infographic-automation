Title: Gharial
Scientific name: *Gavialis gangeticus*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: A pot on the male's nose
   Explanation: Only mature males grow this knob, the ghara; it covers the nostrils, makes calls resonate and signals to females.
2. Heading: Long, thin jaws for fish
   Explanation: More than 100 sharp teeth interlock along the slender jaws, snapping fast at fish in the water.
3. Heading: A belly-slide on land
   Explanation: Its legs cannot lift its body high off the ground, so on land it pushes itself forward on its belly.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2017: Critically Endangered (CR)
