## Story reply
```text
In March to April, the dry season, a female digs into a steep sandbank and lays about 40 eggs, the largest of any crocodilian. Only the mother guards nest and young. On the Chambal, many females nest on the same sandbars every year, so nest counts track the breeding females.
```
