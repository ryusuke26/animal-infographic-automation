## English Thread

1. Mexican Burrowing Toad spends much of the dry season underground.
2. It has a round dark body, tiny pointed head and eyes, short limbs, and orange spots.
3. Inner spades on its hind feet help it dig backward into the soil.
4. Males call in temporary pools after heavy rain. IUCN Red List 2020: Least Concern (LC).
