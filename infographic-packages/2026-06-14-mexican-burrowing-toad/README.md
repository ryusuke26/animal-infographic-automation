## Rationale

Each broad region appeared once in the most recent 8 completed packages, and
the previous run was Africa. Central America/Caribbean was selected without a
consecutive repeat. This amphibian's dry-season underground life, backward
digging, and rain-filled temporary-pool calling differ from recent topics.

The completed Purple Frog legacy package was found during preflight and
rejected as a duplicate before this topic was selected.

## Locked Public Claims

1. *Rhinophrynus dorsalis* lives in Mesoamerican lowland savannas and
   seasonally dry forests, from extreme southern Texas and Mexico to
   northwestern Costa Rica.
2. It spends much of the dry season underground and digs backward with paired
   enlarged spade-like structures on the inner metatarsal area of its hind
   feet.
3. Heavy rains bring it above ground, and males call in temporary pools during
   the breeding season.
