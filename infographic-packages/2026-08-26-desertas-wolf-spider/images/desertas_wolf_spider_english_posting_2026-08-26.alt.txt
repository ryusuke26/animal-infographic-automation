Vertical 2:3 handmade volcanic-island field-notebook poster. In a dry volcanic valley on Deserta Grande in the Madeira archipelago, one stocky grey Desertas Wolf Spider, Hogna ingens, emerges from a burrow beneath a dark rock with all eight black-and-white-banded walking legs visible. The title and scientific name are integrated at the top. Three unequal numbered illustrated cards show its under-rock habitat, grey body and black-and-white legs, and predation on a small lizard. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2014: Critically Endangered (CR).
