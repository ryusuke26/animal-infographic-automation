## Visual Identity Guidance

- Show a small brown, gray, or rust-colored gecko, not a bright fantasy lizard.
- Important cues: triangular head, large eyes with vertical pupils, horn-like scales above the eyes, flattened leaf-like tail with ragged/broken edges, mottled dead-leaf pattern.
- Habitat cues: low Madagascar rainforest understory, mossy branch, leaf litter, soft night or dusk atmosphere.
- Avoid: fake range map, exaggerated devil imagery, red demon horns, snake-like body, generic cute gecko, or tropical flower background.
