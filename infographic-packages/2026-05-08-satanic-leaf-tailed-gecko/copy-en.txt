Satanic Leaf-Tailed Gecko
Uroplatus phantasticus

In Madagascar's eastern rainforests,
this small gecko can rest like a broken brown leaf.

At night, it moves through low branches and shrubs,
searching for small invertebrates.

A ragged leaf-shaped tail and tiny horn-like eye scales
help its outline disappear.

A quiet life spent pretending to be forest leaf litter.

IUCN Red List 2020: Least Concern (LC)
