Title: Lesser Flamingo
Scientific name: *Phoeniconaias minor*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: An upside-down sieve
   Explanation: With its head upside down, it filters tiny algae and other food near the surface.
2. Heading: Between lakes after dark
   Explanation: It often moves to other wetlands at night when local conditions change.
3. Heading: One egg on a mud tower
   Explanation: It builds a raised mud nest and usually lays a single egg.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2018: Near Threatened (NT)
