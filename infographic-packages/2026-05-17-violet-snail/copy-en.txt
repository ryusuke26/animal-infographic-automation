Violet Snail
Janthina janthina

A small ocean-surface snail that drifts while hanging from its own raft of bubbles.
It binds air bubbles with mucus from its foot, using the raft as floating support at the sea surface.

Observation notes
1. Thin violet shell
2. Hangs just below the sea surface
3. Feeds on floating cnidarians such as bluebottles and by-the-wind sailors

Quiet status note
IUCN Red List via SeaLifeBase 2025-1: Not Evaluated (NE)
No population number is used; the focus is its small, upside-down drifting life at the ocean surface.
