A violet shell hangs from a handmade raft of bubbles at the sea surface.

Janthina janthina

This small pelagic snail binds air bubbles with mucus from its foot and hangs from that raft as it drifts near the ocean surface.  
Its thin purple shell and clear bubble raft are the key clues.  
A slightly strange life carried by wind, waves, and floating prey.
