## Image Identity Guidance

- Show a coiled violet-purple sea-snail shell, not a slug-like nudibranch.
- Include the transparent mucus bubble raft as a central identity cue, with the snail hanging from it rather than riding above it.
- Show ocean-surface context; avoid seabed, coral reef, or tidepool-only scenes.
- A small floating cnidarian can appear as food context, but do not turn the poster into a bluebottle/Portuguese man-of-war poster.
- Avoid fake range maps, danger icons, rescue/blame imagery, and population counters.

## Source Notes

- GBIF line data shows *Janthina janthina* as species, family Epitoniidae, and includes アサガオガイ among common names.
- Australian Museum is the main public natural-history source for size, pelagic habitat, broad distribution, bubble raft, diet, and life-history notes.
- SeaLifeBase was used cautiously for the displayed IUCN Red List status/version, max length, and broad distribution.
- Japanese literature was used for context rather than overloading the poster with regional drift details.
- No source-supported population number was found or used.
