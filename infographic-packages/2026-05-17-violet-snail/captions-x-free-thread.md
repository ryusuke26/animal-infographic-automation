## English 140-Character Thread

1. A violet shell hangs from a raft of bubbles. Violet Snail, Janthina janthina. #NaturalHistory #Molluscs

2. This small pelagic snail drifts at the ocean surface while hanging from air bubbles bound with mucus from its foot.

3. It feeds on floating cnidarians such as bluebottles and by-the-wind sailors. The violet shell and bubble raft are key clues.

4. IUCN Red List via SeaLifeBase 2025-1: Not Evaluated. No population number is used.
