## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Rhampholeon acuminatus* Mariaux & Tilbury, 2006; the subgeneric combination *Rhampholeon (Rhinodigitum) acuminatus* is retained for duplicate screening. `Rampholeon acuminatus` is a documented later misspelling, not an accepted name.
- English public name: Nguru Spiny Pygmy Chameleon.
- Japanese public name: ヌグルコノハカメレオン, a published Japanese rendering rather than a claimed standardized national-list name.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Critically Endangered (CR) under B1ab(iii,v), assessed 21 August 2013, record `T172524A1344202`; the assessment is marked Needs updating.
- Public claims: sharp scales trace the head and dorsal crest; mature males carry a larger downward-pointing rostral extension; the species is crepuscular and uses leaf litter and low vegetation in Nguru montane forest.
- Discovery doorway: a tiny leaf-like chameleon emerges in the half-light, its nose and back outlined by points.
