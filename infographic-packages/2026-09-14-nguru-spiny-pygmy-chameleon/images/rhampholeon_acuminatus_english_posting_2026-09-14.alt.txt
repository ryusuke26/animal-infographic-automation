Vertical 2:3 handmade field-notebook poster. In the damp montane forest of Tanzania's Nguru Mountains, one tiny male Nguru Spiny Pygmy Chameleon, Rhampholeon acuminatus, walks along a mossy twig. Its mottled green-brown leaf-like body, broad downward nose process, small pointed head and back scales, grasping feet and nearly straight tapered tail are visible. The common and scientific names sit at the top. Three numbered illustrated cards show the pointed body outline, the larger male rostral extension and dawn-and-dusk activity in the low forest layer. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2013: Critically Endangered (CR).
