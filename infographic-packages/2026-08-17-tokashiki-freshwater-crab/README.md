## Evidence route

The official IUCN assessment PDF and matching current-page capture identify *Geothelphusa levicervix* as globally Endangered (EN) under B1ab(iii)+2ab(iii), assessed and published in 2008 as `T134902A4033497`. The numeric taxon-page route is `134902/4033497`; `134902` alone is not the full assessment identifier. The public footer uses the confirmed 2008 global assessment year only. Japanese national and prefectural categories remain source context and are not mixed into public status wording. The supplied PDF and capture are preserved under [`evidence/`](evidence/).

Okinawa Prefecture supports the species' accepted Japanese name, Tokashiki-only range, large smooth carapace (up to 63 mm), enlarged claw in large males, shaded wet forest-stream habitat, and large-egg direct development. The species is a fully landlocked freshwater crab; public copy does not attach population figures, legal status, or a ranked threat list.
