Vertical 2:3 handmade Ryukyu forest-stream field-notebook poster. One adult male Tokashiki Freshwater Crab, Geothelphusa levicervix, walks on a wet stone beside a narrow, slow stream under dark evergreen forest on Tokashiki Island. Its broad smooth domed carapace, two eyestalks, unequal claws, and four pairs of walking legs are visible. The English title and scientific name sit at the top. Three unequal numbered illustrated cards show the narrow stream, a complete large male with one outsized claw, and a complete egg-carrying female with one small juvenile. The footer reads IUCN Red List 2008: Endangered (EN).
