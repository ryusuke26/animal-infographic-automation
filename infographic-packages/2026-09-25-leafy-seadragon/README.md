## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Phycodurus eques* (Günther, 1865).
- Public names: Leafy Seadragon / リーフィーシードラゴン.
- Status: IUCN record `T17096A67622420`, Global Least Concern (LC), assessed 12 May 2016 and published in 2017.
- Public claims: the branching leaf-like structures are skin flaps rather than propulsion fins; small transparent pectoral and dorsal fins move the fish; females transfer eggs to the exposed underside of the male's tail, where he broods them.
- Discovery doorway: the most conspicuous leaf-like parts are not the parts that swim.
