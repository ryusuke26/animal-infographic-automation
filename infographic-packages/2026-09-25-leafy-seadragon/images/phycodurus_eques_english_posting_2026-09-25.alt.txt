Vertical 2:3 crayon-and-oil-pastel natural-history poster. One Leafy Seadragon, Phycodurus eques, swims side-on among kelp in southern Australian water. Its long yellow-brown body has pale stripes, a tubular snout, elaborate branching leaf-like skin flaps, and small clear pectoral and dorsal fins. Three numbered illustrated cards show that the leafy flaps are not fins, the clear fins used for swimming, and a male brooding eggs beneath his tail. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC).
