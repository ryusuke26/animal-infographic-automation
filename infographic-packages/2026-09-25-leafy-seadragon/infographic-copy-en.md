Title: Leafy Seadragon
Scientific name: *Phycodurus eques*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: The leaves are not fins
   Explanation: Leaf-like skin flaps break up its outline instead of propelling it.
2. Heading: Clear fins do the swimming
   Explanation: Small pectoral and dorsal fins ripple to move the fish forward.
3. Heading: Males carry the eggs
   Explanation: A female places eggs under the male's tail, where he broods them.
