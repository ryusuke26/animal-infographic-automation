# Storybook Image Prompts: Japanese

Shared lock for every page:

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: Japanese mini picture-book page, portrait vertical 2:3, 1024x1536 preferred
Style: wax crayon, oil pastel, colored pencil on warm textured paper; childlike natural-history observation with polished adult picture-book direction.
Snow-kun: one scarlet fleshy Sarcodes sanguinea shoot, no green leaves, overlapping red scale bracts, a tiny friendly face in the upper bracts. On the final page, add many small nodding red-pink bell flowers with pale cream centers.
Kinshi-chan: fine branching luminous white fungal hyphae with one small friendly face; never a capped mushroom.
Layout: illustration in upper 72 percent; exact Japanese story text in one warm-cream box in lower 28 percent; page number in a corner.
Avoid: mushroom, bamboo, ordinary leafy plant, literal food, rescue slogans, logos, watermark.
```
