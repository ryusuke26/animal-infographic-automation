Vertical watercolor poster of a Common Hoopoe on a sunny short-grass verge. It has a cinnamon-pink body, a peach fan crest with black tips, a long downcurved black bill, black-and-white barred wings and a white-banded tail. Title and Upupa epops appear above. Card 1 shows brownish eggs in a tree cavity and a linked schematic magnification of bacteria in oil; its sentence explains that an incubating female's secretion contains bacteria producing antimicrobial compounds. Card 2 shows a small complete bird probing soil beside grass, with a larva below. Card 3 compares folded and spread crests. Every numbered card includes a heading and explanation. The footer gives the global IUCN 2020 assessment: Least Concern (LC).
