Title: Common Hoopoe
Scientific name: *Upupa epops*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Eggs coated with bacterial oil
   Explanation: An incubating female's secretion holds bacteria that make antimicrobial compounds.
2. Heading: A long bill probes the ground
   Explanation: In short vegetation, it probes soil for insects, larvae and other prey.
3. Heading: A crest that opens and folds
   Explanation: Usually folded, the crest spreads like a fan when the bird is excited.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2020: Least Concern (LC)
