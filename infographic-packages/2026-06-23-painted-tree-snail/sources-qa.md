## Exact visual identity guidance

Show one large living land snail climbing naturally on a lichen-covered tree trunk or sturdy shrub branch in humid eastern Cuba. The shell is rounded and globose with a low conical spire, not tall or elongated. Its shell carries vivid but natural combinations of cream or white, yellow, red-brown, dark brown, and black spiral bands or fine lines. The soft body is gray-brown with two long upper tentacles bearing eyes and two shorter lower tentacles. The foot stays in full contact with bark.

Keep the color pattern coherent on one shell. Do not show a collage of multiple shell morphs, a second specimen, a cutaway, or a shell-only collection. Avoid an elongated *Liguus*-like shell, a giant African land snail, a cone snail, a marine setting, eyestalks emerging from the shell, extra tentacles, detached body parts, fake maps, population graphics, collection jars, trade imagery, or threat symbols.
