Giant Gippsland Earthworm
Megascolides australis

Under moist clay, a long body moves slowly through permanent tunnels.
This giant earthworm is known from Gippsland, Victoria, Australia.
The front third is deep purple, fading into a pink-grey body.
When it moves in wet burrows, people may hear a gurgling sound underground.

IUCN Red List 2014: Endangered (EN)
Australia EPBC Act: Vulnerable
Victoria FFG Threatened List 2025: Endangered
