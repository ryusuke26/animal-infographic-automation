## Claim QA

- Names: Giant Gippsland Earthworm; *Megascolides australis* McCoy, 1878.
- Taxonomy: Animalia; Annelida; Clitellata/Oligochaeta treatment varies by source; family Megascolecidae.
- Range: south and west Gippsland, Victoria, Australia; public copy avoids a map.
- Habitat: moist clay soils, creek banks, soaks, and wet slopes above flood level.
- Distinctive traits: very large size, deep-purple front third, pink-grey body, mostly subterranean life, audible gurgling in wet burrows.
- Conservation/status: IUCN Red List 2014 Endangered (EN) is cross-checked through SWIFFT/IUCN-derived references; Australia EPBC Act Vulnerable; Victoria FFG Threatened List 2025 Endangered.
- Threats: package avoids a threat-centered message; only source notes mention hydrology and disturbance context.
- Population: no population number used.
- Visual identity: must read as a giant earthworm in clay, not as a snake, eel, caterpillar, or cartoon mascot.
