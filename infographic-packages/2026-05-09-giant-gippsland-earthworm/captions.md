## English Post

A hidden riverbank giant, known more by sound than sight.  
Giant Gippsland Earthworm  
Megascolides australis

This huge earthworm lives in moist clay soils in Gippsland, Victoria, Australia. It stays mostly underground in long burrows.  
Its front third is deep purple, fading into a pink-grey body.  
When it moves, wet tunnels can make a gurgling sound.

IUCN Red List 2014: Endangered (EN)
