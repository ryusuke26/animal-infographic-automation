## Visual Identity Guidance

- Show one adult male Himalayan Monal in a natural standing or walking pose.
- Keep the compact pheasant body, short strong legs, stout pale bill, dark
  underparts, metallic green crest and head, coppery nape, blue-green wings and
  back, and broad orange-chestnut tail.
- Place it in Himalayan alpine or subalpine shrubland near the treeline, with
  rhododendron, rocky ground, grass, and distant snow-dusted slopes.
- Keep the whole crest, feet, and tail visible.
- Do not make a peacock train, turkey fan, long dangling ornamental tail,
  generic rainbow pheasant, or brown female.
- Do not add extra birds, duplicated limbs, invented crests, maps, lettering,
  numbers, logos, or watermarks.
