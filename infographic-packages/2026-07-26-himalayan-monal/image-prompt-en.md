# English Direct Poster Prompt — Himalayan Monal

Create one complete vertical 2:3 English educational infographic poster,
designed as a hand-painted naturalist field-notebook page. The result must look
like one authored composition, not a photograph with generic UI panels added.
Use the accepted Japanese poster as the visual-language reference: preserve its
hero bird, habitat, hierarchy, paper texture, spot-illustration vocabulary, and
card positions while reflowing the English text naturally.

## Subject and scene

Show exactly one identifiable adult male Himalayan Monal, *Lophophorus
impejanus*, walking in a Himalayan alpine meadow near the treeline after snow
melt. Use a compact pheasant body, short strong legs, stout pale bill, a
metallic green crest and head, coppery nape, blue-green wings and back, dark
underparts, and a broad orange-chestnut tail. Keep the complete crest, head,
both feet, and tail visible. Surround it with low rhododendron, rock, grass,
small melting snow patches, and distant snow-dusted Himalayan ridges.

Do not make a peacock train, turkey fan, long ornamental tail, generic rainbow
pheasant, or brown female. No extra birds, duplicated limbs, invented crests,
maps, logos, signatures, or watermarks.

## Layout and visual hierarchy

Use warm off-white fibrous paper, fine graphite contour drawing, muted
watercolor, and restrained metallic jewel colors on the bird. Keep the bird as
the dominant hero. Place the title at the top with enough room above the crest.
Integrate exactly three irregular, hand-drawn numbered observation cards around
the bird without covering its silhouette:

1. a small rhododendron-and-mountain spot drawing for habitat;
2. a small iridescent feather spot drawing for visible identity;
3. a small two-elevation mountain-slope spot drawing with a simple seasonal
   path for movement.

Each card must have one clearly visible number, one species-specific spot
illustration, and its complete observation sentence. Use the same card order
as the text block below. Put the conservation footer quietly along the bottom
edge. Keep all text fully inside the canvas and highly legible at phone size.

## Text accuracy

Render the following English text exactly. Do not translate, paraphrase,
shorten, respell, or add other words. Line wrapping is allowed only within an
item; preserve every character and the listed order.

Text, verbatim:
"Himalayan Monal"
"Lophophorus impejanus"
"Lives in Himalayan forests and alpine meadows"
"Males shimmer with metallic rainbow plumage"
"Moves up and down the mountains with the seasons"
"IUCN Red List 2024: Least Concern (LC)"

There must be exactly three numbered observation cards and no other labels,
captions, badges, or decorative lettering.
