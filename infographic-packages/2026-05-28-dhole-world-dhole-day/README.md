## Image Identity Guidance

Show a medium-sized Asian wild canid with a rusty red-orange coat, pale throat and underside, rounded ears, slim legs, a short broad muzzle, and a dark bushy tail tip. A small group in forest habitat is appropriate. Avoid domestic dogs, foxes, wolves, coyotes, African painted dog spots, red pandas, attack scenes, blood, fake maps, or population graphics.
