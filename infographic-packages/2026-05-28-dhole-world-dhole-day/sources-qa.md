# Sources and QA

## Source Notes

- Dhole Conservation Fund identifies World Dhole Day as May 28 and describes the dhole as an Asian wild canid with strong social group life and distinctive vocal communication.
- IUCN Red List treats *Cuon alpinus* as Endangered (EN), assessed in 2015.
- Animal Diversity Web supports the scientific name, canid identity, range/habitat context, social pack life, and whistle-like communication.

## Publication-Safe Decisions

- Use `ドール` and `Dhole` as the public names.
- Use `World Dhole Day / 5月28日`.
- Use `IUCN Red List 2015: Endangered (EN)` as the status footer.
- Avoid population numbers and detailed threat claims in the public-facing copy.
- Keep the distinction clear: this is the animal dhole, not a doll/toy.
