An armored fish beneath the swamp surface.
Cuban Gar
Atractosteus tristoechus

In shallow, plant-filled waters of western Cuba and Isla de la Juventud, it often waits quietly at mid-water.
A broad snout, hard rhomboid scales, and the ability to take in air make this an unusual armored profile.

IUCN Red List 2021: Critically Endangered (CR)
