# Short English thread draft

1. An armored fish beneath the swamp surface: the Cuban Gar is endemic to western Cuba and Isla de la Juventud.
2. Its broad, relatively short snout and hard rhomboid ganoid scales create a distinctive profile.
3. A vascularized swim bladder lets it take in atmospheric oxygen when dissolved oxygen is low.
4. IUCN Red List 2021: Critically Endangered (CR). Full source context is in the posting set reply.
