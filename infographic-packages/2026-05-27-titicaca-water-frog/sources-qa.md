## Image QA Checklist

- Depict a broad-bodied underwater frog, not an axolotl, salamander, fish, ordinary smooth-skinned frog, or land toad.
- Keep visible loose folds along the flanks and hind limbs; wrinkles should remain biological rather than grotesque.
- Show a cold freshwater lake-bottom setting with stones and sparse aquatic plants, not an ocean reef or fake range map.
- Use olive-brown, grey-brown or muted mottled colouration with small eyes and webbed feet.
- Avoid population counts, dying animals, collecting scenes, slogans, logos, or a dramatic extinction mood.
- Generated lettering may be imperfect; text-safe assets carry authoritative typography.
