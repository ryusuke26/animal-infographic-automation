## Image Identity Guidance

Show one large Titicaca Water Frog underwater in a cold, clear high-altitude lake scene: a broad, flattened olive-brown to grey-brown frog with very loose rippled folds along the flanks and hind limbs, small eyes, broad head, webbed hind feet, and a bottom-resting aquatic pose among rounded stones and sparse submerged plants. The skin folds must look like natural wrinkled tissue used for gas exchange, not fur, gills, fins, or a comic exaggeration. Avoid an axolotl, toad on land, sea frog, generic smooth green frog, fake map, human hand, threat imagery, or population graphic.

## Status Note

The 2020 IUCN update is important here: IUCN's official 2020-2 statistics table lists *Telmatobius culeus* as moving from `CR` to `EN`, while some older conservation pages still preserve the prior `Critically Endangered` wording. The public footer therefore uses only `IUCN Red List 2020: Endangered (EN)`. Japanese legal-listing and CITES context are recorded as source support for the Japanese name, not blended into the global IUCN footer.
