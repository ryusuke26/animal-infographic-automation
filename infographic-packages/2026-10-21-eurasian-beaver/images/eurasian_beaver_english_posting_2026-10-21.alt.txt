A vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel poster titled Eurasian Beaver, scientific name Castor fiber. A brown beaver holds a leafy twig with its forepaws beside a willow-fringed river. It has small rounded ears, a blunt muzzle, whiskers and a flat paddle tail extending behind it. Three numbered illustrated notes show: 1, a naturally deep river or lake without a dam, explaining that beavers may live there without building dams; 2, a contextual head study with orange incisors, explaining the strengthening role of iron in their enamel; 3, a qualitative bank-burrow section with an underwater entrance rising to an air-filled chamber. Footer: IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC).
