Title: Eurasian Beaver
Scientific name: *Castor fiber*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Life without a dam
   Explanation: In naturally deep rivers and lakes, beavers may live without building dams.
2. Heading: Orange teeth, reinforced by iron
   Explanation: Iron in the incisor enamel helps strengthen teeth for gnawing wood.
3. Heading: Underwater door, dry room
   Explanation: A bank burrow rises from an underwater entrance to an air-filled chamber.
