## Story reply
```text
In southern Australian kelp beds, sparse leaf-like appendages disrupt a narrow body's outline. Tiny fins move it quietly, and a long tubular mouth draws in small crustaceans. In breeding season, eggs passed from a female are arranged beneath the male's tail.
```
