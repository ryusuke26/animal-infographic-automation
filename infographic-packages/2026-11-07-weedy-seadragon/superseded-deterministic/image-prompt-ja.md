# Selected Japanese poster specification

Use case: scientific educational infographic, exact vertical 2:3.
Subject: an adult Weedy Seadragon (*Phyllopteryx taeniolatus*) in southern Australian kelp and seagrass. Preserve the long narrow bony body, horse-like head with a long tubular snout, a small dorsal fin on the back, small pectoral fins behind the head, curled prehensile tail, and irregular leaf-like appendages. A male brood patch is only shown as a small attached egg-row study under the tail, not on every hero animal. It is not a Leafy Seadragon: appendages are sparse and irregular, not a dense leafy cloak.
Style/medium: deep ultramarine-to-teal cyanotype and coloured-pencil marine field plate; fine copper contour lines, pale kelp ribbons, dark water with visible grain. The background stays dense enough to feel underwater but cards retain high contrast.
Composition: one profile hero swimming through a kelp opening; three numbered illustrated cards show egg-bearing tail, outline-breaking appendages, and tubular-mouth feeding. Japanese typography is typeset after illustration.
Text, verbatim:
"ウィーディー・シードラゴン"
"Phyllopteryx taeniolatus"
"オスの尾に、卵が並ぶ"
"メスが渡した卵を、オスが尾の下側の育児部で発生まで支える。"
"海藻のような飾り"
"体の葉のような突起は推進器ではなく、海藻帯で輪郭を崩して見せる。"
"ゆっくり、狙いを定める"
"小さな胸びれと背びれで静かに進み、管状の口で小さな甲殻類を吸い込む。"
