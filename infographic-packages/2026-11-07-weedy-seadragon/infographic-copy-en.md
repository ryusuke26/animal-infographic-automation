Title: Weedy Seadragon
Scientific name: *Phyllopteryx taeniolatus*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Eggs on his tail
   Explanation: The male supports eggs received from a female on a brood area under his tail until development is complete.
2. Heading: Seaweed-like frills
   Explanation: Its leaf-like appendages are not propellers; they break up the outline among seaweed.
3. Heading: A slow, precise hunter
   Explanation: Small pectoral and dorsal fins move it quietly as its tubular mouth sucks in tiny crustaceans.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2024: Vulnerable (VU)
