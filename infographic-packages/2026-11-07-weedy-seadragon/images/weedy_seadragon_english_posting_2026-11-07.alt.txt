Vertical watercolour infographic of sunlit blue water. Among kelp and seagrass, a reddish-orange Weedy Seadragon with fine yellow spots and bluish bars swims in profile: horse-like head, long tubular snout, narrow bony body, a few oval skin flaps, small dorsal and pectoral fins and a curled tail. Three numbered illustrated cards show eggs beneath a male's tail, the seadragon blending into seaweed, and its tubular mouth drawing in a tiny shrimp-like crustacean. Each card has a heading and explanation. Footer: IUCN Red List 2025: Vulnerable (VU).
