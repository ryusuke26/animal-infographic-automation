# Captions: Long-eared Jerboa

## Japanese X Post

砂漠の夜を、大きな耳でそっとたどる。

Long-eared Jerboa  
*Euchoreutes naso*

モンゴルから中国北西部の砂漠・半砂漠にすむ、小さなトビネズミのなかま。昼は身をひそめ、夜になると低い茂みのあいだで昆虫などをさがします。

体より目立つ大きな耳、長い後ろ足、黒白の房がある尾が目印。

砂の上を跳ねながら夜をきく、ちょっと不思議な暮らし。

IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC)

## English X Post

Tiny ears would not do for this desert night.

Long-eared Jerboa  
*Euchoreutes naso*

This small jerboa lives in desert and semi-desert habitats of Mongolia and northwestern China. After dark, it moves through low shrubs and searches for insects.

Its huge ears, long hind legs, and black-and-white tail tuft make it hard to mistake.

A little night-hopping life among desert shrubs.

IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC)
