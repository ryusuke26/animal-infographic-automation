# Short Captions: Long-eared Jerboa

## Japanese X Post

砂漠の夜を、大きな耳でたどるトビネズミ。

Long-eared Jerboa  
*Euchoreutes naso*

モンゴルから中国北西部の砂漠・半砂漠に暮らし、  
夜になると低い茂みのあいだで昆虫などをさがします。  
体より目立つ大きな耳と、黒白の房がある長い尾。  
砂の上を跳ねながら夜をきく、ちょっと不思議な暮らし。

IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC)

## English Short Post

A tiny desert jumper with enormous ears.

Long-eared Jerboa  
*Euchoreutes naso*

It lives in desert and semi-desert habitats of Mongolia and northwestern China,  
moving among low shrubs after dark to search for insects.  
Huge ears, long hind legs, and a black-and-white tail tuft.  
A little night-hopping life among desert shrubs.

IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC)
