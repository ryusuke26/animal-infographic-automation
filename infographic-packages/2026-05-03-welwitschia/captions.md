## English X Post

In the desert fog belt, two leaves keep writing the plant's life.
Welwitschia
Welwitschia mirabilis

This Namib Desert gymnosperm grows as a low woody crown in gravelly desert habitat shaped by coastal fog.
Its only two leaves keep growing from the base and split into long ribbons over time.
A slightly strange life, unfolding two leaves in the path of fog.

IUCN Red List: Not Evaluated
