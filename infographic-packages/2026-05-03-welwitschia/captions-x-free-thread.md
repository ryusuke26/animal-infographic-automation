A Namib Desert gymnosperm. From a low woody crown, it grows only two leaves through a very long life.

Those leaves split into ribbons over time. A strange life, stretched out along the desert fog belt.

### Reply 3 / Status and Sources

IUCN Red List: Not Evaluated. Checked with Kew, SANBI, Nature Communications 2021, and PLOS ONE 2021.
