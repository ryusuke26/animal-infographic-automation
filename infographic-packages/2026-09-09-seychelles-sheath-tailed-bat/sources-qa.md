### Candidate 1 — Seychelles Sheath-tailed Bat / *Coleura seychellensis* — selected

- Global familiarity check: English exposure is concentrated in specialist bat, island-conservation and threatened-species pages and often repeats one rarity superlative; Japanese exact-name exposure is sparse. It is not a recurring zoo, mainstream wildlife-video or generic weird-animal staple.
- Discovery doorway: beneath horizontal ceilings in cool coastal boulder caves, a small dark bat waits for dusk and uses a clear cave mouth to reach forest, marsh and estuary feeding areas.
- Conservation doorway: the directly inspected current official IUCN record `T5112A271898609` confirms Global Critically Endangered (CR) under C2a(i), assessed 13 December 2024 and published in 2025.
- Local-knowledge caution: public copy names Seychelles and its island habitat directly. The Creole name Sousouri Bannan is recorded in source notes but not appropriated as a species-level title without stronger local-language authority.
- Visual viability: two exact-taxon IUCN photographs show a compact dark-brown bat on rock, modest triangular ears, a short muzzle, narrow pointed wings and coherent wing attachment. The hero uses the same natural roost posture; the tail sheath is not forced into view.

## Selected identity and naming

- Accepted public scientific name: *Coleura seychellensis* Peters, 1868, used by the current IUCN assessment and Mammal Diversity Database.
- Historical and infraspecific duplicate names: original spelling *Colëura seychellensis*; *Coleura silhouettae* Thomas, 1915; *C. s. seychellensis* and *C. s. silhouettae*.
- English public name: Seychelles Sheath-tailed Bat, used by IUCN and Mammal Diversity Database.
- Japanese public name: セーシェルサシオコウモリ, exposed by GBIF/Catalogue of Life, BirdNET taxonomy and Japanese aggregators. The package does not claim a formally standardized Japanese mammal-list authority.
- Local-language context: Nature Seychelles uses Sousouri Bannan; it remains context rather than the poster title.
- Editorial classification group: Mammals.
- Exact lineage: Animalia; Chordata; Mammalia; Chiroptera; Emballonuridae; *Coleura*.
- Broad native region: Mahé and Silhouette, Seychelles / western Indian Ocean.
- Habitat: coastal boulder-field caves with cool stable temperatures and horizontal ceilings, connected to native palm woodland, marsh, forest, estuary and other coastal foraging areas.
- Curiosity hook: a horizontal rock ceiling becomes the waiting place for a nightly flight through an open cave mouth.
