## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Coleura seychellensis* Peters, 1868. Historical names retained for duplicate screening include *Colëura seychellensis* and *Coleura silhouettae*; the current IUCN assessment recognizes two subspecies.
- English public name: Seychelles Sheath-tailed Bat.
- Japanese public name: セーシェルサシオコウモリ, exposed by GBIF/Catalogue of Life and Japanese taxonomy aggregators.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Critically Endangered (CR) under C2a(i), assessed 13 December 2024, record `T5112A271898609`.
- Public claims: cool coastal boulder caves with horizontal ceilings; clear flyways at cave mouths; nocturnal feeding on moths, beetles and flies around forest, marsh and estuary edges.
- Discovery doorway: a tiny dark bat waits beneath a cool rock ceiling, then follows a clear cave exit into the island night.
