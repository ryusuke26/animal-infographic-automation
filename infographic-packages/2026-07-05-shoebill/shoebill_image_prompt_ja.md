# Japanese Image Prompt

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: Japanese educational social media infographic, vertical 2:3 poster.
Primary request: Create a gentle curiosity-first natural-history infographic about the Shoebill, focused on discovery and education rather than advocacy.
Subject: ハシビロコウ, *Balaeniceps rex*, a large gray African freshwater-swamp bird with a massive pale yellowish shoe-shaped bill with dark mottling, a hooked bill tip, large forward-facing eyes, long dark legs, long separated toes, and a small rear head crest. It is not a heron, not a pelican, not a crane, not a colorful toucan.
Style/medium: childlike crayon and oil-pastel field-notebook poster, textured warm paper, handmade but polished and readable.
Composition/framing: Vertical 2:3 canvas. Top title and scientific name. Center one large shoebill standing still in shallow freshwater among papyrus and reeds. Exactly three short observation callouts around the bird. Bottom quiet label-free status footer. Generous whitespace.
Color palette: swamp greens, soft gray bird, pale straw bill, warm paper, muted water blues.
Text (verbatim):
"ハシビロコウ"
"Balaeniceps rex"
"パピルス湿地に立つ"
"靴みたいな大きなくちばし"
"じっと待って魚をねらう"
"IUCN Red List 2018: Vulnerable (VU)"
Constraints: Accurate legible Japanese text. Discovery-first, not moralizing. Do not invent population numbers. Do not imply blame. No logos, no watermark.
Avoid: Crowded poster, tiny text, fake maps, threat icons, rescue imagery, zoo bars, people, nests, chicks, duplicate birds, generic heron, pelican pouch, crane posture, flamingo colors, ocean setting.
