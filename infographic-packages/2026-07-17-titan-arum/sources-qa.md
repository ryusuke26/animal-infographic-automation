## Exact visual identity guidance

- Show **one** rooted titan arum in bloom on a humid Sumatran lowland-rainforest floor near a quiet limestone slope.
- Hero anatomy: one thick, upright pale yellow-olive spadix; one large, deep burgundy, strongly pleated spathe wrapped around its base; green-to-cream outer spathe; no petals and no separate bouquet of flowers.
- Keep a natural upright plant posture; show no human, no animal carcass, no fake map, and no duplicated hero plant.
- Three small note-card icon cues may show: a rainforest leaf/limestone hill, a cluster of tiny flowers at the base, and a warm-scent swirl with a small insect.
- Explicit negatives: not Rafflesia, not a generic red lily, not a mushroom, not a Venus flytrap, and not a carnivorous plant.
