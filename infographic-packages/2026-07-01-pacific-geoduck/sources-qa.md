## Visual Identity Guidance

- Show one large geoduck clam in a sandy/muddy Pacific Northwest seabed.
- Body plan: two pale oval shell valves partly open or partly buried; very large tan to beige fleshy siphon extending upward; mantle/siphon much larger than the shell.
- Natural posture: mostly buried in sediment, siphon vertical or gently curved upward into seawater.
