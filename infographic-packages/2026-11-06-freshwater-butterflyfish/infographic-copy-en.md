Title: Freshwater Butterflyfish
Scientific name: *Pantodon buchholzi*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: A leap to escape
   Explanation: When startled, it uses its pectoral fins to jump out of the water.
2. Heading: An ambush at the surface
   Explanation: It waits just below the surface for insects on or above the water.
3. Heading: Butterfly-like fins
   Explanation: Broad pectoral fins spread sideways while long pelvic-fin rays hang below.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2019: Least Concern (LC)
