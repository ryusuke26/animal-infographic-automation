Vertical ink-and-watercolor infographic titled Freshwater Butterflyfish, Pantodon buchholzi. A dominant mottled brown fish rests just below a calm jade-green surface, with a flattened head, upturned mouth, broad patterned pectoral fins spreading sideways and long thin pelvic-fin rays hanging below. Three illustrated numbered notes explain an escape leap from water, an ambush for insects at or above the surface, and the distinction between broad pectorals and hanging pelvic rays.
