## Evidence Lock

- Accepted scientific name: *Dinomys branickii* (Peters, 1873).
- English common name: Pacarana.
- Supported Japanese name: パカラナ.
- Native region: South America; Venezuela, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Brazil, and Bolivia.
- Habitat: montane forests; the Peruvian account also places it in lowland rainforest and Yungas protected areas.
- Global IUCN category/year: Least Concern (LC), assessed 1 March 2016; published 2017.
- Current population trend: Unknown in the official assessment; no trend claim is used publicly.
- Lineage: the only living species of the family Dinomyidae.
- Curiosity doorway: a large white-spotted rodent that sits on its hind legs and holds fruit or stems in both forepaws while eating.

## Naming and status notes

- The Mammal Science standard-name list pairs *Dinomys branickii* with パカラナ and places it in パカラナ科 / Dinomyidae.
- The official IUCN table marks the 2016 VU to 2017 LC change as non-genuine (`N`), meaning a change arising from information, criteria application, taxonomy, or correction rather than a confirmed biological improvement.
- Older pages may still display VU. Public copy follows the current official Global LC record and does not describe a recovery.
- No population estimate, national status, threat ranking, or absolute rarity claim is used publicly.

## Visual identity guidance

- One heavy-bodied, large rodent with a broad blunt head, small rounded ears, dark grey-brown coat, and several clear rows of white spots along the flanks.
- Natural feeding pose: seated upright on two visibly separate hind feet, both forearms visibly connected from shoulders to separate forepaws holding one fruit; one connected furry tail resting on the ground.
- Keep all four limbs anatomically readable: each has a visible origin, separate path, and separate endpoint with negative space between left and right counterparts.
- Not a capybara, paca, porcupine, guinea pig, bear cub, raccoon, or generic rat. No quills, stripes, ringed tail, long mouse tail, hoof-like feet, duplicated animal, or extra limbs.
