3–5 defining features:
1. Broad lateral attachment straight into wood, no upright central stalk — both real photographs and MDC stalk-not-present description.
2. Thin leathery semicircular fans, irregular overlapping shelf profile, small relative to log — photograph foreground and MDC description.
3. Concentric cream/rust/brown/slate-grey bands, light outer margin — upper surfaces in both photographs; not a uniform brown or neon rainbow.
4. Fine velvety upper zones alternating with smoother zones — real detail and MDC; sketch simplification allowed.
5. Pale underside with dense round/angular pores, never radiating gills — partially exposed undersides in detail photograph plus MDC/Midpen descriptions. Magnification is qualitative, pore size not calibrated.
Natural low three-quarter log view; overlaps may conceal bases, but visible cap bases must meet wood. No roots, free stems or detached petals. No forced all-surfaces view. Hidden anatomy mechanism rule not triggered; an exposed underside is ordinary exterior.
