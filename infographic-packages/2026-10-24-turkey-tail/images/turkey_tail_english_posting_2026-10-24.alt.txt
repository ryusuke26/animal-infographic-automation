Vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel poster titled Turkey Tail, Trametes versicolor. Thin fans with brown, slate-blue and cream bands and pale margins overlap on a woodland log. Three numbered illustrated cards: 1 shows a pale underside still attached to wood and a magnified study of tiny pores where spores are produced; 2 shows fine white mycelial threads inside a qualitative wood cutaway, explaining decomposition; 3 shows thin stalkless fans overlapping on hardwood. Footer: No global IUCN assessment confirmed (checked 2026).
