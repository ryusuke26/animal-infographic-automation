# Hispaniolan Solenodon Infographic Package

## 1. Selection Rationale

Hispaniolan solenodon / ヒスパニオラソレノドン (*Solenodon paradoxus*) is a strong curiosity-first subject because it is an island-endemic mammal with a long flexible snout, nocturnal burrowing life, and venom delivered through grooved lower incisors. It invites natural-history interest without needing urgency or rescue framing.
