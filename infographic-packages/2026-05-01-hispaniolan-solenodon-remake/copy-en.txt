A night forager with grooved teeth.
Hispaniolan Solenodon
Solenodon paradoxus

On Hispaniola, the island shared by Haiti and the Dominican Republic,
it moves at night and probes soil and leaf litter with a long snout.
One lower incisor has a groove linked to venom delivery.
A slightly mysterious life, following the night ground by nose.

IUCN Red List 2020: Least Concern (LC)
