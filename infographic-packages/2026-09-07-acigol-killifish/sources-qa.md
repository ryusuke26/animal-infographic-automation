- Global familiarity check: English exposure is concentrated in IUCN, freshwater-fish conservation and specialist aquarium material; Japanese exposure is limited to database and machine-translated scholarly records. - Conservation doorway: the directly inspected current official IUCN record `T1850A135082408` confirms Global Critically Endangered (CR) under B2ab(i,ii,iii,iv,v), assessed 4 July 2023 and published in 2026.

A calm side view carries the identity without difficult motion anatomy.
