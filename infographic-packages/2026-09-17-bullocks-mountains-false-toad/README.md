## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Telmatobufo bullocki* Schmidt, 1952.
- English public name: Bullock's Mountains False Toad.
- Japanese public name: ブロックヤマモドキヒキガエル, a transparent rendering rather than a claimed standardized Japanese list name.
- Status: Global Endangered (EN) under IUCN criterion B1ab(iii), assessed 10 July 2015 in record `T21623A79810144`, published in 2017 and marked `Needs updating`.
- Public claims: the tadpole has a depressed body, sucker-like mouth and muscular tail suited to fast-flowing streams; post-breeding adults use terrestrial forest and have been found about 500 m from breeding streams; adults are olive-brown with a yellow band between the eyes.
- Discovery doorway: a torrent tadpole holds to rock with a sucker-like mouth, then grows into a forest-moving frog.

## Final QA

- Both posters show one dominant adult *Telmatobufo bullocki* with a broad blunt head, two large dark eyes, a broad yellow interocular band, pale lip edge, oval parotoid glands, many smooth rounded dorsal gland masses, a robust olive-brown body and coherent naturally occluded limbs.
- The posters do not drift into a neon poison frog, tree frog, generic finely warty bufonid or black-violet orange-spotted *Telmatobufo venustus*.
- Exactly three numbered illustrated cards cover one intact torrent tadpole, post-breeding movement from stream into forest and the adult's yellow facial band. Every heading and explanation matches Copy Lock; no extra public text remains.
