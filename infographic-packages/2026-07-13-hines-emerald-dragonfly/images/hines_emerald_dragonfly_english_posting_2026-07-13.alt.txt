Vertical field-notebook infographic about Hine's Emerald. One dragonfly patrols a shallow groundwater-fed North American sedge wetland. It has brilliant green compound eyes, a dark metallic-green thorax with two creamy-yellow side stripes, a long blackish-brown abdomen, six legs, and four clear wings. Three numbered illustrated cards explain its wetland habitat, adult field marks, and larval shelter in crayfish burrows during dry periods and winter.
