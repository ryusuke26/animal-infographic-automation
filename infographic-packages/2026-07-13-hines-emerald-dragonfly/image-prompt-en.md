# English Image Prompt

Use case: infographic poster.
Canvas: direct source must be vertical 2:3, no border or padding.
Create a warm childlike field-notebook educational poster with crayon, oil pastel, colored pencil, textured warm paper, imperfect lines, and careful adult art direction.

Show one accurate adult Hine's emerald dragonfly in a North American groundwater-fed sedge fen with a narrow shallow streamlet, low sedges, a small marl/limestone cue, meadow, and soft forest edge. The dragonfly must have exactly four clear wings and six legs, brilliant mature emerald-green compound eyes, a dark metallic-green thorax, two distinct creamy-yellow lateral stripes visible on the near side, and a long slender blackish-brown abdomen. Use a natural low-patrol or light perched posture. It is a true dragonfly, not a damselfly, butterfly, generic blue pond dragonfly, clubtail, or fantasy glowing insect.

Composition: title and scientific name at top; one large hero dragonfly in habitat; exactly three numbered observation cards around it. Each card must include a visible number, a small illustrated icon, and the exact explanatory two-line copy. Card 3 may show only a tiny burrow-opening/crayfish-claw icon; no second hero animal, underground cutaway, lifecycle panel, map, threat scene, people, rescue slogan, logo, or watermark. Put the quiet status footer at the bottom. Keep all text large, sparse, exact, and legible.

Text, verbatim:
"HINE'S EMERALD"
"Somatochlora hineana"
"GROUNDWATER-FED WETLAND"
"GREEN EYES, TWO YELLOW STRIPES"
"INTO CRAYFISH BURROWS"
"IUCN Red List 2018: Least Concern (LC)"

Additional locked card explanation lines, also verbatim:
"Shallow flow supports larvae"
"Adult field marks"
"Larvae shelter from dry spells and winter"

Avoid extra words, misspellings, duplicated specimens, extra cards, anatomical inset, fake map, neon glow, incorrect wing or leg count, crowded layout, moralizing, blame, urgency, and unsupported population graphics.
