## Evidence route

The supplied official IUCN assessment PDF and current-page capture directly confirm *Oroanassa magnifica* as Global Near Threatened (NT), scope Global, assessed 12 May 2025 under criterion C2a(ii), record `T22697232A175978137`, DOI `10.2305/IUCN.UK.2025-2.RLTS.T22697232A175978137.en`. The PDF's taxonomy section records *Gorsachius magnificus* and *Nycticorax magnificus* as synonyms of the current combination. ITIS and AviList independently support *Oroanassa magnifica*.

## Locked visual concept

An authored dusk forest notebook follows one lean dark night heron beside a shaded stream. A slim, partly extended shallow-S neck with a visible narrowing below the head prevents the silhouette from collapsing into the squat barrel shape of a generic Black-crowned Night Heron, while an oval torso keeps it from drifting into an egret or crane. White post-ocular ear-stripes, white cheek and throat, chestnut neck sides, long dark nape plumes, brown streaked underparts, and long yellowish legs complete the identity. The hero remains stable and unobstructed; exactly three unequal illustrated cards curve around the body and stream bank.
