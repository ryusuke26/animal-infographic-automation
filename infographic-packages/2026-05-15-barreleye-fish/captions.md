Barreleye fish

暗い水を見上げる、ちょっと不思議な暮らし。

## English X Post

Inside a clear head, green eyes look upward.  
Barreleye fish  
Macropinna microstoma

This small deep-sea fish drifts in North Pacific midwater. It scans for silhouettes above, then turns its eyes forward when feeding.  
The dark spots near the mouth are nares, not eyes.  
A slightly strange life spent looking through its own forehead.

IUCN Red List: Not Evaluated (NE; FishBase 2025-2)
