Barreleye fish
Macropinna microstoma

Green eyes look upward from inside a clear head.
A small deep-sea fish drifting in North Pacific midwater.
It usually scans for silhouettes above, then turns its eyes forward when feeding.
The dark spots near the mouth are nares, not eyes.

IUCN Red List: Not Evaluated (NE; FishBase 2025-2)
