## Image Identity Guidance

- Draw the transparent dome as a clear shield over the head.
- Put the green tubular eyes inside the dome, not on the outside face.
- Keep the two dark nares above the mouth visually separate from the eyes.
- Avoid anglerfish lures, huge cartoon external eyes, monster teeth, or fake maps.
- Large flat fins and quiet hovering are acceptable because MBARI describes those fins as helping it remain nearly motionless and maneuver precisely.
