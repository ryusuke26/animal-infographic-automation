## English Thread

1. A fish that looks upward through its own clear head: the barreleye fish, Macropinna microstoma. #DeepSea

2. It lives in North Pacific midwater, often in the twilight zone, where faint silhouettes matter.

3. The green tubular eyes sit inside the transparent head. The dark spots near the mouth are nares, not eyes.

4. IUCN Red List: Not Evaluated (NE; FishBase 2025-2). Sources: MBARI, FishBase, GBIF.
