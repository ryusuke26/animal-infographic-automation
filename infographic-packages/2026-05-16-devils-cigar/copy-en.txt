Devil's cigar
Chorioactis geaster

This rare cup fungus rises from decaying wood like a dark, fuzzy cigar.
As it matures, the closed body splits open into a small star.
The smoky puff is a cloud of spores leaving the fruiting body.

It is known from scattered records in Texas/Oklahoma and Japan, with newer Taiwan context noted in the source file.

IUCN Red List: formal global assessment not located (checked 2026)
Japan Red List 5th ed. 2025: Vulnerable (VU)
