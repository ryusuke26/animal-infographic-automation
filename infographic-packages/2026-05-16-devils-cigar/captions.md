## English X Post

A dark cigar on the forest floor opens into a small star.  
Devil's cigar  
Chorioactis geaster

This rare cup fungus grows from decaying wood. Its closed body looks like a fuzzy cigar, then splits open into star-like rays and releases spores.  
The smoky puff is part of its spore release.  
A slightly strange life hidden in scattered forests.

IUCN Red List checked 2026: formal assessment not located  
Japan Red List 2025: Vulnerable (VU)
