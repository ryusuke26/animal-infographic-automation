## Image Identity Guidance

- Show a fungus fruiting body, not a cap-and-stem mushroom.
- Include both stages if possible: dark fuzzy closed capsule and opened star.
- Opened body should have thick leathery rays and tan to orange inner surface.
- Habitat should be forest floor / decaying wood, not ocean, desert, or flower meadow.
- Avoid fake maps and avoid making the spore puff look like smoke from fire.

## QA Notes

- Image Gen Japanese raster: acceptable for identity, with exact typography treated as approximate.
- Image Gen English raster: acceptable for identity, but "Texas, Oklahoma and Japan" should be treated as short public context, not a complete global distribution statement.
- Text-safe SVG/PNG backups contain deterministic poster wording.
- SVG XML parse should pass after creation.
- No population number, fake map, urgency slogan, blame, or rescue framing is included.
