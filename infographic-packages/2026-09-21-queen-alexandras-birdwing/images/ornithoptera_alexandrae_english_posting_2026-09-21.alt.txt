Vertical 2:3 crayon-and-oil-pastel field-notebook poster. In Papua New Guinea rainforest, one female Queen Alexandra's Birdwing, Ornithoptera alexandrae, rests open-winged on a vine leaf. Four broad tailless wings are deep brown with cream markings; the abdomen is pale yellow. Three numbered illustrated cards show the female's roughly 27 cm wingspan, the smaller blue-green male beside the brown female, and an Aristolochia vine used by feeding caterpillars. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2018: Endangered (EN).
