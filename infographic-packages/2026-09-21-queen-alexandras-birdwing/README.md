## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Ornithoptera alexandrae* (Rothschild, 1907).
- Public names: Queen Alexandra's Birdwing / アレキサンドラトリバネアゲハ.
- Status: Global Endangered (EN) under IUCN criterion B2ab(iii), assessed 24 January 2018.
- Public claims: record-size broad brown-and-cream female, smaller blue-green male and larval feeding on *Aristolochia* vines.
- Discovery doorway: the world's largest butterfly is the female, while the male looks almost like a different species.

## Production result

- Evidence Lock, compact visual identity brief and bilingual cards-v2 Copy Lock are complete.
- The Japanese and English direct posters were accepted after full-size and phone-size review. A single English title-only repair experiment was rejected because the original already contained a handwritten apostrophe and the attempted edit needlessly redrew pixels outside that glyph.

- Both posters retain the adult female's broad tailless brown-and-cream wings, thick pale abdomen and coherent four-wing body; the male appears only in the small dimorphism card.
- Exactly three illustrated observation cards, locked text and visible crayon/oil-pastel marks on warm paper are present in both languages.
- Direct-source, X-format, synchronized-sidecar and full-package validation pass.
