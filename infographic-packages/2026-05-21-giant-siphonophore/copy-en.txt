Giant Siphonophore
Praya dubia / Siphonophorae

A very long, thin colonial animal drifting through pelagic midwater.

Many small roles in one line
Swimming, feeding, reproduction, and defense are handled by specialized zooids.

A glowing thread in dark water
Fine stinging tentacles trail through the water and catch small crustaceans and other tiny animals.

A hidden road in the deep sea
It shares the midwater with animals moving between depth and the surface each day.

IUCN Red List checked 2026: species assessment not located
