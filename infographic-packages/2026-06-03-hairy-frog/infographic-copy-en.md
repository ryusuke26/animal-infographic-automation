## Text-Safe Note

The poster uses *Trichobatrachus robustus*, the IUCN-facing and widely recognized name. AMNH Amphibian Species of the World currently treats the accepted combination as *Astylosternus robustus*; the caveat is recorded in README and `sources-qa.md`.
