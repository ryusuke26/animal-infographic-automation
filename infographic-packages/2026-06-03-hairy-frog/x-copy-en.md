## Main Post

In a forest stream, a male frog can look almost hairy.
Hairy Frog
*Trichobatrachus robustus*

This Central African frog is known for breeding males that grow hair-like skin papillae along the body and thighs.
They are skin, not fur.
At the toes, it also hides bony claw-like structures.

IUCN Red List 2017: Least Concern (LC)
