## Thread

1. A forest-stream frog that can look almost hairy: Hairy Frog, *Trichobatrachus robustus*.

2. The "hair" is not fur. Breeding males grow skin papillae along the flanks and thighs.

3. Its toes also hide bony claw-like structures, shown here as a quiet anatomy note rather than horror.

4. IUCN Red List 2017: Least Concern (LC). No population number used in this package.
