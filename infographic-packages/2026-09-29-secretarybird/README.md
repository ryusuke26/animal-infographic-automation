## Locked scope

- Accepted taxon: *Sagittarius serpentarius*; Secretarybird / ヘビクイワシ.
- IUCN Global Endangered (EN), assessed 30 April 2020, directly confirmed by the official assessment PDF and current-page capture.
- One adult in open savanna, ground hunting, tree roost and pair nest; exactly three illustrated explanatory cards.
