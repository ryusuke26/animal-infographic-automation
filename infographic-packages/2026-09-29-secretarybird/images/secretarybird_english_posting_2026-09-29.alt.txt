Vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel observation poster. One secretarybird strides through open African grassland: grey body, black folded wing feathers, swept-back dark crest, orange-red bare face and very long legs. Three numbered cards with small drawings explain hunting on foot, roosting in an acacia at night, and a pair's twig nest in a tree. The footer gives the IUCN global Endangered assessment from 2020.
