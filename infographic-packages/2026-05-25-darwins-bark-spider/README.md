## Image Identity Guidance

Show a female bark spider sitting at the hub of a large orb web suspended above a narrow rainforest stream in Madagascar. Essential cues: web visibly anchored from bank vegetation across the water; a mottled grey-brown, bark-like spider with a broad, angular abdomen and eight legs; small aquatic flying insects near the web rather than a trapped bird or bat. The spider should remain realistically small relative to the stream-spanning web. Avoid generic golden orb-weaver colors, tarantula proportions, giant monster scale, fake maps, and tensile-strength diagrams.
