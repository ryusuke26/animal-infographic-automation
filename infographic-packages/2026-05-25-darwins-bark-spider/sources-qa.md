## Image QA Checklist

- Eight-legged orb-weaver is shown at realistic scale, not a tarantula or giant creature.
- Body is mottled grey-brown and bark-like; avoid bright generic golden orb-weaver styling.
- A complete orb web crosses above a narrow forest stream and visibly attaches to vegetation on both sides.
- Supporting insects are small flying aquatic insects, not trapped vertebrates.
- No fake map, threat claim, population count, strength chart, or Red List category.
- Exact wording is supplied by the text-safe backup if generated lettering is imperfect.
