## English X Post

A flower that waits underground.
Jackal food
Hydnora africana

In dry southern African landscapes, this parasitic plant spends most of its life hidden below the soil.
No leaves, no green color: it is connected to host roots.
After rain, a fleshy flower appears and its scent calls beetles.

IUCN Red List: Least Concern (LC)
SANBI Red List 2021: Least Concern (LC)
