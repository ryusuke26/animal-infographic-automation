## Verdict

Overall: Ready, with one conservation-status caveat.

The package is publication-safe if the footer keeps the distinction between Kew's IUCN LC display and SANBI's 2021 national LC listing. Do not add an IUCN assessment year unless a directly accessible IUCN record supplies one.
