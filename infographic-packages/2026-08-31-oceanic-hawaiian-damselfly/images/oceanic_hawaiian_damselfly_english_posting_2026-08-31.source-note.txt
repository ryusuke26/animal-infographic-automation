Source note: official IUCN assessment e.T12958A510839 lists Megalagrion oceanicum as Global CR under B1ab(iii,v), assessed 17 January 2019. USFWS and Hawai'i DLNR support the Oahu stream riffle, cascade, and waterfall-face habitat; the large robust red-orange male with black stripes; fast adult stream flight; and naiads crawling among gravel and sometimes onto wet moss.
