Vertical 2:3 handmade streamside field-notebook poster. Above a green riffle and small waterfall face in Oahu's windward Koolau Mountains, one male Oceanic Hawaiian Damselfly, Megalagrion oceanicum, perches on a wet diagonal twig. Its long segmented abdomen is red-orange with narrow black marks, and its orange-red thorax has bold black stripes. Four transparent wings fold together over the abdomen; six slender legs connect beneath the thorax and grip the twig. The English title and scientific name are integrated at the top. Three unequal numbered illustrated cards show stream rapids and waterfalls, the large red-orange adult, and a complete aquatic naiad crawling among gravel with three leaf-like terminal gills. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2019: Critically Endangered (CR).
