## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Megalagrion oceanicum* (McLachlan, 1883).
- English public name: Oceanic Hawaiian Damselfly.
- Japanese public name: オセアニック・ハワイアン・ダムセルフライ. No standardized Japanese zoological name was confirmed; this is a transparent katakana rendering of the authoritative English common name.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Critically Endangered (CR) under B1ab(iii,v), assessed 17 January 2019 and published in 2020, record `T12958A510839`.
- Public claims: it breeds in fast O'ahu stream rapids and waterfall faces; the large robust male is red-orange with black stripes; aquatic naiads crawl among rock and gravel in riffles and may forage on wet moss beside the channel.
- Discovery doorway: one life crawls beneath standing waves and over wet moss before becoming a bright red-orange adult flying fast along the same stream corridor.
