# Lowland Streaked Tenrec Infographic Package

Run time: 2026-04-29 22:39 JST

## 1. Species Selection Rationale

Selected species: Lowland streaked tenrec / ローランド・ストリークド・テンレック
Scientific name: Hemicentetes semispinosus

Why this works for a curiosity-first recurring infographic:

- It is visually memorable without needing crisis framing: black-and-yellow stripes, a long snout, and spines.
- Its natural-history doorway is unusual: some specialized spines can be rubbed together to make high-frequency sounds.
- It is Madagascar-endemic, but the copy can stay observational and respectful rather than speaking as an outside rescuer.
- The global conservation footer can stay quiet: IUCN Red List: Least Concern (2016 assessment), with no population-number claim.
