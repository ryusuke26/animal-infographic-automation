Title: Giant Clam
Scientific name: *Tridacna gigas*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Algae share their food
   Explanation: Algae in its tissues use sunlight and pass nutrients to the clam.
2. Heading: A second way to feed
   Explanation: It also filters tiny plankton from seawater.
3. Heading: The largest living bivalve
   Explanation: Its shell can grow longer than one metre.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2024: Critically Endangered (CR)
