Vertical poster in a woodblock-print style. Title "Narwhal" and scientific name on a paper label at upper left. In the centre a grey-brown narwhal mottled with white flecks swims left through indigo water below floating ice; one long, straight spiral tusk grows from the front of its upper jaw, and it has no dorsal fin. Three numbered figures: 1, a round frame at upper right, a see-through top view of the head with two upper-jaw teeth, the left one growing forward into the tusk and the right one a short stub inside the jaw; 2, lower left, two males crossing tusks in a summer bay ringed by mountains, with a tuskless narwhal nearby; 3, a tall panel at lower right, a dotted path from a crack in the ice down to a narwhal diving head-first into the dark, with fish on the sea floor. Each has a heading and explanation. Footer: IUCN Red List 2017: Least Concern (LC).
