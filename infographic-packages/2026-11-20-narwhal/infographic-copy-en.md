Title: Narwhal
Scientific name: *Monodon monoceros*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: The tusk is a tooth
   Explanation: In males, the left tooth of the upper jaw grows forward out of the head, spiraling counterclockwise to 2 to 3 m long.
2. Heading: Crossing tusks, comparing rank
   Explanation: In summer bays, males gently cross their tusks, behavior thought to help them settle social rank.
3. Heading: Winter dives beneath the ice
   Explanation: Offshore in winter, under more than 99% sea-ice cover, it dives deeper than 800 m some 18 to 25 times a day.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2017: Least Concern (LC)
