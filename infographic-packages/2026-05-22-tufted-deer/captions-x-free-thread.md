# Japanese 140-character thread draft

1. 森の暗がりに、前髪と小さな犬歯。今日はマエガミジカ、Elaphodus cephalophus。

2. 中国南部からミャンマー北部の山地林で、夕方や夜に葉や小枝、果実を食べる小さなシカです。

3. オスは黒い前髪に短い角をかくし、上あごの犬歯をのぞかせます。森のしげみで静かに合図する暮らし。

4. IUCN Red List 2015: Near Threatened (NT)。出典はMDD、ADW、Kotobank、Han et al. 2020など。

# English 140-character thread draft

1. A little forest deer with bangs and canines: Tufted deer, Elaphodus cephalophus.

2. It lives in mountain forests of southern China and northern Myanmar, browsing leaves, twigs, fruit, and grasses at dusk and night.

3. Males have tiny antlers partly hidden by a dark forehead tuft, plus long upper canines.

4. IUCN Red List 2015: Near Threatened (NT). Sources checked: MDD, ADW, Kotobank, Han et al. 2020, Leslie et al. 2013.
