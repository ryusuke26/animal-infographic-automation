# Japanese X Post

森の暗がりに、前髪と小さな犬歯。
Tufted deer
Elaphodus cephalophus

中国南部からミャンマー北部の山地林で、夕方や夜にそっと草木を食べる小さなシカです。
オスは前髪にかくれた短い角と、上あごの長い犬歯をもっています。
森のしげみで静かに合図する、ちょっと不思議な暮らし。

IUCN Red List 2015: Near Threatened (NT)

#世界の知らない生きもの #マエガミジカ

ALT: クレヨンとオイルパステル風の縦長ポスター。湿った山地林のしげみに、暗褐色の小さなシカ「マエガミジカ」が立っている。額には黒い前髪のような毛の房があり、短い角が少しかくれ、口元には上あごの犬歯がのぞく。周囲に山地林、夜行性の採食、前髪と犬歯、IUCN Near Threatened の短い注記がある。

# English X Post

A little forest deer with bangs and canines.
Tufted deer
Elaphodus cephalophus

In mountain forests of southern China and northern Myanmar, this small deer browses quietly around dusk and night.
Males have tiny antlers partly hidden by a dark forehead tuft, plus long upper canines.
A quiet, slightly strange life in the understory.

IUCN Red List 2015: Near Threatened (NT)

ALT: A vertical crayon and oil-pastel field-notebook poster showing a small dark brown-gray tufted deer in moist mountain forest understory. The deer has a black forehead tuft, tiny spike-like antlers partly hidden by the tuft, visible upper canines, and a white underside to the raised tail. Short labels note mountain forest habitat, dusk/night browsing, canines, and IUCN Near Threatened status.

Sources checked: Mammal Diversity Database, Animal Diversity Web, Kotobank, Han et al. 2020, and Leslie et al. 2013. Older population figures were omitted; the public footer uses only the IUCN 2015 Near Threatened status.
