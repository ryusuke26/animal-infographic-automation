## Image Identity Guidance

Show a small dark brown-gray deer, not a generic fawn. Important cues: black/dark forehead tuft, small spike-like antlers mostly hidden by the tuft, male upper canines visible but not exaggerated into predator fangs, white underside of raised tail, forest understory with moist mountain-valley plants. Avoid vampire/horror styling, oversized antlers, savanna grassland, snow, and fake maps.
