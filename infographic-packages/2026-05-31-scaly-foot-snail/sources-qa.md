## Source Notes

- JAMSTEC 2019 confirms the Japanese public name `スケーリーフット`, IUCN Endangered registration, and the assessment basis. It also records concern around future seafloor resource development, but final public copy keeps this as background rather than a headline.
- University Museum, University of Tokyo records `ウロコフネタマガイ(スケーリーフット)`, paratype context, Indian Ocean hydrothermal vent habitat, and scale/iron-sulfide details.
- NCBI Taxonomy is used for cross-checking name, authorship, rank, common name, and broad lineage, with the NCBI caveat that nomenclature should be checked against literature.
- JAMSTEC 2026 reports population-genomic sampling across at least eight Indian Ocean vent fields, so this package avoids older public claims that the species is known only from three vent fields.
- No current population estimate is included.
