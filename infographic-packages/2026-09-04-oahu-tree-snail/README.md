## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Achatinella mustelina* Mighels, 1845.
- English public name: Oʻahu Tree Snail, supported by USFWS genus usage and the NatureServe species record.
- Japanese public name: オアフ・ツリースネイル, a transparent rendering; no established Japanese zoological standard name was confirmed.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Critically Endangered (CR) under criteria A1ce, B1+2abcde, assessed 1 August 1996, record `T191A13048229`; the record is annotated Needs updating.
- Public claims: it lives in trees of Oʻahu's Waiʻanae Range; its small smooth glossy conical shell carries variable bands; at night it grazes fungi from leaves and bears live young.
- Discovery doorway: a jewel-like tree snail moves after dark, grazes the leaf surface, and gives birth to one tiny live young at a time.
