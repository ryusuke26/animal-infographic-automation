## Story reply

```text
In Oʻahu's Waiʻanae forests, this snail rests on leaves and trunks by day. After dark it creeps out to graze fungi from leaf surfaces. Years pass before maturity; then a parent bears one tiny live young at a time—a whole life unfolding in one tree.
```
