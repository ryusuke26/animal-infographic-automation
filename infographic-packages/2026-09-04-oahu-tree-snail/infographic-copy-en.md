# English Copy Lock

Title: Oʻahu Tree Snail
Scientific name: *Achatinella mustelina*

Observation labels:

1. Lives in trees of Oʻahu's Waiʻanae Range
2. A glossy conical shell with varied bands
3. Grazes leaf fungi at night and bears live young

Footer/status:

IUCN Red List 1996: Critically Endangered (CR)
