| Claim | Verdict | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| *Hymenopus coronatus* Olivier, 1792 is the accepted species name used here. | accurate | GBIF Backbone page lists rank species and classification. |
| ハナカマキリ is a Japanese common name. | accurate | GBIF common-name field lists ハナカマキリ. |

| IUCN category. | cannot verify | IUCN Red List search checked 2026-05-19 did not surface a species assessment; package does not assign LC/VU/EN. |

## Visual QA checklist

- One clear mantis, not just a flower.
- Raptorial forelegs visible.
- Petal-like lobes visible on middle/hind legs.
- White-to-pale-pink body with green tropical vegetation.
- No fake map or population number.
- No blame, rescue, or urgency imagery.
- Exact text preserved in text-safe SVG/PNG backups.
