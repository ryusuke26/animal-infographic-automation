Title: Fennec Fox
Scientific name: *Vulpes zerda*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Furry soles for hot sand
   Explanation: Fur on the soles shields the footpads from hot desert sand.
2. Heading: Ears that release heat
   Explanation: Those huge ears both detect prey sounds and dissipate excess heat.
3. Heading: Underground until dusk
   Explanation: Daytime is spent in a cool burrow; foraging begins at dusk.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2015: Least Concern (LC)
