A vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel field-notebook poster titled Fennec Fox, with Vulpes zerda beneath. An adult stands by sandy dunes and sparse dry grass, with huge upright ears, a buff back, cream face and legs, black nose and a bushy dark-tipped tail. Three numbered illustrated cards show: 1, an attached furry lower foreleg and paw on sand, explaining protection of the footpads; 2, a head-and-neck study with warm strokes beside the ears, explaining hearing and heat release; 3, a small fennec curled in a sandy den, explaining daytime shelter and dusk foraging. Footer: IUCN Red List 2015: Least Concern (LC).
