# Curiosity-first infographic package: Hispaniolan solenodon

Run date: 2026-05-01

## 1) Species/topic selection rationale

**Selected topic:** Hispaniolan solenodon / ヒスパニオラソレノドン  
**Scientific name:** *Solenodon paradoxus* Brandt, 1833

I selected the Hispaniolan solenodon because it is a lesser-known island mammal with a strong discovery hook: a long flexible snout, nocturnal leaf-litter foraging, and an oral venom system in a mammal. It works well for a curiosity-first field-notebook poster because the story can stay focused on observation rather than emergency framing.

The species also has an authoritative recent global assessment: IUCN Red List 2020, Least Concern (LC), with the assessment noting ongoing habitat destruction/degradation in parts of its range but no evidence of recent subpopulation declines or extirpations. That lets the footer stay quiet and precise.
