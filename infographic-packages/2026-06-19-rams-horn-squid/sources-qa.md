## Visual Identity Guidance

- Show one small squid-like cephalopod in dark open water, not a nautilus, snail, ammonite, jellyfish, or ordinary squid.
- Body should be compact, reddish-brown to silvery, with large eyes, ten appendages near the head, and small paired fins at the rear.
- A pale, ghosted internal ram-horn spiral shell may be visible inside the rear body as a field-note transparency, but it must not be drawn as an external shell carried outside the body.
- Add a tiny greenish glow near the tail between the fins to suggest the photophore.
- Posture should follow the 2020 in situ observation: near-vertical in open water, head and arms upward, mantle/tail and photophore downward. Avoid the older head-down reconstruction and avoid a horizontal swimming-squid pose.
- Avoid fake maps, beach shell-only scenes, external spiral shells, oversized tentacle drama, predator attack scenes, and threat graphics.
