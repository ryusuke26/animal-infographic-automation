## Main Post

```text
In a misty mountain forest, one plant combines a feeding station and a toilet.
Low's Pitcher Plant
Nepenthes lowii

This climbing pitcher plant grows in northern and north-central Borneo.
Mountain tree shrews feed on white secretion beneath a mature pitcher's lid, then leave droppings that provide the plant with nitrogen.

IUCN Red List 2000: Vulnerable (VU)
```
