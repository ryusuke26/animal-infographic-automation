## Locked Public Claims

1. *Nepenthes lowii* is a climbing pitcher plant native to northern and north-central Borneo.
2. Mature aerial pitchers have a bulbous lower chamber, narrow waist, wide opening, and an upright or reflexed lid that produces a white food secretion.
3. Mountain tree shrews feed at the lid and deposit droppings into the pitcher, providing the plant with nitrogen.

## Visual Identity Guidance

Show a mature aerial pitcher of *Nepenthes lowii* hanging from a climbing vine in misty Bornean montane forest. The pitcher must have a rounded bulbous lower chamber, a dramatic narrow waist, a broad flared upper opening, and a large lid held upright or reflexed. Show pale white secretion beneath the lid. A mountain tree shrew may straddle the opening while feeding from the lid, with its hindquarters over the pitcher; show a small slender squirrel-like mammal with pointed snout, rounded ears, four limbs, and a long tail. Avoid a generic tubular pitcher plant, a North American pitcher plant, a porcelain toilet, a flower, a monkey, a rat inside the pitcher, extra limbs, a giant mammal, fake maps, population counters, or rescue imagery.
