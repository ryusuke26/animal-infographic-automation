# SANBI biological field transcription

Source: https://biodiversityadvisor.sanbi.org/search/detail/80866
Directly rendered and read in the in-app browser on 5 October 2026.

- Morphological description, attributed to Tolley and Burger 2007: short tail; pointed casque; absent gular crest; spaced knob-like dorsal spikes become smaller toward tail and do not extend onto tail; relatively long legs; variable body colours with pale flank patches.
- Trophic strategy, citing Branch 1998: locusts, crickets, lizards and snakes listed as food. Public copy retains insects and occasional small lizards; no daily consumption number.
- Global distribution: Angola, Namibia, South Africa.
- Generic chameleon movement text is not used to override the species-specific terrestrial account in Suricata 2014.

Manual field record, not a screenshot or independent observation. SANBI regional status is not used as the global IUCN footer.
