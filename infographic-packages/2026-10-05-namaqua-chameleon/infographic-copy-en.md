Title: Namaqua Chameleon
Scientific name: *Chamaeleo namaquensis*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Colour changes help manage heat
   Explanation: It darkens when cold and pales when hot to help regulate body temperature.
2. Heading: A chameleon on the ground
   Explanation: It lives and forages on sand and gravel in arid landscapes.
3. Heading: More than an insect menu
   Explanation: It eats insects and sometimes small lizards, too.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2009: Least Concern (LC)
