- Individual/stage: adult-sized, sex unspecified. No sex-specific reproductive/display claim. Two photographed colour states are identity/variation references, not a controlled same-individual temperature experiment. SANBI description covers variable brown/green/burgundy colours; use grey-brown hero and pale card study. Do not invent fixed sex colours.
- Reference roles: references/iucn-namaqua.jpg (Krystal A. Tolley) whole lateral adult, casque, eye, spine row, feet, tail; references/sanbi-pale-namaqua.jpg (R. Babb, Suricata p. 301) pale complete individual, long legs/short tail. Existing purple-frog benchmark controls strokes/warmth only.
- Defining features: (1) oval compressed robust torso and relatively long legs, whole IUCN profile; (2) turret eye with central opening, blunt short snout, pointed backwards-swept casque, IUCN head and SANBI description; (3) widely spaced knob-like back spikes becoming smaller towards tail and not continuing onto tail, IUCN back/SANBI morphology; (4) short tapering tail, no longer than shown reference, broad tail base and mild tip curl, IUCN tail/SANBI pale photo; (5) irregular dark flank blotches and pale round patches, subtly ochre throat grooves, no gular spike crest/neck flap/nose horns, IUCN body/SANBI morphology.
- Feet: opposed fused digit groups visible in IUCN photograph, coherent supporting limb paths. Natural stable lateral walking/standing pose; far-side legs may be hidden by torso or nearer legs. No forced four-endpoint rule. No exposed tongue/feeding apparatus.
- False silhouettes: no long-tail green tree chameleon, horned Jackson's chameleon, sail-like veiled-chameleon head, thorny devil or dinosaur spikes. Preserve modest pointed casque and knob row, not a continuous exaggerated jagged sail.
- Uncertainty: sex not assigned from photographs; each visible structure is checked by connection and shape rather than inferred sex/stage. Natural variation in spots not made into fixed counts.
