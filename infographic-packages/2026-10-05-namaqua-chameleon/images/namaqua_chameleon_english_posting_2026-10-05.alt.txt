A vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel field-notebook poster. A large grey-brown Namaqua chameleon faces left on Namib sand and gravel, with a pointed rear casque, turret eye, spaced back spikes and a short tail. Three numbered illustrated cards explain darker cold and paler hot colours in thermoregulation, life and foraging on arid ground, and a menu that includes insects and sometimes small lizards. The quiet footer gives the IUCN Red List's 2009 global Least Concern assessment.
