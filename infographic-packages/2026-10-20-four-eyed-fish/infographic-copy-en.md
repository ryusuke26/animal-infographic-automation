Title: Four-eyed Fish
Scientific name: *Anableps anableps*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: One eye, two worlds
   Explanation: Each of its two eyes has upper and lower sections for seeing in air and water.
2. Heading: Algae are on the menu
   Explanation: In a north Brazilian study, red algae made up most of the diet.
3. Heading: Born as little fish
   Explanation: Young develop inside the mother and are born alive, rather than laid as eggs.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2020: Least Concern (LC)
