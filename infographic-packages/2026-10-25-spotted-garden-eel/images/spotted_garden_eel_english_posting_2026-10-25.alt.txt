Vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel poster titled Spotted Garden Eel, Heteroconger hassi. A slender pale adult with tiny black spots, larger dark patches and yellow-green eye rings curves above an Indo-Pacific sandy seabed in teal water; its rear body is buried. Three numbered illustrated notes show a qualitative mucus-lined burrow cross-section, a head facing current-borne zooplankton, and an eel retreating tail-first into sand. Footer: IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC).
