Title: Spotted Garden Eel
Scientific name: *Heteroconger hassi*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Mucus reinforces a sandy home
   Explanation: Mucus from the body helps keep the sandy burrow from collapsing.
2. Heading: Face the current for a meal
   Explanation: With its rear body in the sand, it eats passing zooplankton.
3. Heading: Retreat in reverse
   Explanation: When alarmed, it slips tail-first into its burrow and hides in the sand.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2016: Least Concern (LC)
