## Selected identity and naming

- Accepted scientific name: *Beduka amboli* Dubois, Ohler & Pyron, 2021.
- Historical/source-retrieval names: *Xanthophryne tigerina* and the original masculine spelling *Xanthophryne tigerinus*.
- English public name: Amboli Lateritic Toad, used by the current official IUCN assessment and Zoological Survey of India checklist.
- Japanese public display name: アンボリ・ラテライト・ヒキガエル. No established Japanese standard common name was confirmed; this is a transparent rendering of the supported English name. That caveat stays in evidence and source/context material, not on the poster.
- Editorial classification group: Amphibians.
- Exact lineage: Animalia; Chordata; Amphibia; Anura; Bufonidae; *Beduka*.
- Broad native region: Amboli, Maharashtra, northern Western Ghats, India / Asia.
- Habitat: mid-elevation exposed lateritic rocky outcrops and nearby forest, where early monsoon rain fills shallow temporary pools.
- Curiosity hook: a nearly bare red-rock surface becomes a brief nursery and nighttime soundscape after rain.

## Taxonomy and source reconciliation

The current IUCN assessment, ITIS record derived from Amphibian Species of the World, and the Zoological Survey of India's April 2025 checklist all accept *Beduka amboli*. The 2009 description and 2016 reproductive study use *Xanthophryne tigerina* or *X. tigerinus*; they remain biological evidence for the same species, not a second public name. The Indian Amphibians species page still displays the superseded 2011 CR status, so it is used only for local photo/habitat context and not for the public conservation label.
