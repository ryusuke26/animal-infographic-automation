## Story reply

```text
For most of the year, Amboli's laterite is bare rock. Early monsoon rain fills its shallow hollows, and 3 cm males gather to call for only a few nights. Eggs hatch in 3–4 days; tadpoles grow in pools barely deeper than a fingertip.
```
