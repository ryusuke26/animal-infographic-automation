Vertical 2:3 handmade northern Western Ghats laterite field-notebook poster. One small Amboli Lateritic Toad, Beduka amboli, crouches beside a shallow rain-filled hollow in iron-red rock. Its ochre-yellow body has irregular dark-brown to black stripes and mottling. The title and scientific name are integrated at the top. Three unequal numbered illustrated observation cards show a monsoon-filled rock hollow, a complete 3 cm yellow-and-dark toad, and a small male calling at dusk. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2020: Endangered (EN).
