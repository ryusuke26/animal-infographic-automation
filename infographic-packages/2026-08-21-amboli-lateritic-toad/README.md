## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Beduka amboli*; the older combination *Xanthophryne tigerina* is retained only as a taxonomy and source-retrieval synonym.
- English public name: Amboli Lateritic Toad.
- Japanese public display name: アンボリ・ラテライト・ヒキガエル.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Endangered (EN), assessed 16 September 2020.
- Public claims: monsoon-filled laterite rock pools, a small yellow-and-dark body, and short breeding bouts after early monsoon rain.
- Discovery doorway: a dry-looking red-rock plateau briefly fills with shallow pools and a chorus of tiny toads.
