## Compact Source List

- [Mammal Diversity Database: *Chlamyphorus truncatus*](https://www.mammaldiversity.org/taxon/1000409/), American Society of Mammalogists, current database v2.4 released 2026-01-02.
- [Animal Diversity Web: *Chlamyphorus truncatus*](https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Chlamyphorus_truncatus/), Sara Douglas, 2017; accessed through current crawl.
- [IUCN Red List DOI: Superina, M., Abba, A.M. & Roig, V.G. 2014. *Chlamyphorus truncatus*](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2014-1.RLTS.T4704A47439264.en), The IUCN Red List of Threatened Species 2014: e.T4704A47439264.
- [Delsuc et al. 2012, Molecular Phylogenetics and Evolution](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1055790311004799), notes fairy armadillos as elusive, nocturnal, subterranean, and Data Deficient.
- [Basso et al. 2020, Journal of Anatomy / PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7163662/), middle-ear study; summarizes endemic central Argentina range, sandy plains/dunes/scrubland, rare observations, and Data Deficient listing.
- [Torres, Abba & Superina 2015, Mammalian Biology](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1616504715000701), climate/habitat suitability study; supports rarity/patchiness and limited ecological knowledge.
