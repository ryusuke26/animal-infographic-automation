Vertical 2:3 crayon-and-oil-pastel field-notebook poster. In lowland forest on Dinagat Island at dusk, one grey-brown Dinagat Bushy-tailed Cloud Rat, Crateromys australis, climbs a branch slowly with a long, hairy black-and-white tail. The title and scientific name sit above. Three numbered illustrated cards show its first wild record in 37 years, its diagnostic two-coloured tail, and its after-sunset climb. The footer reads IUCN Red List 2024: Endangered (EN).
