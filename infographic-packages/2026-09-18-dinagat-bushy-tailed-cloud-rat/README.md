## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Crateromys australis* Musser, Heaney & Rabor, 1985.
- English public name: Dinagat Bushy-tailed Cloud Rat.
- Japanese public name: ディナガットフサオクモネズミ, from the Mammalogical Society of Japan standard-name list.
- Status: Global Endangered (EN) under IUCN criterion B1ab(iii), assessed 24 June 2024 in record `T5499A260476217` and published in 2024.
- Public claims: the species was photographed in the wild in 2012 after the 1975 holotype; living animals have a grey-brown-reddish body and diagnostic black-white hairy tail; after-sunset observations recorded slow climbing on vines and branches to 5-6 m.
- Discovery doorway: at dusk, a black-and-white tail moved slowly through branches after a 37-year scientific record gap.
