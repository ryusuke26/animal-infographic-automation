## English X Post

A tiny green shape living on algae, often nicknamed a leaf sheep.
Tengu sea slug
Costasiella kuroshimae

This sacoglossan sea slug lives on shallow marine algae. Its leaflike back is made of soft cerata, and it can keep algal chloroplasts for a while after feeding.

IUCN Red List: Not Evaluated (NE; checked 2026)
