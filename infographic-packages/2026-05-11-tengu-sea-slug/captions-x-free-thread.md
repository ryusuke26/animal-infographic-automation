## Japanese 140-character thread draft

1. 藻の上に、葉っぱみたいな小さな体。テングモウミウシ / Costasiella kuroshimae。#ウミウシ

2. 浅い海の藻場で見つかる、嚢舌類の小さなウミウシです。英語では leaf sheep と呼ばれることもあります。

3. 背中の葉っぱのような突起は毛ではなく、藻を食べて得た緑をしばらく保つ暮らしのかたち。

4. IUCN Red List: Not Evaluated (NE; checked 2026)。分類はWoRMS/GBIF/SeaLifeBaseで確認。

## English 140-character thread draft

1. A tiny green shape on algae: the Tengu sea slug, Costasiella kuroshimae. Often nicknamed a leaf sheep.

2. It is a sacoglossan sea slug recorded from shallow marine algae in the Indo-West Pacific, including Japan.

3. The leaflike back is made of cerata. After feeding, it can keep algal chloroplasts for a while.

4. IUCN Red List: Not Evaluated (NE; checked 2026). Taxonomy checked with WoRMS, GBIF, and SeaLifeBase.
