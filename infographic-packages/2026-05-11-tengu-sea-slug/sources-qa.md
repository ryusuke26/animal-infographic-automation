## Source Notes

- WoRMS and GBIF agree on the accepted scientific name *Costasiella kuroshimae* M. Ichikawa, 1993, within Sacoglossa / Costasiellidae.
- SeaLifeBase provides a compact public species profile and displays Not Evaluated status context.
- SeaSlug.World and the Japanese field-guide references it lists support the Japanese common name テングモウミウシ.
- Online "leaf sheep" usage can be broader than one species; image/copy guidance therefore avoids making the nickname the only identity cue.

## Image QA Checklist

- Shows a small green sea slug on algae, not a mammal.
- Includes white/pale face, dark eyes, black-tipped rhinophores, rounded green cerata.
- No fake map, no flags, no unsupported population icon.
- Conservation footer remains quiet.
- Text-safe SVG backups contain the authoritative copy.
