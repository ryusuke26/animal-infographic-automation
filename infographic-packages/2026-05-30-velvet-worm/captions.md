## English X Post

In a damp fallen log, a velvet worm opens a net of slime.
Velvet Worm
Euperipatoides rowelli

This Australian onychophoran lives in moist forest-floor spaces such as decaying logs and leaf litter. It fires sticky slime from paired oral tubes to tangle small invertebrate prey, then walks on many tiny clawed feet through the dark wood gaps.

IUCN Red List checked 2026: Not Evaluated (NE)

#VelvetWorm

ALT: A blue-grey to brown velvet worm moving beside damp leaf litter and a decaying log. The soft elongated body has many short clawed legs, two antennae, and paired slime papillae near the mouth. Poster text notes damp fallen logs, sticky slime threads, tiny clawed feet, and IUCN Not Evaluated.
