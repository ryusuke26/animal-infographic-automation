## Image Identity Guidance

- Show a small, soft-bodied onychophoran, not a caterpillar, millipede, leech, slug, or fantasy worm.
- Include two antennae, small eyes near the antenna bases, paired oral slime papillae/nozzles, and many short stubby legs with a low walking posture.
- Habitat cues should be damp leaf litter, moss, and a decaying log. No fake map, no population icon, no rescue/blame symbolism.
- The generated text may be imperfect; text-safe SVG/PNG backups are the authoritative readable assets.

## QA Notes

- Tone: discovery-first; no rescue, blame, urgency slogan, or action demand.
- Conservation footer: quiet NE wording only; no population number or threat list.
- Japanese common-name caution: "カギムシの一種" is used as a broad Japanese label for the group, not as a confirmed species-specific standardized Japanese common name.
- Text density: poster copy limited to a title, scientific name, three observation notes, and one quiet status note.
