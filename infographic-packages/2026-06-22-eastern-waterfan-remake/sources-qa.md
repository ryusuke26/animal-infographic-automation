## Exact visual identity guidance

Show one large patch of leafy, fan-shaped olive-green to blue-green lichen attached to rounded gray rocks in a shallow, clear, shaded forest stream. Part of the thallus should be visibly below the water surface. One naturally lifted edge may reveal branching fan-like veins on the underside. Keep the organism irregular and lichen-like, with soft lobes rather than a single plant leaf.

Avoid seaweed, kelp, moss cushions, coral branches, mushrooms, flowers, roots, a terrestrial leaf, fake maps, microscopic diagrams, cutaways, duplicated specimens, and lifecycle panels. The poster should not imply the lichen swims or floats freely.
