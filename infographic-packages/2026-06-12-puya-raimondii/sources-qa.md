## Locked Public Claims

2. It forms a giant basal rosette of many stiff, narrow, spiny leaves and can
   raise a very tall central flower spike.

## Visual Identity Guidance

Show one mature flowering *Puya raimondii* on an open, treeless high-Andes rocky
slope. The plant must have a huge ground-level spherical rosette made from many
long, narrow, rigid, grey-green leaves with visible toothed/spiny margins. From
the exact center, show one extremely tall, thick, upright, unbranched,
candle-like flower spike densely covered with many small pale greenish-white
flowers and short lateral flower clusters. The flower spike should be much taller
than the leaf rosette. Include distant ochre mountains, stones, sparse puna grass,
and cool high-altitude sky.

Avoid agave or aloe flowers with candelabra branches, pineapple fruit, cactus
columns, palm trunks, trees, tropical jungle, dense forest, giant single petals,
multiple flower spikes from one rosette, fake maps, people, population counters,
rescue imagery, or unsupported animal interactions.
