# Japanese Image Prompt

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: Japanese educational social media infographic, vertical 4:5 poster
Primary request: Create a gentle infographic about *Puya raimondii* focused on curiosity, natural-history discovery, and education rather than advocacy.

Subject: One mature flowering *Puya raimondii* on an open, treeless, rocky high-Andes slope in Peru or Bolivia. Show a huge ground-level spherical rosette made from many long, narrow, rigid, grey-green leaves with clearly toothed/spiny margins. From the exact center rises one extremely tall, thick, upright, unbranched, candle-like flower spike, much taller than the rosette, densely covered with many small pale greenish-white flowers and short lateral flower clusters. Include stones, sparse golden puna grass, distant ochre mountains, and cool blue high-altitude sky.

Style/medium: childlike crayon and oil-pastel field-notebook poster, warm handmade educational tone, textured paper, imperfect lines with polished adult art direction.

Composition/framing: vertical 4:5 poster; top title and scientific name; center one complete flowering plant with the full rosette and full flower spike visible; three integrated observation labels; bottom quiet status footer; generous whitespace.

Color palette: grey-green leaves, pale greenish-white flowers, ochre rock, dry gold grass, cool blue sky, warm off-white paper.

Text (verbatim):


Constraints: Discovery-first, not moralizing. Accurate legible text. Keep every Japanese character and the scientific name exactly as supplied. One rosette and one central flower spike. No logos, no watermark, no population numbers, no blame.
