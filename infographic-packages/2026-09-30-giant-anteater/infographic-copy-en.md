Title: Giant Anteater
Scientific name: *Myrmecophaga tridactyla*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Walking with curled claws
   Explanation: Its large front claws curl inward as it walks on the outer edges of its forefeet.
2. Heading: A tongue for insects
   Explanation: Toothless, it gathers ants and termites with a long, sticky tongue.
3. Heading: A tail like a cover
   Explanation: While resting, it can drape its long, bushy tail over its body.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2024: Vulnerable (VU)
