Vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel field-notebook poster. One giant anteater walks through South American grassland and open woodland. It has a long narrow snout, a pale-edged black shoulder marking, curled foreclaws and a broad shaggy tail. Three numbered illustrated cards explain its forefoot gait, sticky-tongue feeding and tail used as a cover while resting. The footer gives the IUCN global Vulnerable assessment from 2024.
