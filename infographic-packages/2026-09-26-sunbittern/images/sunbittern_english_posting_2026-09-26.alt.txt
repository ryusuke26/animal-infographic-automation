Vertical 2:3 crayon-and-oil-pastel natural-history poster. A Sunbittern, Eurypyga helias, stands by a shallow stream in a tropical American forest with its wings and tail spread. The brown barred feathers open into chestnut-orange, black and pale eye-like markings. Three numbered illustrated cards show the sudden wing pattern, streamside hunting and a parent’s broken-wing distraction near the nest. The footer reads IUCN Red List 2019: Least Concern (LC).
