## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Eurypyga helias* (Pallas, 1781).
- Public names: Sunbittern / ジャノメドリ.
- Status: IUCN record T22691893A163625651, Global Least Concern (LC), last assessed 4 December 2019; page checked in IUCN Red List version 2026-1.
- Public claims: a startled sunbittern spreads its wings and tail to reveal chestnut and orange patches; it walks and hunts along shallow, wooded streams; either parent can use a broken-wing display to distract a predator from chicks near the nest.
- Discovery doorway: the quiet brown streamside bird can suddenly open a vivid fan of eye-like wing markings.
