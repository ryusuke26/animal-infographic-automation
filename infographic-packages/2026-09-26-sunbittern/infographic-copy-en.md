Title: Sunbittern
Scientific name: *Eurypyga helias*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: A flash of eyespots
   Explanation: When startled, it spreads its wings and tail to show chestnut-orange patches.
2. Heading: A streamside hunter
   Explanation: It walks through shallow water, then strikes quickly at small prey.
3. Heading: A broken-wing decoy
   Explanation: Near a nest, a parent may feign injury to draw a predator from its chicks.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2019: Least Concern (LC)
