Title: Monkey Puzzle
Scientific name: *Araucaria araucana*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: A conifer with triangular leaves
   Explanation: Stiff, pointed leaves overlap to cover each branch.
2. Heading: Different trees, different cones
   Explanation: Male trees usually bear long pollen cones; females bear round seed cones.
3. Heading: A cone comes apart
   Explanation: Ripe female cones break apart to release their seeds.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2011: Endangered (EN)
