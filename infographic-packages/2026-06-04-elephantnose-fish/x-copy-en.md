## Main Post

A small "nose" moves over a dim riverbed.
Elephantnose Fish
*Gnathonemus petersii*

This West/Central African freshwater fish lives close to slow, dark river bottoms with mud and vegetation.
The nose-like part is a lower-jaw chin organ.
It explores the water with weak electricity, a quiet and curious way of living.

IUCN Red List 2019: Least Concern (LC)
