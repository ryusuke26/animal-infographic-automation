## Publication-Safe Copy

エレファントノーズフィッシュ / Elephantnose Fish (*Gnathonemus petersii*) is introduced as a West/Central African freshwater fish that lives close to dark river bottoms. The "nose" is a lower-jaw barbel/chin organ, and the fish uses weak-electric sensing while exploring its surroundings. Conservation footer: IUCN Red List 2019: Least Concern (LC).

## Notes

- The display Japanese name is verified through Wikimedia/Wikidata vernacular-name listings. It is treated here as a public display name, not a formal Japanese standard-name claim.
- Habitat wording is kept conservative because Georgia Aquarium notes that the exact habitat is not fully known.
- The package avoids aquarium-care claims, group-size claims, pet trade framing, and population numbers.
- Image guidance: show a dark slender fish, lower-jaw barbel, mouth above the barbel, small rounded fins, muddy vegetated freshwater bottom, and subtle weak-electric sensing marks. Do not show a mammal-like elephant trunk, upper-nose trunk, catfish whiskers, saltwater reef, fake map, or glass aquarium.
