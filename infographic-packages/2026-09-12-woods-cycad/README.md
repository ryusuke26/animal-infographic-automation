## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Encephalartos woodii* Sander; historical name *Encephalartos altensteinii* var. *bispinosus* J.M.Wood.
- English public name: Wood's Cycad.
- Japanese public name: ウッドオニソテツ, a Japanese horticultural display name recorded by Satake; it is not presented as a current standardized national plant-list name.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Extinct in the Wild (EW), assessed 20 May 2020, record `T41881A243434007`.
- Public claims: all known cultivated plants descend clonally from the single original male; mature males carry upright orange pollen cones; the last wild stems were removed by 1916 and the species now survives in cultivation.
- Discovery doorway: many living trunks, but one male genetic beginning.
