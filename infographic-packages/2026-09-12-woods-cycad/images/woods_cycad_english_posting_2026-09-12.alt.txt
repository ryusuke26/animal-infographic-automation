Vertical 2:3 handmade botanical field-notebook poster. In a historic botanic-garden glasshouse, one male Wood's Cycad, Encephalartos woodii, stands as the hero. Its thick trunk is armored with overlapping old leaf bases. Long glossy dark-green pinnate fronds radiate in an umbrella crown, with narrow leaflets along each rachis. Thick cylindrical orange pollen cones cluster upright at the crown center. The common and scientific names sit at the top. Three numbered illustrated cards show cultivated clones from one male, the orange pollen cones, and a timeline from the species' disappearance from the wild by 1916 to survival in gardens. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2020: Extinct in the Wild (EW).
