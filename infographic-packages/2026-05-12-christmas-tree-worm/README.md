## Rationale

This topic gives the daily series a different lineage and habitat from the most recent packages: a reef-dwelling annelid/polychaete rather than a sea slug, sponge, earthworm, plant, or gecko. The discovery hook is the visible pair of spiral crowns: they look like tiny holiday trees, but they are living feeding and breathing structures of a tube worm hidden inside coral.
