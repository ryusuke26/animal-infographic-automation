## English 140-Character Thread Draft

1. A tiny winter tree opens on coral.  
Christmas tree worm / *Spirobranchus giganteus*

2. The two spiral crowns are gills, not decorations. They help the worm breathe and catch tiny food from the water.

3. Most of the body stays inside a calcareous tube. When startled, it slips back in.  
IUCN: assessment not located (checked 2026)
