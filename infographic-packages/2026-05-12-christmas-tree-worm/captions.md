## English X Post

A tiny winter tree opens on a coral surface.
Christmas tree worm
Spirobranchus giganteus

This reef polychaete keeps most of its body inside a calcareous tube. The two visible spiral crowns are gills that help it breathe and catch tiny food from the water.

When a shadow comes close, it slips inside, a quietly strange life in coral.

IUCN Red List: assessment not located (checked 2026-05-12)
