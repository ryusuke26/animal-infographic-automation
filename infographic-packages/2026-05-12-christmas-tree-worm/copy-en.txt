Christmas tree worm
Spirobranchus giganteus
Polychaete annelid, family Serpulidae

Two spiral crowns open from a small tube in coral.
Feather-like gills gather tiny food from the water and help it breathe.
When a shadow comes close, the worm slips back into its calcareous tube.

IUCN Red List: assessment not located (checked 2026-05-12)

Note:
The public name "Christmas tree worm" is often used broadly for similar reef serpulids. This package anchors identity to *Spirobranchus giganteus* and uses conservative Caribbean/western Atlantic wording.
