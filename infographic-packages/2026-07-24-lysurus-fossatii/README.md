## Rationale

*Lysurus fossatii* is a recently described Argentine fungus with an unusually simple stinkhorn body: a pale short stalk carrying a compact dark-green fertile mass, without the arms or cage found in many lookalikes. Its egg stage, mainly nighttime laboratory emergence, and changing gleba make a curiosity-first story without relying on population numbers or advocacy framing.

Broad region: South America. Lineage: fungus, Basidiomycota, Phallales. Habitat: dry forest and grassland soils in Argentina. Hook: a “stem-only” stinkhorn that rises from a net-seamed yellow egg.
