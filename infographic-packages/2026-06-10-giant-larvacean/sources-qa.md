## Locked Public Claims

1. *Bathochordaeus stygius* is a pelagic tunicate with a transparent, tadpole-like trunk and long tail.
2. It occupies a much larger mucus house whose inner filters concentrate fine suspended particles as the tail pumps water.
3. A used house can sink with trapped organic material, carrying it toward deeper water and the seafloor.

## Visual Identity Guidance

Show one small *Bathochordaeus stygius* suspended in dark blue open-ocean midwater. The animal has a compact translucent oval trunk and one long, broad, ribbon-like tail, giving a delicate tadpole-like outline. It must sit inside a much larger transparent, cloudlike mucus house with paired outer lobes and a visible inner filter structure. Show subtle streams of tiny suspended particles entering the house and concentrating inward. A small lower inset or downward trail may show an abandoned house collapsing and sinking as marine snow.

Avoid a jellyfish bell, octopus arms, fish fins, frog legs, eyes and mouth drawn as a cute face, multiple animals, a hard glass bubble, a submarine, a nest, a plastic bag, fake maps, population counters, or rescue imagery. The house should read as a fragile biological filter, not the animal's body.
