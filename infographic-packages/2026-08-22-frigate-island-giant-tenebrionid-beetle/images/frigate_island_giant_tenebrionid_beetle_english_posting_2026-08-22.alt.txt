Vertical 2:3 handmade Frégate Island coastal-woodland field-notebook poster. It has a small head, broad pronotum, strongly rounded tubercled abdomen, six connected walking legs and two antennae. The title and scientific name are integrated at the top. Three unequal numbered illustrated cards show an adult on a daytime trunk, a magnified tubercled back with a 3 cm cue, and a complete pale larva inside rotten wood.
