## Main Post

A dark stick insect still walks the night rock ledges.
Lord Howe Island Stick Insect
Dryococelus australis

Near Lord Howe Island, this large wingless phasmid feeds at night on Ball's Pyramid and hides in rock crevices by day.
Its quiet life is tied to sparse leaves on a remote volcanic stack.

IUCN Red List 2017: Critically Endangered (CR)
