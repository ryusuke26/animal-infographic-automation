# 140-Character Thread Drafts (English)

1. A dark stick insect still walks night rock ledges: Lord Howe Island Stick Insect, *Dryococelus australis*.

2. It is a large wingless phasmid tied to Ball's Pyramid, a volcanic stack near Lord Howe Island.

3. By night it feeds on leaves; by day it hides in rock crevices. A quiet life on a very narrow ledge.

4. IUCN Red List 2017: Critically Endangered (CR). No population number used here because sources vary by scope and year.

5. Sources: IUCN/Phasmida Species File, DCCEEW, NSW Environment and Heritage, Australian Museum.
