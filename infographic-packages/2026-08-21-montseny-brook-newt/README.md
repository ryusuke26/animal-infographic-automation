## Evidence route

The preserved official IUCN assessment PDF and matching species-page capture directly confirm *Calotriton arnoldi*, assessment identity `T136131A89696462`, DOI `10.2305/IUCN.UK.2022-1.RLTS.T136131A89696462.en`, Global and Europe scope, Critically Endangered under A3ce; E, and the assessment date 20 August 2021. The PDF was published in IUCN Red List version 2022-1, so the public footer correctly uses the 2021 assessment year rather than the 2022 publication year. Amphibian Species of the World supports the accepted species and English common name. Barcelona Zoo and the Montseny LIFE project support the cold oxygen-rich forest-stream habitat, flattened brown body, yellow-gold side markings, granular skin, reduced lungs, and claw-like toe tips used against the current.
