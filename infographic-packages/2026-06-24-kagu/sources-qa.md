## Exact visual identity guidance

Show one living Kagu standing naturally on the damp leaf-litter floor of a dense New Caledonian forest. Use a medium-sized, long-legged ground bird with pale ash-gray plumage, a slim orange-red bill, red-orange legs, and a long narrow crest swept backward from the crown. The body is rail-like but more upright and soft-feathered, with a modest tail and broad rounded folded wings. Small horny nasal covers sit over the nostril area near the bill base, but they should remain subtle rather than enlarged into a false facial structure.

Use a safe natural foraging pose: head angled toward the leaf litter or bill lightly probing among damp leaves. Keep the wings folded so the poster does not require a difficult display pose. Do not show a crane, heron, secretary bird, chicken, rail with a short crest, pigeon, kiwi, or bird in flight. Avoid an oversized fan crest, blue-gray legs, black bill, webbed feet, talons, extra wings or legs, fake maps, population graphics, threat symbols, rescue imagery, logos, and watermarks.
