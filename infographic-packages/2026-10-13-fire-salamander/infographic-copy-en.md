Title: Fire Salamander
Scientific name: *Salamandra salamandra*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Born into water as larvae
   Explanation: Most females give birth to gilled larvae in streams or other waters.
2. Heading: Each yellow pattern is different
   Explanation: The yellow patches on the black body vary in shape and arrangement.
3. Heading: Defence behind the eyes
   Explanation: Raised glands behind the eyes release toxic secretions.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2022: Vulnerable (VU)
