## Source cautions

- The direct IUCN Red List species page was not reliably resolved during this run. IUCN category is therefore cited through Birds of the World's "Data provided by IUCN (2024) Red List" display and secondary species guides that also show Least Concern.
- Public copy avoids exact country-by-country boundaries, threat rankings, and population numbers.
- Kotobank uses older-style Japanese encyclopedia taxonomy labels; package copy avoids relying on Japanese order-level wording where modern classifications may differ.
