Hoatzin
Opisthocomus hoazin

A blue-faced, crested bird of river-edge forests and swamps in the Amazon and Orinoco basins.
It mostly eats leaves and lets microbes ferment the plant material in an enlarged crop.
Chicks can use tiny wing claws to climb through branches around waterside nests.

A slow, leaf-fermenting riverbank life.
IUCN Red List 2024: Least Concern (LC)
