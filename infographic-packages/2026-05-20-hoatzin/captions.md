## English post

A bird that digests leaves by fermentation in river-edge forests.
Hoatzin
Opisthocomus hoazin

The hoatzin lives around swamps and riparian forests in the Amazon and Orinoco basins. It mostly eats leaves, using microbes in an enlarged crop to ferment plant material. Chicks can climb through branches with tiny wing claws.

IUCN Red List 2024: Least Concern (LC)
