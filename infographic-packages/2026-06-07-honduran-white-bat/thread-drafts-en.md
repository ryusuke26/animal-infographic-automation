# 140-Character Thread Drafts (English)

1. Under a green leaf, a white bat rests by day: Honduran White Bat, *Ectophylla alba*.

2. It lives in Caribbean-slope lowland forest from Honduras to western Panama and feeds mainly on fruit.

3. It cuts leaf ribs so a broad leaf folds into an inverted-boat tent, then roosts beneath the living roof.

4. Snow-white fur meets yellow ears and nose. Research identified the carotenoid lutein in its bare skin.

5. IUCN Red List 2015: Near Threatened (NT). Sources: IUCN, ASM MDD, Brooke 1990, Galván et al. 2016.
