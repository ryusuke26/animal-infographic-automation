Vertical 2:3 crayon and oil-pastel field-notebook poster. One dark mottled wild-type axolotl moves among submerged plants in a Mexican freshwater canal. Feathery external gills attach behind its broad head and a finned tail continues from its body. Three numbered illustrated cards explain the wild color contrast, adult gills, and limb regeneration. A quiet footer gives the IUCN global Critically Endangered status assessed in 2019.
