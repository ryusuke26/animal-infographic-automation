Title: Axolotl
Scientific name: *Ambystoma mexicanum*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: Wild axolotls wear mottled brown
   Explanation: Their dark wild colors differ from the pale captive forms many people know.
2. Heading: Adults keep their feathery gills
   Explanation: They retain external gills and stay aquatic after reaching adulthood.
3. Heading: A lost limb can regrow
   Explanation: Given time, an axolotl can rebuild the tissues of a missing limb.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2019: Critically Endangered (CR)
