## Locked scope

*Porpolomopsis calyptriformis* / Pink Waxcap / アケボノタケ. Global VU under A2c+3c+4c, IUCN T125434807A151188119, assessment date 1 November 2018, errata publication 2019. Three discoveries: cap spreading/splitting, pink cap with pale gills/white stem, British low-input old grassland. No population or exclusive worldwide range claims.

## Acceptance caveat

The user-supplied official IUCN PDF and species-page capture directly confirm Global VU, A2c+3c+4c, assessed 1 November 2018 and published 2019. The 2019 erratum corrects the distribution map; it is not a new assessment date. Both evidence copies are SHA-256-identical to the supplied originals. The earlier retrieval caveat is retired. Japanese official records use the same name for アケボノタケ; the artwork specifically follows European Plantlife references and does not claim that Japanese material has been molecularly reconciled with the assessed European concept.
