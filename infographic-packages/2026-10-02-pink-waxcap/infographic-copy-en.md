Title: Pink Waxcap
Scientific name: *Porpolomopsis calyptriformis*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: A cap that opens and splits
   Explanation: The young cone-shaped cap spreads and splits at the edge.
2. Heading: A pink cap on a white stem
   Explanation: Beneath the cap, the gills are pale pink to white.
3. Heading: Grassland with little fertiliser
   Explanation: In Britain, it grows in old grassland with little added fertiliser.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2018: Vulnerable (VU)
