Title: Caterpillar Fungus
Scientific name: *Ophiocordyceps sinensis*

Observation cards:
1. Heading: A stalk from a caterpillar's head
   Explanation: In spring or early summer, a slender fungal stalk grows up through the soil from the head of a ghost moth caterpillar that died underground.
2. Heading: Caterpillar outside, fungus inside
   Explanation: Fungal threads fill the caterpillar's body and turn it into a hard mass, while its outer skin keeps its shape.
3. Heading: Years underground on roots
   Explanation: The host caterpillar lives in alpine meadow soil for three to four years or longer, feeding on plant roots.

Footer/status:
IUCN Red List 2019: Vulnerable (VU)
