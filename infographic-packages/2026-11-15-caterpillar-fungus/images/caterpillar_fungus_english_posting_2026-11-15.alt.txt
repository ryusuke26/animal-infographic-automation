Vertical poster in the style of an old lithographed school wall chart. Title and scientific name sit in a framed panel at the top. The centre is a cutaway of an alpine meadow with snow patches and distant snowy peaks. Underground, a golden ringed caterpillar lies head up; from its head one slender dark-brown stalk rises through the soil and above the grass, ending in a pale tip. Three numbered figures: 1, a round frame at upper right, a close-up of the stalk growing from the caterpillar's head; 2, a tall oval at left, the body cut lengthwise, its golden skin packed with white fungus; 3, lower right, a living white caterpillar chewing roots in a soil tunnel. Each has a heading and explanation. Footer: IUCN Red List 2019: Vulnerable (VU).
