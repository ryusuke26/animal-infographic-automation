## Source Reconciliation

- AMNH ASW lists *Alytes muletensis* and records *Baleaphryne muletensis* and *Baleaphryne talaioticus* as synonym/history items. Public copy uses only the accepted name.
- GBIF Backbone Taxonomy lists *Alytes muletensis* as species, records *Baleaphryne muletensis* and *Baleaphryne talaioticus* under synonyms, and includes マヨルカサンバガエル as a Japanese common name.
- The current IUCN Red List citation record is Martinez-Solano, Schmidt, and IUCN SSC Amphibian Specialist Group 2024, `e.T977A207979274`, with DOI `10.2305/IUCN.UK.2024-2.RLTS.T977A207979274.en`; direct IUCN page rendering failed because the site could not be fetched, so the DOI and citation details were cross-checked through the crawled citation block.
- Older MITECO/Spanish atlas material records the species as Critically Endangered under old global and Spain Red List framing. That is kept as historical context only and is not used as the public footer because the newer IUCN citation is 2024.

## Locked Public Claims

1. A small midwife toad native to Mallorca's Serra de Tramuntana stream ravines.
2. Males carry strings of eggs around the legs before releasing tadpoles into water.
3. Adults use limestone crevices and stones around ravine pools as shelter.

## Visual Identity Guidance

- Show one small tan, brown, or olive-brown midwife toad on pale limestone beside a clear mountain stream or pool.
- Body should be compact but not warty like a generic true toad; head relatively broad, eyes large with vertical pupils, legs fairly long for climbing.
- Include a visible string or cluster of pale eggs wrapped around the hind legs, but do not turn it into jewelry or a nest.
- Habitat cues: limestone ravine wall, stones, shallow pool, Mediterranean mountain stream; no fake map.
- Avoid: generic big bumpy toad, tree frog with toe pads, poison-dart frog colors, tropical rainforest, desert, duplicate frogs, exaggerated tadpole diagram, snake attack scene, rescue slogans, population numbers.
