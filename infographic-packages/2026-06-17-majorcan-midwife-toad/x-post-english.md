A tiny toad first known from bones, then found alive in Mallorca's ravines.
Majorcan Midwife Toad
*Alytes muletensis*

It lives around cool streams and pools in the limestone gorges of the Serra de Tramuntana.
Males carry strings of eggs wrapped around their legs before the tadpoles meet the water.
A small, hidden life between stone cracks and mountain pools.

IUCN Red List 2024: Endangered (EN)
