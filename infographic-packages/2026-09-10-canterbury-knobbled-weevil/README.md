## Locked scope

- Accepted name: *Hadramphus tuberculatus* (Pascoe, 1877). Duplicate screening also covers *Lyperobius tuberculatus* and *Karocolens tuberculatus*.
- English public name: Canterbury Knobbled Weevil.
- Japanese public name: カンタベリー・コブゾウムシ, a transparent rendering rather than a claimed standardized Japanese insect-list name.
- Global status: IUCN Red List Critically Endangered (CR) under B1ab(v)+2ab(v), assessed 19 December 2013, record `T39307A21424332`; the official page is marked `Needs updating`.
- Public claims: not seen after 1922 and rediscovered alive in 2004; adults feed on speargrass leaves and flowers while larvae feed on taproots underground; the large knobbled weevil is flightless.
- Discovery doorway: an insect absent from records for about eighty years was still living among Canterbury speargrass.

## Production status

- Evidence Lock and bilingual cards-v2 Copy Lock are complete.
- Three exact-taxon photographs are retained locally. They control the stout oval body, grey-brown scaling, knobbled wing-case ridges, short down-curved rostrum, elbowed antennae and robust walking legs.
- The 2013 IUCN population number and one-location account are not repeated publicly because the official assessment predates the second population reported by New Zealand's Department of Conservation in December 2024.
- The Japanese first generation and English first-generation companion were accepted without retries. Both keep one dominant adult, exact cards-v2 copy and three unequal numbered illustrated cards.
- Four canonical PNGs are exact 1024 x 1536. Direct-source, X-format, package, pixel-identity, full-size, phone-size, anatomy, false-silhouette, typography, card, composition and whitespace QA pass.
