Vertical 2:3 handmade field-notebook poster. In Canterbury high-country grassland, one stout Canterbury Knobbled Weevil, Hadramphus tuberculatus, walks along a speargrass blade. It has grey-brown mottling, hard knobbled wing cases and a short downward-curving rostrum. The common and scientific names sit at the top. Three numbered illustrated cards show the 1922-to-2004 rediscovery, adult and larval use of speargrass, and its flightless walking body. A quiet footer reads IUCN Red List 2013: Critically Endangered (CR).
